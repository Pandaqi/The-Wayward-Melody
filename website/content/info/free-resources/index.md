{{% section-foldable header="Is free really free?" %}}

Yup. Simply add the resources to your cart, tell us the email address where we should send them, and you're good. 

No delay, no forced newsletter subscription, no need to create an account.

In fact, some resources (under "Direct Links") are just links to raw files, which bypass the entire store.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="Why do you do this?" %}}

This online store was founded by someone who loves learning, but hated every second at school. The entire system of education is either useless when it comes to learning or actively harmful when it comes to health and personal growth. At the same time, as he grew up, he noticed the stark divide between those who could afford proper education and help, and those who could not (for whatever reason).

All his life, he dreamed of providing a _better_ curriculum. One based on games and social activities, one based on what science says works, one that he would have loved to have when he was young. And he wanted to make this curriculum _free_, with no strings attached.

The reality of society requires that the store makes _some_ money. To put food on the table and keep the servers running. That's why it's a _store_ and many resources have a price tag. 

But, whenever possible, we make the oldest / cheapest products completely free. Hopefully, in time, the store will be big enough that we can provide a free curriculum to anyone in the world.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="Is this sustainable?" %}}

There's an unfortunate clash between principles and society. We'll admit that it's hard to stay true to this vision, but definitely not impossible.

Our store does _not_ work through subscriptions, or abusive licensing, or any other shady tactics. (Subscriptions don't really work anyway when you sell so many different products, because everyone likely only needs a small specific subsection.)

You simply pay one price and get the product (and any updates) forever. And, of course, many products are completely _free_.

The only way we can make this work is by making paid products "relatively expensive". By balancing the free with the paid. Our resources sometimes cost twice or thrice what you would pay on other platforms (for something similar). 

At the same time, we often put **a lot more** into a single product. We bundle related work into a single resource per topic. We build our own powerful systems or give you the raw source files to edit. Quality over quantity, content over cover. This is unfortunately not that apparant from the outside, but we always firmly believe our resources are worth the price we ask.
