# The Troupe of Toysmiths

Comm the pear looked everywhere. He was a father fruit looking for a home well and good. He was not having much success, and the letter from his wife gave him even more stress.

_My pear husband, come home! The baby is coming soon, I swear!_

_Also, I am bored to bits, is there something FUN to do out there?_

You must know, dear listener, that Comm did not have much fun growing up. He had no toys, no room to play, he barely had a plate and cup. He thought about his wife back home, in their tiny hut on a boring land. That was no place for a baby, he thought, and so he made a different plan.

He had to find a place with toys. Or a home with a large playground next door. At least, he thought, they should live somewhere where fun things happen more often than before.

And so he ran away again, looking for slides or swings in the wild. He found a teddy bear in the mud, which probably belonged to another child. 

He found a field full of soccer balls, but without a home to stay in. And then he found what he'd been dreaming of: a big playground to play in.

It had a swing, and a seesaw, and a rack for climbing. It even had large shelves with fun books to read, because a narrator had trouble rhyming.

Surely, Comm thought, my daughter would love it here. Let's build a home nearby, and she will never shed a tear! He was satisfied and sure, so he started to write a letter to his wife. Telling her he'd found the place where they'd start their new life.

He tested the slide, of course, and it worked as it should. But when he reached the bottom, he bumped into a troupe.

_Troupe_ is another word, dear listener, for a group of people that move around. It's mostly used for performers, who act, dance, or entertain you with sound.

"Do you live here?" asked Comm. "I'm looking for the place with the best toys."

"You're in luck," they said. "We are the Toysmiths, also called the Playground Boys."

They led him to a different playground, built in a clearing. It was already filled with young apples and peaches, laughing, playing, and cheering.

It was bigger, with more toys, and a sand castle where you could hide.

But then ... they showed another playground with an even longer slide!

More toys is always better, Comm thought. And who wouldn't want a realistic statue of a dragon being fought?

So he kept asking the Toysmiths if they knew a playground with even more toys. And sure, every time, they led him to a place with larger joys.

The day was done, night set in. He was still asking for longer slides and swingier swings, and the Toysmiths said they could always build such things.

He was about to ask them to build the largest playground ever. Then he remembered his wife's message, and thought he wasn't being very clever. Maybe his daughter had already been born! He should have already found a place---and he should be home.

Maybe a few toys, a small playground outside, is... enough. What his daughter really needs is a proper inside place full of soft beds and love ...
