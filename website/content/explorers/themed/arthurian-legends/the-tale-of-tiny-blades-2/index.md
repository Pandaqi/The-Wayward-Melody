_King Uther rules Britain with iron fist, mean and miserable. He has lost his only son---the promised prince---and needs to find him again. Thus, he holds The Prince Trials, and forces all boys of the right age to compete. The Trials are sometimes funny, sometimes interesting, and always hard. But when the king isn't looking ... Arthur secretly grows his band of Knights to stir up trouble._

**Quest for a Prince** is part 2 of the book series **The Tale of Tiny Blades**. It shows Arthur as a young boy, growing into a teenager. He makes new friends and grows his circle of Knights, while trying to stay alive in The Prince Trials.

* It contains **@TODO:XX chapters** of about **1,500 words each**. You can read one chapter a day _to_ children, or they can read this story themselves.
* Each chapter is written as a **self-contained story**. It reminds you of what happened last time. It explains difficult words in a fun way. It shows a new Trial that Arthur has to beat. That said, it is a _book_ (not a story bundle), which means there's an overarching plot and it's best read in order.
* The book is written in our trademark style. **Language is simple and sentences are short.** It contains a lot of whimsy and **humor**. But this simple and playful approach allows it to show core themes of the Arthurian Legends (honor, knights, kingdoms) and be somewhat historically accurate. 

As we always say: _"Kids can handle deeper themes and serious events---they just don't know a lot of words yet."_ 

After reading this book, you might want to continue with part 3 of The Tale of Tiny Blades: [Quest for a Queen](/explorers/arthurian-legends/the-tale-of-tiny-blades-3/).

We are very proud of this set of stories. We hope they bring educational value (in teaching about the historic events that led to the Arthurian Legends) as well as exciting and emotional stories (about what it means to be honorable and a good man/king).
