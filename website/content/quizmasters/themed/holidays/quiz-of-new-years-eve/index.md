This is a **quiz about New Year's Eve!** Ideal for playing with friends or family on the day itself. Or use it in the classroom to teach a bit about the how and why, and how the celebration differs in different countries and cultures.

We've kept this one **shorter than usual** and **laser-focused on New Year's Eve**. Most people prefer doing a general "year recap" pub quiz on New Year's Eve, especially if all members of the group/family are a bit older. That's why we made this quiz simple and short enough to play with young kids too. It's _not_ about any news or pop culture or other references from the past year.

It contains 4 ROUNDS, 40 QUESTIONS (exactly 10 each), namely

* Common Knowledge
* Fireworks Frenzy
* New Year Around The World
* Countdown Questions (a special round where the length of the right answers counts down from 10 to 1!)
