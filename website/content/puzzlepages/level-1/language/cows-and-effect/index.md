This is a bundle of **story puzzles** or pre-readers (or young kids). 

The idea is simple.

* Every puzzle is a **story** with ~5 different illustrations to cut out.
* The story has a clear **"cause and effect"** that puzzlers need to figure out! Something happened, which meant this other thing happened, which caused this other ending to the story. Example: you left ice cream out in the sun, now it melted.
* You've solved the puzzle if you can place the illustrations on the timeline in the **correct order**.

They're a great textless way to get used to stories and have fun thinking about common situations and their consequences. For more practice with stories, and considering consequences of actions, it's of course best to just read as many stories as possible and experience many different things in the real world.
