**Jump on your boat and travel around the ocean to clean it up. Learn about waste, animals, and the climate in a tense Classroom Game about being smart and working together.**

Like all our Classroom Games, it's played over multiple _rounds_ in which everyone _acts at the same time_. Nobody is waiting on their turn. Nobody is forced to sit still. You don't have to play the game all in one go---you can just play one 5-minute round a week, for example, as a Friday afternoon reward for your students.

The game supports 8 boats (at most). Each boat is a group of 4 players (at most), because that's the number of unique _roles_ to play: Captain, Helmsman, Cleaner, Engineer.

Each boat has a **unique set of actions** written on its Boat Card. This means teams really need to work together, because nobody can do everything on their own. It also means they barely have to learn the rules beforehand: it's written on the card anyway.

Furthermore, every role in the team has a clear purpose. Something only _they_ are allowed to do/see/decide. This means **everyone is playing** all the time.

Over the course of 10--15 rounds, players will travel the ocean, clean up waste, shut down polluting factories, and recycle the waste into upgrading their boats. Will your team focus on cleaning? On helping the animals? On upgrading? Are you going to work together with that other team to fix this really problematic zone? Everything is possible! 

Classroom Games were made to _simulate_ a system as best as possible, so players can have fun while actually learning. In this case, it simulates _why_ waste appears, _why_ it's bad for the ocean/animals, but also _why_ it can be hard to clean it up. Everything in the world is a system. The fastest way to learn _deep, practical, useful knowledge_ is to play with the system using an immersive activity like this.

This is one of our simplest Classroom Games. It includes **extra material and simplified rules** for really young players. It's as textless as possible. Even at its most "complicated" ... the entire rulebook is only two pages long.

Use this game for an educational block about waste, nature, climate, or related topics. Use it for an immersive birthday party themed around the ocean or ships.

Have fun!
