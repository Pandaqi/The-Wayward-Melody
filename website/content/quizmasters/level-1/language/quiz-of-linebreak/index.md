This is a **digital quiz** (with 5 short rounds of increasing difficulty), because it needs to play **sound** for every question!

It teaches children the next step in learning language: identifying that sentences are made up of smaller units called "words". Because everything happens through sound, no other skills or explanation are needed yet.

The Teachers resource for this topic practices more or less the same things. In that resource, however, the Guardian and Apprentices have to make the sounds themselves. (It also allows a few more creative activities that simply don't fit in a quiz-like format.)
