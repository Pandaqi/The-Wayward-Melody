This is a quiz themed around **Glee** (the popular TV show). It's a perfect gift for someone who loves the show, perhaps on their birthday. Even though the questions are of easy to moderate difficulty, it's recommended to play with those familiar with the show. (As several rounds are almost impossible for you to play otherwise.)

This quiz is divided into 8 rounds, 73 questions total. Though you can select which ones to play in the settings.

* General Knowledge
* Perfect Performances (about the musical side of the show)
* Through The Seasons (describes an important event; give the season + episode in which it happened)
* High School Drama (about the high school where it takes place and its supporting characters)
* Jukebox Round! (video + audio of performances, give the original title + artist)
* Queer Quotes (a quote round with a _secret theme_! Either fill in the blank or know who said it.)
* Colorful Characters (about the main characters; name, what they do, real actors)
* Guest Stars (about the many guest stars over the years)

**Fun Fact:** I created this quiz as a birthday activity/present for my little sister. Let's just say that, whenever someone so much as mentioned a song or started singing its first line, my sister would always chime in by immediately stating the exact episode in which Glee featured this song. 

Unlike my [Quiz of Sims](/quizmasters/themed/video-games/quiz-of-sims/) that I also made for her, I have actually seen _many_ Glee episodes myself. This is thanks to the time I shared my workspace with my _older_ sister, who watched entire seasons in a week while I was trying to do my homework next to her. And I'm a musician first, so I didn't mind diving into the music and including that for questions.
