# My First Writing


**On Handwriting**: People differentiate Book/Manuscript ("Blokletters") and Cursive style. In practice, however, a _mixed handwriting_ style (using manuscript when you want, cursive when you want) has proven both most efficient and most legible.
=> @SOURCE: Sample fonts made by someone who studied what kids find most legible = http://www.sassoonfont.co.uk/fonts/sample.html
