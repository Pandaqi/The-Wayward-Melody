This is a book of exercises and solo/group activities for the youngest children (Level 1; up to age ~5) about **Halloween**! Explore different challenges and exercises connected to Halloween.

* Over **30 completely different exercises**.
* Colorful and thematic.
* Fun to look at and play around with.
* Keep children **busy and engaged** during the holiday.
* Includes both **pencil on paper** activities, as well as **hands-on activities** (cutting, folding, matching, etcetera)
* **Educational challenges** practicing core skills like pencil control, colors, shapes, basic patterns and more.
* Great for **early finishers** or **high ability students** at school.

A fun and easy way to kill some time during Halloween!

About half of the pages require cutting out some material, and half do not.

Because such young children usually can't count or read yet, none of the exercises require it. It's still recommended, however, to guide your child through the book and help them (explain/cut out/steer in the right direction). It's much harder for a child of that age to 100% figure out what they're supposed to do on their own!
