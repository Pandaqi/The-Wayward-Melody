This is a **kids quiz** about **Halloween**! It contains a wide variety of questions about the holiday, its history, movies and stories about it, and more. It's a perfect activity to play on the day itself, especially with very young kids or in the classroom!

Don't worry, the quiz itself is _not_ scary or gory. It contains many image, audio and video clip questions to keep even the youngest players interested. It also contains a special round with shuffled letters at the end, which can of course be skipped if your players don't know the alphabet yet.

{{% remark %}}
For more challenging and less educational questions, which will also interest older kids and adults, check out the [Quiz of Halloween: Family](/quizmasters/themed/holidays/halloween/quiz-of-halloween-family/) instead!
{{% /remark %}}

It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically. The questions are short, simple, and varied. Almost all of them include an image, audio, or video.

It's divided into 6 rounds, 36 questions total.

* General Knowledge
* Creepy Creatures
* Halloween on the Big Screen
* Spooky Stories
* Witch Answer Is Correct?
* Scary Shufflings (anagrams of Halloween concepts/terms)
