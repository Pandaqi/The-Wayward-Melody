This is an **escape room** about **figuring out where Hannah's house is**! At the title suggests, it mysteriously disappeared, which is never great when a house does that.

The escape room contains a beautiful map and a large stack of "story snippets". It needs _one_ person present (Guardian) who can read these snippets out loud. You need to listen carefully and think logically about cause and effect to pick up the hints inside the stories. If you do, you'll be able to place more and more townspeople on the map, until you figure out the remaining location where Hannah's house must be.

{{% example %}}
A story might talk about someone leaving the building next to the forest. In the next story, that same person is fighting a fire. Then you might deduce that this person is a firefighter and that specific building is the fire station.
{{% /example %}}

Over time, you create a literal timeline (or sequence of events) while training listening and/or reading comprehension. The escape room contains puzzles and themes that should be appropriate for both pre-readers and (early) readers.
