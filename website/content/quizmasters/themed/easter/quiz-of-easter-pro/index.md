This is a (pub) quiz themed around **Easter**, most suitable to **experts** or **hardcore quizzers**! Or those who already played our simpler Easter quizzes the previous years. It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically.

The questions cover the widest possible range of topics and can be very tough to answer! Some questions use other media (such as images and video). Expect the entire quiz experience to take up ~2 hours.

It's divided into 10 rounds of ~10 questions each, though you can select which ones to play in the settings.

* General Knowledge
* Easter Riddles
* True or False
* Bible Brainteasers
* What Does It Mean?
* Film & TV
* Food & Drink
* Around The World
* Happened During Easter
* Easter Eggs! (Variety round)
