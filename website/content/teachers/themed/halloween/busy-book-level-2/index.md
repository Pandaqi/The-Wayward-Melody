This is a book of exercises and solo/group activities for the youngest children (Level 2; up to age ~8) about **Halloween**! 

* Over **30 completely different exercises**.
* Colorful and thematic.
* Fun to look at and play around with.
* Keep children **busy and engaged** during the holiday, be it at home or in class.
* Includes both **pencil on paper** activities, as well as **hands-on activities** (cutting, folding, matching, etcetera)
* **Educational challenges** practicing core skills like counting, alphabet, logical problem solvoing, and more
* Great for **early finishers** ("fast finishers") or **high ability students** at school.

Because this book is in _Level 2(+)_, it assumes children have a very basic understanding of numbers and letters. If their understanding is good enough, they can enjoy and understand this book completely on their own. Otherwise, a bit of help by a Guardian might be necessary. 

For younger children, try the [Level 1 Busy Book](/teachers/themed/halloween/busy-book-level-1/).

For actual _puzzles_ (e.g. sudoku, crosswords), check out the Halloween materials in the Puzzlepages section. For example, [Halloween Puzzle Pack](/puzzlepages/themed/halloween/puzzle-pack/).
