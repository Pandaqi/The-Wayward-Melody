# Alphabet Order

**Description**:
Once you've learned your letters
It's time for something even better
This resource explores the alphabet as a whole
And how letters are ordered to reach any goal

**Extra**:

We recommend exploring this resource _after_ learning the letters. It's perfectly doable to teach someone what letters mean before they have any clue about "alphabet" and overarching concepts. Conversely, it's impossible to teach the order of the entire alphabet (and other properties) if Apprentices haven't even seen all letters yet! ;)

This topic, therefore, also contains some stories (at Explorers) about alphabet in general, and some escape room experiences (at Runaways) that use the entire alphabet for clues and puzzles. The Teaching Resource you're looking at now merely _teaches_ and _tests_ the alphabet and its order.
