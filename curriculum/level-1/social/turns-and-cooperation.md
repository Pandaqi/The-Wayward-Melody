# Turns & Cooperation

**Description:**
A child quickly learns that in this world they're not alone
Other people can help and hold them, but their desires might clash with their own
This resource teaches cooperation, taking turns or sharing
A great first step towards games, projects and a collaborative bearing

**Extra**:
Sharing and cooperation are natural properties of humans, and essential ones too. But it's easy to forget that a concept like "taking turns" isn't necessarily something with which everyone is born. 

In more intuitive settings, kids often don't need this explained. Maybe one or two mini "clashes" with other children are needed, but that's all. For example, if _you_ eat all the food, then _others_ have none of it. If _you_ are using the fancy pen, then _nobody else_ can use it. Hence, taking turns.

But in our society, we quickly move away from those settings, into situations where it's not _necessary_ to take turns or collaborate. Such as board games: many kids struggle to understand why they can't just do their thing _now_. What's stopping them!? Why are we waiting to have fun!?

This resource aims to introduce these concepts and foster the mind-set of social behavior and cooperation.

**Contents**:
* A simple (fairy tale) story about taking turns and cooperating.
* A simple object/drawing that you _take turns_ owning. (Like that teddy bear that goes around from school to school.)
  * And doing so gives you some self-determined special powers or whatever?
* Exercises that you must do together.
  * Either by alternating and passing the paper around.
  * Or because you must _hold_ the paper together, balancing something or whatever.
* A simple "game" to teach taking turns. 
  * Show an image where the two players _don't_ take turns, with sad faces and stuff.
  * Then show an image where they _do_ alternate, with happy faces.
  * The game itself is just roughly drawing stuff into boxes => maybe combined to tell a story or create a map or whatever. Maybe you draw into _one_ cell on your turn, and all cells combined are one drawing?

**Advanced Spells**: Exhausted this resource? Then continue exploring this topic with:
* (Quizmasters) The Quiz of Many => a very straightforward quiz, but the idea is that you do it in (large) _teams_. And maybe that, taking turns, one team doesn't give answers but presents the next questions? Or maybe _grading_?
* (Runaways) The Crackel Collective => about a mysterious bunch of people who collaborated to achieve something huge. (Maybe inspired by simple fairy tale story before it.)
* (Gamiverses) The many Fun Frontier games I envisioned for this.

_The advanced spells in this topic are almost exclusively games and escape rooms, as those are the very definition of taking turns and teamwork._
