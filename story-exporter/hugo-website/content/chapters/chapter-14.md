# The Last Drop (Makes The Cup Run Over)

Astra Buni had been locked in the dungeons for months. King Triangle captured her long ago because she was a bunny, who looked like a bunny, not a Triangle. Her ears were two nice and round shapes. Some called it a Tear shape, some a Droplet. When she was happy, the ears could flop too, and then nobody knew what to call their shape.

Her whiskers were thin Tear shapes, sticking out to the side. Her fluffy little tail was a Tear shape if you looked closer. Her eyes were Tear shapes, and so was her nose that wiggled as she smelled something.

How Astra had escaped the dungeons, you ask? She wasn't sure. 

Strange things had happened since that day she met Tessella and Piqafox. She suddenly saw a color that nobody else could see. She saw shapes that nobody else could see. They had given her a glowing paint brush---its tip, of course, a Tear shape.

She had a desire to paint things with the brush. She kept painting, and painting, and painting. Red squares. Orange circles. Until, suddenly, she stood somewhere else in the castle. Magic! She only wished that she'd grabbed her father and mother, because they were still stuck in the dungeons.

Astra had tried to do the same trick again. An entire hallway in the castle was covered in her paintings. In a way, she was Shapesong's first graffiti artist. But she couldn't make the magic happen again. Maybe she hadn't done anything at all, and some other magician had saved her.

She found an open door. She nudged it further open with her nose, then crept through.

She let out a squeak and came to a sudden stop. The door led nowhere! If she had hopped one more time, she would have fallen from a very high place. She carefully hopped to the side, clinging to a balcony. Careful now, Astra. Don't want to fall. Just get down to the ground. The dungeons were below the ground, right?

When she was on the last balcony, she froze again.

Piqafox appeared below her. She was surrounded by Triangle guards. Her paws were tied up, and she looked defeated.

Oh no! Had they captured her? Had the Rebelshapes lost?

Astra looked closer ... and saw her paws weren't tied up at all. Piqafox smiled a little.

"Good job, guards!" said another Triangle guard. He opened the castle gate and let them through. "Lock that fox up tight!"

And those guards around Piqafox ... they weren't real guards. They were rebels wearing guard uniforms! With King Triangle's logo and everything!

One guard at the back was carrying a large box. To anyone else, it would look like a wooden box. They claimed it held the money that Piqafox had stolen from them.

But ever since Astra received that paint brush, she had another skill. She could look through paint, through illusions and trickery. She saw that it was actually the legendary Toolbox of Tessella. Which meant the one carrying it was probably Tessella in disguise ...

Whatever they were doing, Astra needed to be part of it.

She hopped to the ground to land inside the Toolbox. Tessella let out a soft "oh!" She couldn't react any more, because it would give her away.

Once inside, the rebels immediately let Piqafox go.

"Tessella, follow that map," she said. "Follow the message from Arrow Hood and the Merry Band."

"Prince," she said. Astra was surprised to see Prince Circlesnake throw off a uniform too. When did _he_ become part of this? "Are you sure?"

"I am," said the Prince, already rolling away. "I know where King Triangle stores all his gold. We will steal it all today."

"Lunib, Ancib, all Bearchitects---with me! We're going to look for a Shaeprint. Like the Headmaster said, this castle is built on a hill with lots of Shaeprints. If we find one, we activate it. And hope this has _good magic_ that we want."

It sounded pretty dangerous to Astra. Everyone had only just learned what Shaeprints were, and how to activate them. But nobody could _read_ what they were going to do. Tessella had learned a lot. She had explained some of it to Astra. But still ...

Astra decided to be safe. She stayed inside the Toolbox, going with Tessella. Who mumbled to herself, again and again: "_Square, move left, move left, move right, move up, move up, Cross Triangle Cross, move down, move down, move down, Triangle._"

They had just walked through the only square entrance of the castle. At the first possibility, Tessella turned left. And then left again.

They had arrived in the castle kitchens. As Tessella walked past, she stole some bread, cheese, and chocolate. She dumped it into her Toolbox without thinking, right on Astra's head.

"Hey!"

"Sorry! Forgot you were here! You're Astra, right? We met before?"

"Yes!" Astra was delighted that the great Tessella had remembered her name. She also hoped she didn't want her paint brush back. It was a very nice tool that could do magic. If only Astra understood how it worked ... "So where are we going?"

"_... move right, move up, move up ..._" mumbled Tessella. 

She took the first door on the right, out of the kitchen. A staircase, of course. Tessella sighed and started climbing, still carrying her heavy Toolbox.

At the end of the staircase was another staircase. So they went up again. Just like Arrow Hood's arrow had pointed when they read the message. Let's hope they read it the right way.

Suddenly, Tessella stopped. She pressed herself flat against the wall, shaped like a shivering Square.

Triangle guards ran past. Real ones, not fake ones. They looked panicked and mumbled something about hearing strange noises around the hill. They never noticed Tessella.

"_... Cross Triangle Cross ..._" she mumbled as she walked into a large hallway.

The very first door showed a huge Triangle. It was made of gold and silver, diamonds set into the wood. It had an incredibly large lock, and a sign that said "do not disturb me while sleeping!"

This must be King Triangle's bedroom. It has to be.

Tessella already found a tool to break the lock.

"No!" hissed Astra. "It said _Cross Triangle Cross_. Doesn't that mean ... you should _not_ enter it?"

Tessella paused. "But ... but I thought we were going to capture the king. Where else would the directions point?"

She stepped back, confused. She left the door alone and decided to trust Astra.

"_... move down, move down, move down ..._" 

Ugh. Even more stairs. Tessella shaped herself like a Rectangle. So she could slide down the stairs like a _sleigh_, the Toolbox on her back.

They passed one staircase. After sliding through a hallway, they slid down another. The air was getting cold. The walls were growing gray.

They fell down a final staircase; and Astra realized where they were going.

"The dungeons," she whispered, shivering. 

But she hadn't seen this side of the dungeons before. They were ... they were _much_ larger than she thought. Thousands and thousands of animals were all locked up here. Looking at this, she imagined half of Shapesong being locked in the dungeons!

The map had led them to the dungeons. But not only that, it had led them to the guard entrance. Which meant there was a large box full of _keys_ waiting for them.

Astra and Tessella took in a deep breath.

Then they started grabbing keys and unlocking doors.

Every key only unlocked _one_ door. There were hundreds of doors, so they couldn't guess. They had to _know_. They had to look at the shape of the key, then the shape of the lock, and see if they matched.

Fortunately, if there were two beings in Shapesong who could do this, it would be Tessella and Astra.

They ran around, never stopping. They threw each other keys when they thought the other needed one. They called out the shapes of locks.

"Square-Circle-Triangle End!" Astra would yell.

Two seconds later, Tessella would throw a key her way. And it would have the right shapes to unlock the door.

One by one, cell doors were opened, and entire families of Shape Animals escaped. They thanked the Rebels, even gave them hugs and kisses, then made sure to _get away_.

"We're going to activate a Shaeprint below the castle," said Tessella to everyone. "So don't linger. Get away. Get safe. Go home."

Astra said the same things. But the animals didn't listen to her as much. She was just a really really young bunny, and she was so small that many overlooked her completely. She didn't mind. The King had worked for a _year_ to lock up all these animals---who did nothing wrong, except not looking like a Triangle---and now they were all free again.

The Rebels were winning.

Or were they?

Only a single dungeon cell was left. Astra stood on the other side, pushing her paw through the bars. Her father and mother waited on the other side.

But no matter how long Tessella searched, she couldn't find the right key. "They keyhols is like ... it's a bit of a Circle. But also a bit of a Triangle. It's like ... it's like ..."

Tessella looked down on Astra.

"It's like your ears! A Tear shape!"

Tessella grabbed a hammer. And she _slammed_ a different key to change its shape.

Astra nudged her.

"Erm ... Tessella ..."

Triangle guards ran into the dungeons. From all sides. Every possible escape was removed.

"Use the brush," said Tessella without looking. With some mighty swings, she had "repaired" a key and made it a Tear shape. She pushed it into the door, and freed her parents, as well as a family of Pentagon wolves and Octagon elephants.

"I don't know how! I don't---"

The Triangle guards started their attack. Pointy end forward, charging like a bull. They had completely surrounded them---making a Circle around them.

Astra hopped to her parents. With tears in her eyes, she gave them a big warm hug. She didn't feel ready for this. She had only just started to understand the magic! And there were _so many_ guards. The rebels had done everything right, executed a great plan, and it still wasn't enough!

How could they ever hope to win? For good?

Astra just started crying even more. Large Tear shapes fell from her eyes and onto the dungeon floor.

"Astra! Use---the---paint---brush!" yelled Tessella. 

Astra screamed in fear as one guard came close. She closed her eyes and waved the brush before her like a sword. It connected with a Triangle's face.

It wasn't an actual sword, of course. It wasn't sharp. In fact, if you ever held a paint brush, you know the end is very soft and squishy. So this wasn't going to hurt the Triangle.

But she had painted something on his cheek. Multiple Tear shapes and Triangle shapes, mixed together.

She swapped places with the guard.

The guard was suddenly behind her, and ran into the wall.

She had used magic again---and she didn't know how she did it, again!

The next time a guard came close, she closed her eyes and painted something else. This time, the guard started to float, until his pointy head was stuck in the ceiling.

This continued until no guard dared attack any more. And half of them were either floating, unconscious, or upside-down.

They fled the dungeons. Their work was done: all the cells empty, all animals going back home.

As they left, they noticed Prince Circlesnake rolling away in the other direction. The rebels behind him carried wheelbarrows full of golden coins. Tessella smiled and gave him a thumbs up: he had done his part too.

Freedom was back. The King wasn't rich anymore. Only one very important part remained: the King was still alive and well, sitting on the throne and ordering the guards to attack the rebels.

Astra hopped alongside Tessella as they looked for Piqafox. Where were they? Had they found a Shaeprint to use? What part of the hill?

No matter how hard they looked, they couldn't find them.

Would the Rebels fail anyway?
