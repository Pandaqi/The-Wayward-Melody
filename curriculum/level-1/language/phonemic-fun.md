# Phonemic Fun

**Description**:
Once you've learned what phonemes are
And how they build every word you know
This resource playfully takes you far
By adding, deleting and changing them as you go

**Extra:**
This resource is rather short and doesn't explain any new information about phonemes. It is intended, instead, to playfully test existing skill and stretch it much further than regular old exercises could do.

It's one thing to recognize the different phonemes in a word. It's another to completely _change_ words by purposely adding or deleting sounds. 

This also encourages an intuitive Eureka moment in many children: the fact that different words are usually just one sound away from each other. (Say "delete CH from CHAIR" and they suddenly realize you get a new word "AIR") This is a nice feeling that leads into the idea that all words might be constructed from the same tiny pieces ... called the alphabet.

**Advances:** phantasy-phonics

**Contents**:

* _Add_ something to the start/end. ("What do we get when we add _th_ before _ink_?")
* _Remove/Delete_ something from start/end. ("What do we get when we remove _c_ from _cat_?")
* _Change_ a sound. ("In the word _sing_, change the _ng_ sound to the _t_ sound.")
* _Blend_ it yourself => pick any two phonemes and smash them together.

**@NOTE:** Often, the different parts are represented by _different objects_ first. (Such as actual different tiles in size/color, one for ONSET and one for RIME.) You touch the object representing a "part" of a word and make the sound. Once clear, you push the objects together and say the whole word/blended sound.

**Advanced Spells:**
* (Explorers): 
  * The Tricksy Translator => somebody who "translates" for you, but they merely apply some very simple trick ("replace every T with a B")
* (Quizmasters) 
  * Quiz of Phonemes Advanced
    * You hear a word. Pick the answer that is the same word, but with one thing changed/added/deleted.
    * You hear a word. Now give your own answer (open-ended) that is the same but with one thing changed/added/deleted.
