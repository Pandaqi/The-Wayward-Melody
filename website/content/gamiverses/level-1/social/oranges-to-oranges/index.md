This is a game about **creativity and association**, and one of the rare ones that can be played with pre-reader kids.

It contains two large decks of cards: our _Pre-Reader Prompt Cards_ and _Adjective Cards_. These contain the most common/simple nouns and adjectives, respectively, displayed using clear illustrations.

On your turn, you pick an _Adjective Card_ (e.g. "Strong"). All other players must now play a _Noun Card_ from their hand that they believe fits the adjective best (e.g. "Elephant"). When done, you give points to whoever has the best story/explanation for why _their_ card absolutely fits the best!

It's a simplified version of _Apples to Apples_, but completely playable by pre-readers (because of the images) and with some clever twists and expansions to make it more of a "game" and keep it interesting far longer. It also has variants to (mostly) prevent bias or cheating, such as one where you don't know who played wich noun.
