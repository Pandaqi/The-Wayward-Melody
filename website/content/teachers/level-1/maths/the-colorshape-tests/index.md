This **activity** contains 11 basic colors. Each color page has a jar (or "container") with that unique color, as well as ~10 images of recognizable concepts to cut out.

The activity is simple: place the jars on the table and let the Apprentice sort the correct colors into the correct container! For example: the orange Fox should be placed onto the orange container.

The most common usage is to practice an Apprentice's **colors**. But most of the jars and images employ basic shapes too (not all of them have a clearly defined shape), allowing you to play this sorting activity to practice **shapes** as well. Once an Apprentice can do that, they're ready for the little **board game** attached to the pieces too!
