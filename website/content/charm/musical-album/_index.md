A Musical Album is a **combination of tracks** and other files/work associated with that particular musical project. These were all published under my artist name _Tiamo El Troubadour_.

{{% section-foldable header="How is the product delivered?" %}}

It will be a **ZIP** file. Extract it. It usually contains,

* All the tracks at the highest quality.
* The original cover artwork
* Any demos, notes, discarded recordings, and other work for the album.
* (MAYBE) The _original_ (DAW) files for the whole project. (This is only possible on tiny projects, as these files are HUGE.)
