**Bookshelfy Business** is one of our final board games in this part of the curriculum (that teaches about _grouping_). It has slightly more rules and deeper strategy than the other games, so we recommend trying one of those first if you haven't already.

In this game, players get a personal library with a handful of bookshelves.

* You try to fill it **as much as possible**!
* But the library has strict organization rules: it only wants books **from the same group** on the same shelf!
* In other words, the moment you've placed two books on a shelf, it is now "locked down". You've decided: all other books on that shelf need to be the same group.

How do you get those books? There are random groups of them in the center of the table. On your turn, you pick one group ... and then you get _all of them_. 

In other words, the game asks players to constantly see groups, evaluate groups, and pick the best ones. What group should I make _this_ bookshelf? Should I grab the group with _more_ books that are harder to place, or the smaller group with books that fit better?

The base game is as simple as that. You already know the rules now. Once players are comfortable with that, you can move on to the many upgrades.

* Special actions, special spaces in your library, special everything!
* More competitive play (such as creating groups of books that are the absolute _worst_ for the player next to you ...)
* Finally, a switch from "fill your library the most" to actual counting and numbers with "score the most points"

Because everyone has their own library that others can't touch (_in the base game_), the game is quite friendly and players can just focus on their own skill. The library shows clear icons and spaces for books, making it easy to learn or remember the rules. The upgrades are there to increase the interaction and really put those grouping skills to the test.

As we said, this is probably the "final boss" of our many grouping games ;)

Have fun and keep learning!
