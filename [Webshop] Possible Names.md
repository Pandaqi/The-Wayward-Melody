## 🌟 “A WORLD YOU STEP INTO” NAMES

*(Best if you want the store to feel like a magical destination)*

### **Wonderwhiz**

**Why it works**

* Combines *wonder* (magic, curiosity) with *whiz* (clever, smart).
* Instantly signals “fun learning.”
* Easy to pronounce, spell, and remember.
* Strong brand potential for characters, mascots, and series.

---

### **Playhaven**

**Why it works**

* Suggests a safe, cozy place where play and learning belong together.
* Feels welcoming to parents and magical to kids.
* Very flexible: games, puzzles, books, activities all fit naturally.

---

### **Brightburrow**

**Why it works**

* “Bright” = learning, intelligence.
* “Burrow” = cozy, hidden, discoverable world.
* Feels storybook-like and whimsical without being childish.
* Excellent for visual branding and lore.

---

### **The Curious Lantern**

**Why it works**

* Lanterns symbolize guiding light, discovery, and exploration.
* Feels magical and educational at the same time.
* Slightly mysterious in a good way — invites curiosity.
* Works well for storytelling-based products.

*(Emphasis on games, puzzles, and hands-on fun)*

**Why it works**

### **BrainSprout**
