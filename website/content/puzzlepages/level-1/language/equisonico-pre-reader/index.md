_Equisonico_ is our newest invention for pre-readers. Because the world of puzzles for pre-readers is far too barren, and we want to change that!

This puzzle is based on **homophones**: two different words that _sound the same_ when spoken. It's quite common to give kids a few lessons on them, providing them with a list of common homophones and some fill-in-the-blank exercises. In our view, however, this is certainly not a very fun and engaging way to teach it. It also requires the kids can already read well.

That's why we developed this puzzle. 

Pre-readers might not know how to spell a word, or what the alphabet even is, but that doesn't matter here. All the puzzles are **image-based**. You get 2 images (or more) representing words that _sound alike_. It's your job to figure out those words.

There are **3 "rounds" of increasing difficulty**. This allows the puzzle to start at its absolute simplest, with "perfect homophones", so even the youngest kids don't struggle to understand. But by the end, you're not just guessing words anymore. You get _near homophones_ (that sound very similar, but not identical), and have to come up with your _own_ third homophone that matches the given images!

Of course, it's hard to create such puzzles based on images only and using words that young kids might know. It broke our heart that we had to move our best puzzles to the other version of Equisonico (which uses written hints). Fortunately, enough good ones remained for the pre-reader version.

As a nice bonus, we also added a simple **interactive story** at the end. You can read this story and let kids "fill in the blanks" with homophones. ("Where have you ___?" => Image of BEAN) We felt this was a unique, final test to see if they really understood how _phonemes_ make up words.

_We will release a version of Equisonico with much harder homophones (and using text instead of images) in the near future, once we've started work on Level 2 resources. If we forget to update this page, let us know!_
