This quiz is about **pirates**. What are they? When did they live? Did they really have a patch over their eyes and a parrot on their shoulder? Why were most of them "privateers" first?

It's ideal for a pirate-themed party or classroom. It was designed with young kids in mind; older ages will find a better challenge in [Quiz of Piracy](/quizmasters/themed/pirates/quiz-of-piracy/).

Like all our quizzes, it uses our advanced system that allows playing the quiz however you want: digitally, physically, hybrid, with custom answer sheets, and more.

Not only that, there's an optional **interactive component: a Treasure Hunt!** We've added a large pirate map to hang on the blackboard or place on a table for all to see. A treasure has been buried at some of the many locations. Whenever players get a question right, they get another clue as to where a treasure is. This makes the quiz more than just question-answer, practices logical thinking, and gives a nice reward by the end!

Have fun!
