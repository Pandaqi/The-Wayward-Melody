The fairy tales included in this resource are meant to be read to young children. As expected, they are short (at most ~1,000 words) and mixed with pictures. 

They are also intended to be slightly interactive. After reading a section, there will usually be a picture and the child has the chance to recognize/pick out the element that does or doesn't match. For example, _The Twin Tigers_ is about finding a magical tiger toy that's identical to another (to lift a curse). The pictures show people who try to _trick_ you by showing a toy that _almost_ looks the same ... but something is different.
