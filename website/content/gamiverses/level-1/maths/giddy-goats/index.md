Oh no, all the animals are on the loose and running around! Capture animals within your shape ... but you only score points if you create a group with **matching animals**!

_Giddy Goats_ (or "Groupy Goats") is an educational One Button Game that teaches shapes, comparing, and grouping. It also provides a nice introduction to numbers and counting (especially on higher difficulties). And, of course, it teaches about many different animals and their sound effects ;)

The game can be played **cooperatively** (you work together to beat the game) or **competitively** (you try to reach the highest score).

Gameplay is simple: **press your button to capture all animals within your shape**. Each player has a unique capture shape (circle, square, triangle), which grows and rotates over time, so you have to meticulously check what animal is inside or outside.

Most importantly, you only score points if the group you caught contains **multiple animals of the same type**! Two pigs? Great job. Three pigs? Even better job. One pig and one chick? No points!

A fun, accessible, playful way to become really good at recognition, grouping, and preliminary counting. Score feedback is given in textless ways---such as your goat getting bigger---so that even pre-counters can understand who's winning.

In the settings you can enable extra rules for higher difficulties. For example, you _lose_ points for every lonely animal in your group, or your goat _moves around_ too. These changes help keep pace with your players as their skill grows and keeps the game fun for a much longer time.

Have fun and keep learning!
