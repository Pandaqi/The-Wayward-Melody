Welcome!

Welcome to The Wayward Melody. Take a seat, one that doesn’t growl at you. Browse the magical library with walking books. Do a magical quiz, solve a spellbound exercise, or learn about new topics. Or … realize the doors are locked and you must now escape by solving puzzles.

We sell unique experiences that combine games and learning. Some products are definitely more on the educational side, and some are definitely more on the game side. Take a look and see if something magical catches your eye.

The Magical Way

This webshop has a unique philosophy when it comes to education, entertainment and life in general. You can read the details here, but the summary is quite simple.

Every main category in this webshop is part of a path towards fun and learning. Start with materials in the first "Factions" (or "Categories"), and once satisfied move to later Factions, until you end up at the place where learning actually happens: games and play.

Because fun comes first, though, we sell products for all ages and situations, not just school or education. Have a look around!
