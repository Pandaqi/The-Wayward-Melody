This is one of the "advanced" Easter logic puzzles. It is more traditional, in the sense that you have to put pen to paper and fill in all the empty squares in the right way. (Like a Sudoku or Crosswords.) At the same time, it has very thematic rules and only requires basic knowledge of low numbers.

Over time, more and more rules are added. But the simple core rules remain the same:

* You must draw eggs. Every egg has exactly _two_ patterns (e.g. dots, lines, ...)
* Numbers around the puzzle indicate how often a certain pattern appears in a row or column. ("This row has 2 dotted eggs.")
* Every egg is unique: the same combination only appears once on the entire board.
* Fill all empty squares correctly!

We must admit that it was quite a challenge to get this puzzle as simple yet enjoyable as it is. Our original idea was fine, but all "expansions" we tried at first didn't work, and it was far too easy. Only once we realized we could have each egg contain _two_ patterns (instead of one), and shifted the target audience to include (younger) children, did the puzzles come together into a nice final product. 

We hope you enjoy these too!
