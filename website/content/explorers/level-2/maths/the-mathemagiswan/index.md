This is a **bundle of short stories** about an Apprentice's first numbers. Every specific number (_from 1 up to and including 10_) has its own story devoted to it. More than likely, the Apprentices at this age can't read yet, so the stories are made to be read out loud _to_ them. It also includes Bingo cards (like most of our stories) to test story comprehension and keep them engaged with interactivity, if so desired.

**The Mathemagiswan** is, as expected, a magician. And a swan. But they have a very special kind of magic. Every number has a special power to them! If they manage to collect three bunnies in a hat, a specific spell fires. If they manage to hold five sheep at once, a different specific spell fires.

Follow the Mathemagiswan as they fight the treacherous _Numburglars_ in ten magical adventures.

These stories are a great way to teach and practice an Apprentice's first numbers (1--10). By adding some fun magic, action, emotions, and meaning to each number, they are sure to be remembered more easily.
