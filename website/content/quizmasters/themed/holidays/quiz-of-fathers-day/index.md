This is a family quiz themed around **Father's Day**. A perfect activity to play on the day itself! The questions are family-friendly and of medium difficulty.

It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically. The questions are short and simple. Almost all of them include one or more images, and some have audio/video.

It's divided into 7 rounds, 73 questions total, though you can select which ones to play in the settings.

* General Knowledge
* Dad Jokes!
* History of Hommes
* Fatherhood & Secrets (a longer round with a secret theme that helps if you figure it out)
* Famous Fathers
* Around the World
* Fathers in Film

{{% remark %}}
When testing this quiz it turned out slightly more difficult than our usual family quizzes, mostly because of that "secret theme" round. If you figure it out, it's easy points. If you don't, you don't. Something to keep in mind!
