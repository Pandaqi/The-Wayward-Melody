This is a family quiz about **Halloween**! It contains a wide variety of questions about the holiday, its history, movies and stories about it, and more. It's a perfect activity to play on the day itself, especially with kids or in the classroom!

Don't worry, the quiz itself is _not_ scary or gory. It's aimed at casual families and kids, with a mix of easy and challenging questions.

{{% /remark %}}
