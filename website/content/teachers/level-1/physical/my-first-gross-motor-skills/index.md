The "physical" category of this online store is understandably scarce. The "exercise" here is to ... go outside and exercise! The best thing for any body, but especially a young body, is to move a lot and challenge their muscles in many different ways. Sitting around a table, or studying some sheet on a table, is the antithesis of that. 

For every hour using a resource like this, we'd recommend letting your kid run around and climb walls for at least two hours. Such large movements train so-called **gross motor skills**. This resource mostly gives ideas for different exercises to do, and simple things you can teach children about doing so in a healthy way. (Good posture, _why_ exercise is so important, a schedule of varied movements, etcetera.)

Once you're sure someone is exercising their body enough, you can start looking at our other resources that train _smaller_ movements, like cutting and folding. Those are called [Fine Motor Skills](/teachers/level-1/physical/my-first-fine-motor-skills/).

The crucial activity of [Pencil Control](/teachers/level-1/physical/pencil-control/) proved so interesting and multi-faceted, that it received its own teaching resource too!
