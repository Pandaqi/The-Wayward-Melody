This quiz tests your skill in **grouping**. It starts with the absolute simplest version, such as being able to tell that one thing is bigger or smaller than another. With each round, however, the difficulty is increased and the questions become more creative and challenging.

As always, you can play this quiz digitally, physically, or something in-between. It's easy to customize. For example, only play the rounds you want to focus on today, and leave the rest for another time.

The rounds contained within are,

* How Are They The Same? (show two things, ask for properties they have in common---establishes the basic idea of grouping)
* Which One Fits? (show a group of things, find the multiple choice answer that would fit that group---this is by far the largest round)
* Which One Fits Both? (show two things, find the multiple choice answer that could be a group with both things)
* Odd One Out (which of these answers does NOT belong to the same group as the others?)
* Name This Group (a creative round about finding a good name/common property for a group of things)

{{% remark %}}
This quiz focuses on the core skill of knowing what a group _is_ and what it means to put things into groups at all. Questions are simpler and more objective.
