Sam, who loves summer more than candy, gets in trouble on holiday and is saved by a stranger. In his shock he forgets to ask her name. When he gets home, and the seasons change again, he realizes just how much she helped him.

And that she is likely in terrible danger.

Sam embarks on a journey to figure out who she is. Where she lives. Has anyone seen her? Does anyone have a clue? He spends the next summer traveling the world, searching in the strangest and most unknown places, trying to find someone about whom he knows _nothing_. Except that he must find her or he'll forever feel guilty.

Will he meet her again? And do so in time?

This story is ideal to read during the summer or take with you on holiday. It's packed with action, mystery and whimsical locations based on real-world tourist attractions. We tried to give it the perfect length: not so short that you're done before even halfway your trip, not so long that it feels overwhelming for kids to read.
