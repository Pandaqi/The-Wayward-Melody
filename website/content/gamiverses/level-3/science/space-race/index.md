**Split your students into different "nations" that each try to be the first to land on the moon! Learn about history and space travel by simulating it in this Classroom Game.**

Like all our Classroom Games, it's played over multiple _rounds_ in which everyone _acts at the same time_. Nobody is waiting on their turn. Nobody is forced to sit still. You don't have to play the game all in one go---you can just play one round a week, for example, as a Friday afternoon reward for your students.

The game supports 6 nations (at most). Each nation is a group of at most 5 players, because there are 5 unique _roles_ to play: Investor, Scientist, Engineer, Spy, and Astronaut.

Each role has one or two simple actions they can take. This has two advantages:

* You barely need to teach rules before starting. The rules a player needs to remember are on their Role Card!
* Everyone is actually playing all the time. For example, only the "Astronaut" is allowed to decide where their team's spaceships move, not anyone else from the team!

Over the course of 10--20 rounds, players will research technology, build spaceships, and navigate the perilous path towards the moon in secret. Other teams will Spy on them, finding out how far along their spaceship is or stealing some technology they need. Or maybe your players are more cooperative, trading technology, becoming allies, lending money. Or maybe you quickly launch a cheap Satellite to try and sabotage another player before they get too far ...

Everything is possible! Classroom Games were made to _simulate_ a system as best as possible, so players can have fun while actually learning all the rich details and possible strategies. The entire rulebook is only a few pages long. Within those rules, players can try to win the Space Race any way they see fit. All in on technology? All in on diplomacy? All in on economy? Their choice. The good or bad consequences of their actions become clear the next round.

The expansions---also included for free---include elements that teach the specific history of the Space Race (between USA and SU) and a more educational space map (with black holes, asteroids, and more).

Use this game for an educational block about space or the space race. Use it for an immersive birthday party themed around space.

Have fun!
