This is a **digital quiz** (with 8 rounds of increasing difficulty) because it needs to play **sound** for almost every question! It does provide a physical version of the quiz to print out, but know that only a small minority of the questions can be done without sound.

This quiz is the "evolution" of the basic [Quiz of Phonemes](/quizmasters/level-1/language/quiz-of-phonemes/). We recommend playing that quiz first, as it provides a gentle introduction and testing of the simplest and most common phonemes. This quiz introduces more uncommon phonemes, and asks questions that are much harder and more creative. Most of all, players will be practicing their skills _adding, deleting and changing phonemes_ in existing words. (To get new existing words, of course!)

The Teachers resource for this topic practices more or less the same things. In that resource, however, the Guardian and Apprentices have to make the sounds themselves of course, and it includes some extra activities/little games that a quiz can't do.
