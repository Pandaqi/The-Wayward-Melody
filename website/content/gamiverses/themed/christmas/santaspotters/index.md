**Santaspotters** is a tense, social, funny, Christmas party game about hidden cards and secret identities. All the players will pretend to be innocent children who sometimes wake up and try to spot Santa in their room. 

In reality, everyone has a secret role that makes them either _Team Nice_ or _Team Naughty_.

* Team Naughty tries to figure out who _Santa_ is. They win if guessed correctly.
* Team Nice tries to _help_ Santa. They win if Santa managed to get all the right (gift) cards in their hand.

The other roles have fun abilities and restrictions to either help or hinder. The actual game is played through special cards that can have fun actions to help your investigation---or might actually be the thing that gives you away to Krampus. Because every role gets a "personal deck"---exact cards that only _you_ have and start with---you need to be careful about who you trust to see those.

This Christmas-themed game is great for **large groups**! It requires little material per player, it's about figuring out hidden roles (one team against another), and it easily allows taking a break for a bit if people want to stretch their legs (or worry about their food burning in the kitchen).

The base game only has a few roles and cards, so you can quickly explain and get started. You can even play with young kids / non-readers. Once familiar with that, you can add the expansions (including the chaotic _Neutral Roles_), though those add cards with text that players regularly need to read.
