**Santaspotters** is a tense, social, funny, Christmas party game about hidden cards and secret identities. 

All the players will pretend to be innocent children who sometimes wake up and try to spot Santa in their room. 

In reality, everyone has a secret role that makes them either _Team Nice_ or _Team Naughty_.

* Team Naughty tries to figure out who _Santa_ is. They win if guessed correctly.
* Team Nice tries to _help_ Santa. They win if Santa managed to get all the right "Santa cards" in their hand.

The tricky balancing act here, of course, is that you want to help Santa while keeping up the pretense that you might _be_ him. The majority of players is always Nice ... but they don't know who to trust or where to send their gifts! Spot Santa too well and Krampus will spot him too, snatching victory.

This Christmas-themed game is great for **large groups**. Each added player only requires 1 more Role Card and 5 more Play Cards. It's about figuring out hidden roles in teams, so players can lean on others if they missed rules or don't understand something. In fact, honest forgetfulness is the greatest pokerface and misdirection in the world ;) Because _you_ vote whether the game ends, play time is controlled and it allows taking breaks. If, say, people want to stretch their legs ... or worry about their food burning in the kitchen.

The **base game** only has a few simple roles and two main rules ("gift a secret card on your turn" + "vote if you want the game to end"). You can explain this in 5 minutes and get started. You can even play with young kids / pre-readers. Once familiar with that, you can add the **expansions** (including the chaotic _Neutral Roles_), though some add cards with text that players regularly need to read.

**More advanced roles**, should you choose to include them, have abilities and restrictions that shake things up. The actual game is played through special cards with actions to help your investigation---or give you away to Krampus. Because every role gets a "personal deck"---exact cards that only _you_ start with---you can't get (un)lucky and ruin the game before it starts. But you need to be careful about who you trust to _see_ those specific cards ...

Have fun!

{{% remark %}}
This is a social game. To get the most out of it, players will need to talk, ask questions, try to decipher if someone is lying, and so forth. Especially the base game is so simple that it relies a lot on this aspect; the expansions add a lot more strategy and actions to get information in other ways. If you think your playing group won't match well with such a game, we recommend checking out our other (Christmas) games!
