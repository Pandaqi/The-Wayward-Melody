# The Gospyll According To Saulo

Reliable sources told me what happened to my ancestors, even though it was very hard to hear.

During the First Conflict, the Amor Empire expanded and conquered these provinces. They not only captured my grandparents, they _enslaved_ them. They had become property, not living beings, to be used as they wished! Awful, awful, awful.

But they forgot the resilience and kindness of the Jurad people. Upon hearing of this, all Jurads living in Amor paid large sums of money to free these slaves. They gained Amor citizenship---a rarity not often seen afterwards.

In memory of these events, I founded a new temple: the _Synagogue of Freedmen_. With it came considerable power, as I learned how to read and write and became an expert on Momo's Law. I would never forget what Juradism did for me, and I would protect the religion at all costs.

Which is why I would not let another fake messiah be a threat to Juradism. Never, never, never!

Trustworthy sources told me where I could find governor Pillar that morning. He agreed to an audience because of my considerable wealth and status.

The wolf looked tired. Protests about killing the messiah were still going on outside his home. He still washed all his paws every morning, washed them until they were cleaner than cleanest.

"I must admit my confusion," I said. "Am I not speaking to Pillar? The one who sentenced Krystina to death by crucifixion?"

"Yes?"

"Then why are you not agreeing with me? This new belief, this idea that Krystina was anything but a charlatan, is _dangerous_."

"They call it _The Way_," said Pillar, sipping his wine. "And it has been far more peaceful than you Jurads ever were."

"_Excuse me?_ Oh that is rich," I said, pushing away the wine offered. "Coming from those who enslaved my ancestors."

"I had no part in that," said Pillar. "Thus, unless there is anything else you want, Saulo, I suggest you go back to where you came from. Enjoy your Synagogue of Freedmen, your beating of Krystians without reason ... while you still can."

"Unbelievable, unbelievable, unbelievable," said I. It is important to note, at this point, that I am a panda. Though we may look fluffy and harmless, we can squish anyone who is foolish enough to accidentally walk underneath us when we fall from a tree. 

And so I made a final attempt to persuade Pillar, making myself as large as possible. "I'm asking one final time. Do your _duty_, Pillar! Uphold the law. If you called Krystina guilty, then you must call all her new followers guilty too!"

"Of what crime?" asked Pillar, who now started consuming the grapes. Everyone knows Amor loved their grapes, despite them being slightly poisonous to wolves, and very poisonous to dogs. Common side effects were headaches, panting, and the sudden desire to violently conquer the entire world.

"Well ... well ... showing people the _wrong_ Way!"

"That's not a very strong argument, Saulo. Their new Way has been very good to us. Far fewer revolts and fights between Amor and the Jurads. We have not actually improved their living conditions, but the poor animals seem happier and have faith that things will turn out for the best. If you ask me, only one animal needs arresting, and it is _you_."

Saulo rolled backwards to get to the door, as Pillar's armored guards stirred.

"You are mad, mad, mad! I have it on the highest authority that Krystina did not come back from the dead! It's all a fake! And _The Way_ will ruin all that was good and faithful!"

"Please leave my home peacefully," said Pillar calmly. "You know the way."

I don't have a clear memory of the following days. Some sources claim that I beat up random Krystians I saw on the street---though they didn't call themselves such names yet. Very _untrustworthy_ sources, I would say. Other sources claim I went into the synagogue, prayed for three days straight, and returned with newfound resolve.

If the Amor Empire wasn't going to do it, then I had to do it myself. 

I started traveling from town to town, arresting, poisoning, or publicly shaming anyone who believed in Krystina. From time to time, I'd run into one of the remaining apostles and try to have my way with them. More and more, however, it seemed Amor actively wanted me _not_ to touch any of them. Soon I heard the frightening news that Pillar had converted to _The Way_ himself.

Fearing for my life, and my freedom, I sought support. And of course, I found it with my Jurad friends. We Jurads are the strongest faith in the world, and we look out for each other. And I will never, never, never let Krystina's faith tear us down!

The High Priest didn't mind writing "friendly letters" or even "contracts" commanding me to arrest Krystina's followers. If anyone wanted to stop me, I could show them those papers. Arrest _me_? Why, no, you must take it up with the High Priest!

And so it was that I traveled to Dumbask to rain terror on any newly hatched Krystians.

___

I have it on good authority that my infamous reputation preceded me. In other words: Dumbask knew I was coming. And they were terrified. 

I was only halfway on the journey when messengers came from the other direction, pleading I stay way. Pleading to leave them alone. They had heard of my arrests, beatings and general nasty business in other cities. They claimed there were _zero_ Krystians in Dumbask and that I needn't come. Ha! Ha! Ha! Now _that_ is an unreliable source.

Their pleading only made me travel faster. I'll admit that, being a panda, this is not an impressive pace. But within days I could already see the towers and ancient trees of Dumbask in the distance.

Until I saw none of that any longer.

I bumped into someone I completely overlooked. I bounced one way, she bounced the other way, and I rolled down a long hill---just my luck---before coming to a standstill.

I rose my fist and shook it in anger. 

"I had the right of way!" I yelled as I started my uphill climb. I prepared this whole speech that I would give to this nasty animal who dared touch me. "You should have waited until the red Traffic Tree turned green!"

The sorry state of traffic signs in ancient Amor is not that relevant to this Gospyll. But I still want to clarify that _she_ broke traffic rules, not me. The wooden sign next to the dirt road _clearly_ said "Animals without a beak have right of way over those with a beak"! And the magical trees---which we planted at all intersections now---were _red_ on her side, indicating she should wait.

When I reached the top of the hill, however, I fell silent instead. My mouth hung wide open, my fist disappeared.

There she was. The spitting image of Krystina. The wounds from the crucifixion clearly visible and still somewhat fresh.

"Oh I am sorry," she said. "Did not see you there. Forget you saw me."

"I ... you ... you are Krystina. How did you do it? How did you trick them into thinking ..."

Krystina put on a slightly lower, booming voice. "I didn't. I truly rose from the death. Is my appearance not evidence enough? Do you not feel honored that I came down from the heavens just for you?"

There you have it. The highest possible authority proves that Krystina _only_ appeared to me. Me, me, me. I was special, chosen by God, because they saw how innteligunt I was and how well I could write and spell words.

"Then ... then what did you see, those days that you were dead? What's life after life like?"

"Oh it is wonderful. But, erm, not _so_ wonderful that you should try to get killed!" Krystina looked around, possibly planning her escape. "They have infinite bamboo for pandas to use. And a special panda, erm, playground? Yes, all your wishes come true, if you only have faith in me and God."

"Are there wars? Oppression? Amor soldiers?"

"No. There should not even be a place for that in the real world."

"Are the people one?"

"Everyone who shares faith in me will share the bond of life."

"Does slavery exist there?"

Krystina looked appalled. "Of course not! Who do you think I am?"

"Increasingly ..." said Saulo, reaching out his arm and almost touching Krystina. "I think you might actually be the messiah. And I am Saulo, honored to stand before you."

At the mention of my name, Krystina stiffened.

I have it on great authority---namely, myself---that when I touched Krystina, I felt nothing. Not in a figurative sense, no, I felt a whole range of emotions, faith, and disbelief. But in a literal sense: my paw passed through her as if she were a ghost. A spirit. She had died, come back to life, and had reached the heavens. 

And now she had come down just for _me_, to do ... what? Convince me to---

"Why do you hate me so, Saulo?" asked Krystina simply. 

I must have stood there for minutes, maybe hours, unable to find an answer that sounded right. I hated that my ancestors were beat down by Amor---so why did I start beating others down myself? I called Krystina dangerous because she went against Momo's Laws---but the Jurad story clearly shows that the Laws have been changed and adapted many times before by messiahs. I didn't believe someone could come back to life or live without a body---but here she was, proving me wrong.

"You know how this ends. Alone. More and more are dropping Momo's Law, while they believe in my resurrection. Surely, Saulo, you don't want to end up solo?" Krystina approached me then. And though I could not touch her, she could surely touch me. "I ask again: why do you attack my peaceful followers?"

"Because I was misguided and lacked faith," I said humbly. I closed my eyes and bowed before her. "Oh I have been so wrong, wrong, wrong. Please, I want to change. What should I call you?"

At that moment I heard shuffling and scraping. Paw steps, running maybe, someone else approaching. 

Something hit my head.

And my vision went black. I was blinded as punishment for my lack of faith! Blinded, blinded, blinded.

"Oh lord! Sorry!" yelled Krystina from quite a distance, and then from nearby as she touched my eyes in an attempt to heal them.

"Then I shall call you Lord," I whispered.

"But I am a woman?"

"Lordess?"

"That will have to do."

Fumbling around in my pockets, I took out the contract with the High Priest. I showed it to Krystina---or at least, I _think_ I did---then ripped it apart---or at least, I _hope_ I did, and it wasn't some other important document.

"Now go forth, Saulo, whom will henceforth be known as _Paulo_."

"Why ... why are we changing my name?"

"Because that is what messiahs do. And panda names should simply start with a _P_, it's what God would have wanted."

"I will be Paulo then. I shall go go forth, but to where?"

"To Dumbask. To explain you have converted to The Way, and to apologize for any wrongdoings against my followers."

"They will not believe me. They'll think it's a trick, or a trap. They know me only as a pure Jurad with a pure hatred for you."

"No. That just makes it worth _more_!" Krystina sounded further and further away, and as if she were smiling. I couldn't _see_ that to confirm, of course. "If they see that even you will eventually accept me ... then I believe we'll have a new global religion before the century has passed."

I tried to get back on my feet, but it was no use. I had to stumble and crawl the rest of the way.

"But ... how will I reach Dumbask without sight?"

"I will guide you along The Way," said Krystina. "Also, it's just straight ahead for a few more miles. Can't go wrong, really."

She was wrong, wrong, wrong about that. Many things can go wrong when you're blind. I spent two days in a completely different town altogether before I figured it out. Then I asked someone to be my guide, but they just stole my money and left me stranded back on the same intersection.

Krystina may be the messiah, I believe that now. She still has a lot to learn about traveling and traffic rules.

Eventually, though, I arrived in Dumbask. I visited reliable sources to confirm it!

And the moment I had declared my change of mind, and made my public apology, my vision returned. 

Krystina had opened the eyes of the spiritually blind. Now I had to continue that mission in her name.

___

First of all, I decided that _The Way_ was a terrible name. So generic, so vague. It sounded more like a bad traveling novel, or some obscure festival where the newest horse-drawn carriages were sold. 

No, no, the right name seemed obvious to me. Juradism was named after the member of the tribe who inspired the religion, who was in turn named after the region where they lived: Jurea. Buhadism was named after an animal called Buha who inspired it, who in turn took their name from their inspiration called Prebuha.

And so, in my first letter I sent out to all believers, I suggested we name ourselves after _Krystina_. We should call ourselves _Krystians_.

Secondly, I decided to take control of the Gospylls. Many of them are reliable sources, I believe, and we should include them in a holy text. A history of Krystina's life, her miracle, and the faith that should come from it. After workshopping a few names for this holy scripture, I decided on _Le Bjib_.

The Gospyll of Jamus, though, is _not_ a reliable source. It added a lot of mistakes to this story. The most egregious of them? Krystina had no family! Her first followers, who were not good enough to be disciples, simply called themselves "Brothers of Krystina". The same way a band of soldiers may be called "Brothers in Arms", even though they are completely unrelated. Or how a group of nuns may be called "Sisters", even though they are not family. I believe Jamus just wanted to make himself look special and purposely mistranslated the word. Either that, or he was as bad at language as his supposed sister was at mathematics.

I repeat: my reliable sources find no evidence of Krystina having a large family. Of course such a holy, magical being has no mortal, normal siblings. It would be blasphemy to suggest otherwise! If you ask me, Jamus' account of the events should be thrown out and replaced by mine. I am absolutely certain Le Bjib would become a best-seller of the ancient world, selling at least fifty copies. But not too many, please, because copying these texts by paw is really exhausting work.

Some were still suspicious of me. They still found it unbelievable that you could go from arresting Krystians to being one of their leaders---all because of one holy encounter on the path to Dumbask! But it must be noted that I was one of the few _literate_ animals in these days. If they wanted this history recorded, and they wanted to spread the word, they needed someone who could actually _write_ words.

They needed me. Me, me, me.

I teamed up with a Bearchitect to travel around and erect Krystian churches. In most towns, the number of believers could be counted on one hand. For most animals, lacking fingers, that means that number was simply _one_. Usually, some traveler somewhere had bumped into one of the disciples spreading the message. Or they picked up the famous resurrection story, but thought no more of it.

As this number of Krystians grew, however, they needed more organization. They needed guidance. And I provided.

Letters would arrive every day. They talked about problems they faced and how to respond in the right, faithful way.

One church mentioned a very young kid who asked if they could "get a day off from school" every year on Good Friday. I told them school was important, but faith even more so. As long as Timothy prayed for good grades, I said, we should indeed establish some holidays. The next year, they sent a letter that Timothy's grades had plummeted, but they were going to keep the days off from work and school.

Another church mentioned running out of toilet paper. Should this not be taken as a sign of God to stop using toilet paper, or stop going to the toilet, or possibly both? I responded they should have faith that toilet paper would return soon. 

That same church later ran out of doors too, which led to a handful of believers who believed doors were the work of the devil. I told them that if God closed a door, he would open a window. This merely made them remove all the windows too.

Several churches had questions about how to reconcile the differences between Momo's Law and what Krystina said. "Reconcile", dear reader, is a word which here means "find the best excuses to keep believing two opposite things". After all, both Momo and Krystina were messengers _of the same God_. Saulo---or Paulo---would reply that it was more important to _have faith_ than to follow Momo's Law strictly.

It was this lesson, I believe, that eventually turned Krystianity into its own religion. It was the final straw that broke the camel's back. The specific camel referenced in this metaphor famously walked the desert for a hundred days. It tried to decide whether they wanted to be a Jurad or a Krystian, or whether these were different religions at all. After weighing all the arguments, they had finally decided, but alas, the unlucky camel broke their back and could not say their final wisdom.

A particularly unlucky letter mentioned Amor soldiers had burned down the church and killed them all. I told them to endure the suffering, but not strike back, as Krystina would have wanted. Some day soon, I preached again and again, Krystina would return and Judgement Day would arrive. The Second Coming of Krystina would reward all who were faithful and endured; and it would punish all that caused the suffering. The Gospylls I read were clear: this Second Coming would occur soon, within only one generation!

Only _after_ sending my reply did I wonder who sent me this letter in the first place, if they were all supposedly dead after the Amor attack.

Years went by, and I received more letters about this rebel called Timothy. By now, the town had also invented an entire _week_ of holidays called Krystmas. Little Timothy barely went to school and altogether ate far too much sugar. Whenever he learned a new part of the Krystian story, he would always say "This calls for a celebration!" or "We could make a holiday out of this!"

They were at their wit's end, which here means "didn't know what to do with Timothy anymore".

In my view, the little boy was right. The next step to growing Krystianity was to establish rituals and common practices. Nothing as strong as a habit. 

And I turned to the obvious animal to help me here: Timothy himself. 

I taught him how to write, so they could write the next Gospyll, because my panda paws are tired. And I suspect I'm not long for this world, with the current emperor of Amor---who literally calls himself Empero---blaming Krystians for the Great Fire of Amor and killing them all.

I have it on the highest authority that my Gospyll is completely accurate. Reliable sources tell me that I am a reliable source. This was the Gospyll according to Saulo, also known as Paulo, also known as The Panda Preacher.
