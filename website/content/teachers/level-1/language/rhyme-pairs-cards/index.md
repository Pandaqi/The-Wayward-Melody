This is a large collection of **rhyming pairs**. Two cards that rhyme with each other, shaped like puzzle pieces that fit together. Some common word sounds have multiple pairs, which even allows activities like finding _all_ the rhymes.

We created this product because we needed it for many of our (rhyme/language) games! When we were done, we realized some people might like to have these cards for their own purposes. And so we made them available separately.
