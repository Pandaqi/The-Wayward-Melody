**Trick or Treat Escape** is a Halloween escape room with differentiated difficulties. It means you can play the activity with kids young and old, depending on which puzzles you decide to include.

* The **basic set** uses no numbers or letters at all. Young kids should all be able to play. Playtime is short.
* The **advanced set** contains harder puzzles that you can _choose_ to include as well. This keeps it interesting for older kids or the teacher/parents playing along. Playtime is longer.

As always, we prepared a very special escape room for you with unique twists and creative thinking.

* **Quick Setup:** Cut out the homes, cut out the "treats", and you're done. The goal of the escape room is to match the right treat with the right home :)
* **Physical:** The 3D homes _are_ the puzzles. Players will constantly move around, pick things up, and have to inspect homes/treats from all sides.
* **Ink Friendly:** We always try to minimize the amount of paper and ink needed. Better for the climate, better for your wallet.
* **Satisfying puzzles:** We don't do boring "puzzles" that are really just homework exercises. Each puzzle requires some creative insight or clever logical leap. Puzzles sometimes work together. Once you discover it, solving the puzzle is easy.

If possible, we recommend **replacing the paper treats with _actual_ treats**. The escape room was designed to work just as well with actual candy. It makes it even more tangible ... and of course gives kids a nice reward at the end ;)

A perfect educational activity for Halloween, or maybe for a Halloween-themed party. Have fun!

_What if I have more than 5 players?_ Split them into groups. Print the escape room multiple times and let groups race against each other.

_Can the person who printed/prepared the escape room play too?_ They can, but the puzzles are likely too easy for them anyway.
