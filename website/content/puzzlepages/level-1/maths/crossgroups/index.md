**Crossgroups** is a slight evolution of [Box of Belonging](/puzzlepages/level-1/maths/box-of-belonging). Don't worry, you don't need to have played that puzzle to understand this one.

Box of Belonging simply asked you to figure out the secret groups inside a list of images. You had to realize, for example, that 3 of the images were _animals_ and 3 of them were _furniture_. A simple Grouping exercise for kids!

This puzzle takes it slightly further by placing those groups on a grid. Each row/column contains one of the groups you think you discovered. But rows and columns cross over, of course. And when they do ... that square needs something that belongs to _both groups_.

And so you get practice doing this skill _twice_. Figure out the general groups, then figure out the two or three things that belong to multiple groups at once and should be at those crossroads squares.

As usual, this starts really simple. Tiny puzzles with only a few images to place. Then it scales up, and a few rules twists are added for even more excitement, until you've become a grouping master without realizing it.

You can play this game **physically**, by cutting out the unique images at each puzzle and placing them _on_ the puzzle. You can also play by simply **drawing** the images you see into the spaces yourself. As long as you can somewhat recognize which of the images it's supposed to be, it's fine ;)

The final puzzles show you how much you've learned by solving these, as well as give a glimpse at what comes next by creating Crossgroups puzzles with _numbers_ or _letters_. (Which is very very close to actual _Crosswords_ puzzles, of course.)
