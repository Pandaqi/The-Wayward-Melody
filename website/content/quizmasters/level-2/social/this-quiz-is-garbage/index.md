This an educational quiz that teaches (young) kids about **waste and pollution**. It playfully explores waste sorting---what goes where---but also gives a sense of _why_ it's so bad and the terrifying _scale_ of garbage that humanity creates.

You can play the quiz the usual way---ask questions, check who knows the answer---but we also added an **interactive, physical version**. In this version, you place several "garbage bins" across your classroom and players answer by chugging waste into the right one! 

For an actual _game_, look at our entries in the Gamiverses category. For example, [The Ocean Cleanup Game](/gamiverses/level-2/social/the-ocean-cleanup-game/).

Like all our quizzes, it uses our advanced system that allows playing the quiz however you want: digitally, physically, hybrid, with custom answer sheets, and more.
