This is a quiz about **Summer** (the season). It contains questions about what typically happens in this season, interesting properties about it, and how to stay safe during it.

It's a perfect activity to play during the summer, especially with young kids or in the classroom!

It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically. The questions are short, simple, and varied. Almost all of them include an image. (A single question includes a video, but only as a bonus when the answer is revealed, so it's not crucial to have sound while playing this quiz.)

It's divided into 8 rounds, 62 questions total.

* General Knowledge
* Summer Blockbusters
* Summer Survival (_easy educational questions about good habits, such as using sunscreen; can be skipped_)
* Summer in the Animal Kingdom
* Summer Around the World
* Hard-Heating Questions (_some extra challenging questions if you're up to it; can be skipped_)
* Hot Numbers (_questions with numeric answers, where you're still correct if close enough_)
* Melting Points
