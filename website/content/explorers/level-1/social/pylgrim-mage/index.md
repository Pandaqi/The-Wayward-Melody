_Every kid in Educo must walk a "Pylgrimage": a journey from start to finish past Eduzones. Each stop is a building or magical area that teaches a new skill. And you physically CAN'T continue until you've aced their tests and beaten their challenges!_

_Need to learn reading a map? The building has infinite storeys and you have to find a different classroom every day. Need to learn counting? You're not allowed to pass the gate until you've perfectly counted all the sheep._

_Petyr doesn't want to do it. So much work. He's not interested in the difference between a square and a circle! And so, when a mysterious person visits and claims they can get Petyr to the end without having to do anything, he accepts ..._

This is a **bundle with 10 short stories** (or "chapters"). Each story is short and can be read standalone, but when you read them all (in order) you get a bigger connected story. In fact, each individual story explores a new "Eduzone" that's completely about the next skill on the path.

At its core, the story is to prepare kids for going to school, or help those who already go to school but struggle with the system. It explains the idea of "school", the idea of a curriculum and topics, using the fantastical analogy of the "Pylgrimage". It turns abstract "topics" and "information" into physical buildings, and games, and challenges, and magical classrooms completely designed to immerse you in _that topic_.

It also, however, discusses something more fundamental and more useful: **learning how to learn**. Why is learning new skills important? What works best for our brains? What is effective and what is just busywork? In a sense, this story explains why our online store works the way it does and how best to ... travel through its offerings. 

It's also why this story and its world reappears in every level of the webshop. After reading it all, you've seen a quick overview or summary of the main skills that level tried to teach.
