# The Watchers of Face

Once upon a time, there was a folk called the FACE. They lived in magical faraway lands, very different from any other place. Every day children would sit on the highest cliff they could climb. And they'd watch, and watch even longer, but what did they hope to find?

To help you understand, let's follow young girl Zaram. She woke up that day, like any day, and immediately stumbled to her mom. She playfully pulled her cheeks, studied mother's freckles and said: "Let me see your face again, so I will never forget."

She repeated this every day, she would never think to quit. Though sometimes she said: "Let me see your face a bit longer, so I will always recognize it."

Sometimes her parents, though, pushed her away. Because Zaram was special: she was the princess of the FACE. Her parents had very important tasks, being king and queen. A child staring at them all day would only come between.

Sometimes, though rarely, visitors would come. They'd frown, and laugh, at all the children intensely staring at their mom. For hours the FACE could study their reflection in the water. And then they'd go back to staring ... at their father.

Until that dreadful day, the one Zaram had feared. She woke up, looked everywhere, but her parents had disappeared!

Such was the way it goes, lovely listeners, with the magical FACE. Once someone was old enough, they had to leave the place. Nobody knew why, nobody understood the spell. They only knew you had to wait a hundred years, until you could return and all was well.

But a face can change a lot, you know, give it a hundred years. Most of the parents who returned only saw confusion and tears.

Zaram hoped she'd done enough. Every day she'd check if she still recognized her parent's snout. But as they stayed away, for years and years, she started to doubt.

Now the FACE were in chaos, lacking a leader! Who held the magic and made the decisions? Who made sure to feed her?

Until the watchers on the cliff saw a hedgehog approach. He walked into their camp and confidently spoke:

"It is I, your king, and I have finally returned! Now give me the gold and the power I have earned."

The children bowed to him, but Zaram wasn't convinced in the least. Sure, a person can change---but into a completely different beast!?

"You are not my father," said Zaram, "why do you come here to lie?"

And the hedgehog admitted he, like many others, had overheard a rumor about the FACE. And sure, before long, many animals came to visit the place!

"I am your king!" the males said.

"I am your queen, I have returned!" the females would add.

Zaram would watch them, from afar and from nearby. She'd pull their cheeks, and count their freckles, but it always ended with _goodbye_.

There were too many! And how could she be sure? Exhausted and overwhelmed, she turned them _all_ away.

This did not dissuade the tricksters. They still came, but now in disguise. They learned their language, painted their faces, and copied their sayings oh so wise. 

Every time it was harder and harder to see if they were their lost queen. Until one person succeeded, and made quite a scene.

She gave orders like "everyone wears eyepatches", and "build a statue in my name". Until Zaram caught her trying to steal their magic, and furiously sent her away!

Zaram grew older and got children of her own. Now _she_ was watched all day, helping her children remember her form. She had them sketch her face and chant her name. Until she looked at her reflection in the water, and realized: _What's the same thing ... may not always stay the same._

Her face had more lines in it than before, her ears seemed larger. Her hair was changing color, and her nose reached farther. She was still the same Zaram, the same princess as before. But she looked very different, now a mother of four.

And then she realized her major mistake. All those years ago---she sent her real parents away! They were in that crowd of tricksters, she remembered that now. Ashamed and sad, she fled that same night, hoping she could still find them somehow.

And so it is, lovely listeners, her children now watch from the cliff. They don't understand what drove mother Zaram away, and they call their lands cursed. But they are _determined_ to still recognize her, when---if---she returns.
