title: "Quiz Adventure"

{{% double-view %}}

{{% double-view-item %}}

Quiz Adventures are **quiz experiences** that do _not_ use our custom framework for digital/physical quizzes. Instead, they are often a **custom adventure** using physical attributes, storytelling, and unique rules.

They blur the lines between "activity" and "quiz". You might need to answer questions by _doing_ something. You might need to look for the questions in the first place.

{{% /double-view-item %}}

{{% double-view-item %}}

{{% section-foldable header="Why would I be interested?" %}}

Their biggest advantage over our usual Trivia Kits is that they allow **much more variety and creativity**. If we're not bound to our system, to a typical quiz framework, then _anything_ is possible!

This allows Quiz Adventures to be more physical. To have bigger reveals and tougher questions. To allow players to _figure out_ the answers, instead of merely testing _if they already know it_.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="Why would I not be interested?" %}}

The biggest disadvantage of Quiz Adventures (compared to our regular Trivia Kits) is that they **have more requirements**. They require preparation and setup. Perhaps they require a large room, or a number of players that's large enough. 

You "lose" the absolute simplicity and instant-start of a pub quiz. Because Quiz Adventures are a "bigger" activity that requires a bit more.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="How is the product delivered?" %}}

You always get a **ZIP** file. When extracted, it usually contains,

* A PDF with all the _questions_.
* A PDF with all the _answers_.
* A PDF with a "guide" for whoever runs the experience, telling them how it works and what to do.
* A PDF with any material needed to make it all work.
