This is a bundle of pen-and-paper games based on the (somewhat well-known) **Game of Sim**. 

The first page explains how that game works, which is a great introduction to these types of games and usually adored by young Apprentices. It's a two-player game where, every turn, you simply draw a line between two points (that don't have a line yet). The first one who is forced to create a triangle this way loses.

Then, however, we take that idea in interesting directions by applying it to _other basic shapes_. And, when needed, by twisting the rules to challenge the players even more.

These games are simple. Most of them just have you draw a single line from point A to point B on your turn, until some condition is met. But with every attempt, you discover a better first move, a deeper strategy, a new way to look at the shape and its connections.
