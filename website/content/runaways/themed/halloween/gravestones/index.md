Gravestones is a unique **escape room** for kids and families that's a perfect activity for **Halloween** (or the fall holidays).

Players are presented an empty graveyard with 16 spots ... and 15 "gravestones" to place. **Each gravestone has a simple puzzle** that tells you _where_ it should go. They unlock new puzzles as you go, so you're never overwhelmed and players can "parallel puzzle" instead of all being stuck on the same one.

When all is said and done, only _one_ empty spot should remain: Dracula's grave!

This means the escape room ...

* **Requires no preparation.** Just cut out the graves and fold them once (so they stand upright), then place the beautiful graveyard map on the table.
* **Does not rely on color or extra work.** You can print in grayscale if you want. We do our best to minimize paper waste or printing costs. After printing 11 pages, you need _nothing else_.
* **Is very physical.** You're not just staring at papers or riddles. You're actively placing the tombstones in a 2D space as you fill up the graveyard. It looks nice on the table ;)
* **Is suitable for all ages.** Each puzzle is extremely small and many of them have no text or numbers at all. Though each player should absolutely have _some_ basic reading and counting ability, which is why we marked it 7+.
* **Will lead to many satisfying revelations.** We don't do boring "puzzles" that are really just homework exercises. Each puzzle requires some creative insight, some clever thought, a change in perspective---once you get it, you immediately know where that gravestone goes. Later puzzles rely on earlier ones. The graveyard is used extensively to expand what's possible.

A perfect educational/classroom activity, or a family activity for Halloween, or maybe a Halloween-themed party. Have fun!

_What if I have more than 6 players?_ Split them into groups. Print the escape room multiple times and let groups race against each other.

_Can the person who printed/prepared the escape room play too?_ Not really, sorry! Simply seeing all the graves and the "victory card" (once you find Dracula's Grave) probably gives you too many hints. We aim to make a Halloween escape room in the future for adults/older ages that _will_ allow even the person who prepared it to play.
