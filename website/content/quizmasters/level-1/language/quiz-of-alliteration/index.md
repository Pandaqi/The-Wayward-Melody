This is a **digital quiz** (with 4 short rounds of increasing difficulty) because it needs to play **sound** for almost every question!

After making the [Quiz of Rhyme](/quizmasters/level-1/language/quiz-of-rhyme/), we thought it would be fun to create a quiz about alliteration too. The Quiz of Rhyme was already pretty long and rich, so we didn't want to just tack it at the end there. Instead, we made this separate quiz, and made it **freely available**!

Alliteration is a brother to rhyme. Instead of wanting the _final sounds_ of words to match, you want the _first sound_ of words to match. That's it. This quiz tests the skill in many fun ways, so that Apprentices are truly ready for the next step in language awareness (which is phonemes).
