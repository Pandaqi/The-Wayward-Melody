This product contains **70 pentomino puzzles**. You get some recognizable image ("silhouette"), such as one of a bird or a house. You have to _recreate_ that image by combining the "pentomino" pieces given to you!

The puzzles start easy (only 4 pieces), but grow to the full set (12 pieces) towards the end.

Each puzzle has _its own page_, which allows you to use it like a **"puzzle mat"**: solve it by placing the pentominoes directly on the page, until you've filled the entire silhouette. That same page also has a **hint** and the **full solution** at the bottom.

But you can solve these puzzles on any surface (in any way you like), by simply picking a silhouette from the overview page to recreate. 

These kinds of puzzles also have the nice feature, in practice, of being a good puzzle for _adults_ too ;) Just try to solve some of the harder ones and wait until there's a crowd around you trying to be helpful ...

Can't get enough of Pentominoes? Or you'd rather play a game with others instead of alone? Then try out [Pentomino Games](/gamiverses/level-1/tessellas-toolbox/pentomino-games/) instead!

This product is part of **"Tessella's Toolbox"**. "Tessellation" means the act of dividing a larger shape or image into smaller parts, such as creating a mosaic or a jigsaw puzzle. Pentominoes are just one of many ways to do this, as this bundle asks you to combine them in such a way that you recreate the image of an animal or other common object. If you liked this, check out the many _other_ puzzles/challenges in this project. A good recommendation is to play the [Tricksy Tangrams](/puzzlepages/level-1/tessellas-toolbox/tricksy-tangrams/)
