**Numnogram** is a puzzle variant inspired by the famous **Nonograms**. Those puzzles give you numeric hints at the edges, and if you follow the rules and fill in the squares, you end up with a recognizable _image_. 

As the name suggests, Numnograms does the same ... but with _no numbers_. We found several ways to remove the requirement that you must be able to count, while keeping the very nice feature that every puzzle gives you a picture.

* The clues at the edges are not numbers, but literal _squares_. You merely need to literally copy them into the empty spaces below.
* The first puzzles stop right there, because that's already a good challenge to understand for very young kids.
* But, at higher difficulties, the puzzles turn into actual puzzles by asking you to think about exactly _where_ to copy the squares too. Because there can be any amount of empty spaces between them. The only way to know the right place, is by checking both the horizontal and the vertical clue(s).

It also contains a level with _multiple colors_, which leads to prettier pictures and a slightly different puzzle. It only requires a basic set of coloring pencils, though we do also support playing with a single color still.

The final puzzles are challenging on their own, and also prepare the player for _actual_ Nonograms by showing _numbers_ on the clues as well.
