This quiz has only one type of question.

> You are shown a bunch of things. Can you figure out which ones are connected, and **which one doesn't belong?**

It's a fun blend of basic problem solving skills, some creativity, and a lot of common knowledge about the world.

As usual, we included as many questions as we could think of, giving you as much as we can for the price. It is, however, certainly not required to play the whole thing in one go. That would take a while! You can **pick which rounds you want to play**. There is, however, a question that only makes sense once you've played the whole quiz ...

The rounds included are,

* General Knowledge
* Geography
* Nature
* Science & Tech
* Music
* Media
* ... mystery round ...
* (Pop) Culture
* Sports & Games
* Stories
* Food
* History

{{% remark %}}
Unlike most of our quizzes, this one has NO images or interactive elements. We chose this to make it very easy to take the quiz with you on paper and play physically too, and to make this type of question more practical, because you're not wondering "what tiny detail in this image/song/video do I need to focus on".
{{% /remark %}}
