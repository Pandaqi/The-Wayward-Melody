This is a game for all ages, even pre-readers, about **telling stories together**! 

The game comes with our _Pre-Reader Prompt Cards_ (a huge set of clear illustrations for common/easy words) for free, but also includes a second set of story-specific prompts (such as an icon of a knight, wizard, or other typical story element). On your turn, you simply draw several of these cards and must then tell a story that _must_ include these topics.

Then, there are several ways to play.

* The easiest mode is just to improvise these stories as well as you can.
* You can go one step further by assigning a "Story Master" each round, which picks the story they liked the best and gives them points.
* Or you can make it a full "game" by introducing our simple variants. For example, after everyone has told their story, players are asked to guess what the "prompts" were in another player's story. You'll notice it's much easier to remember that ... if the story was _good_!

This makes Storymprovise a very _flexible_ game for all ages. You can be cooperative or competitive, go for creativity or strict point scoring, as long as you keep improvising cool stories based on simple prompts :)
