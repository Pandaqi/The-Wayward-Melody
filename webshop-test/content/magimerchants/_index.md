{{% section-foldable header="What can I expect?" %}}

We're a very practical bunch of wizards over here. We mostly sell ...

* Merchandise that actually brings new value (such as a bag for storing things).
* Merchandise that you don't need to "fit" or "judge" in real life (to prevent situations like a T-shirt being the complete wrong size).

Most merchandise is directly attached to a brand, such as _Wildebyte Arcades_ or _Pandaqi Games_. Some stand on their own and were created just because someone woke up at night with a brilliant idea for a pun---or so they believed.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="Do you make these yourself?" %}}

No, no, no. We'd love to be able to handcraft these, but **unfortunately, we don't know the right spell!** 

We _design_ them, sure, using our assets and previous work. But we use several popular "print-on-demand" services for creation and delivery.
