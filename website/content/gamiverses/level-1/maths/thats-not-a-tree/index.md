**That's Not A Tree** is a fun and simple game, supporting large player counts, about learning to identify _groups_. Should these things be part of the same group? Are _all_ things in this group the same or not? It's a more involved continuation of our simplest grouping game called [Meadowmaster](/gamiverses/level-1/maths/meadowmaster/).

This game is meant for young kids (pre-readers and pre-counters), which means the Guardian (parent / teacher) plays a big role. You are basically trying to _fool_ the kids half the time! You reveal a random tile and claim it's part of some group. The players must react quickly if they think you are wrong, to prevent it from going to the wrong group. But they obviously should _not_ interfere if you're actually speaking the truth.

Once players understand this base game, you can move to the upgrades. They add _more_ possible groups (or "categories") to play with, a simple start with double grouping (subgroups within groups), and even a small competitive mode that a single player can win on their own.

Like most of our games, it's designed to be quick and educational fun with any group of (very) young players. The game's title---"That's Not A Tree!"---is likely to be shouted a hundred times as you play the game over and over. At the end, players should be very comfortable with identifying groups based on certain properties, as well as knowledgeable about some major groups they will encounter in the real world (such as "animals").
