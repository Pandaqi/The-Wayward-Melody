A **CoupleQuiz** is a special activity designed to experience as a couple (e.g. on a date night), or when playing with multiple couples. It works best with romantic couples (heterosexual), but can be enjoyed by people with any kind of relationship if needed. 

How does it work? This variant (_Sexuality_) is basically a light-hearted **Battle of the Sexes**! It has questions about romance, relationships, the ways in wich men and women are different or the same, what studies show about how to be a good couple, and more.

* You can play as a couple, against one another, to find out who knows best. 
* You can play with a larger group, say "Team Girls" against "Team Boys".
* Or you can cooperate with your partner and play against other couples.

Simple. Fun. Nothing else needed. A great low-stakes way to spend some time together and perhaps learn a thing or two.

This specific CoupleQuiz is also suitable for high schools. It's just a fun and easy way to introduce topics (such as sexuality or relationships) that can be tough to introduce otherwise. It's a _game_, on purpose, not a textbook list of facts to know about men, women, or sex.

You can display the questions on a screen (using our digital quiz tool) or print out the physical version. As usual, we provide a large number of diverse questions, but you can simply skip rounds if you want to save time or don't like them. (Or spread the quiz over multiple moments.)

The rounds provided are,

* **Common Knowledge** (variety of general warm-up questions)
* **Battle of the Sexes** (simple questions about which sex has more of a certain biological trait)
* **In His Shoes** (about things men wish more women would know/understand)
* **In Her Shoes** (about things women wish more men would know/understand)
* **What's Love Got To Do With It?** (about, well, love and everything it touches)
* **Happy Marriage, Happy Life?** (about long-term relationships, marriage)
* **Summer of 69** (about sex and intimacy)
* **What Would You Do?** (bonus round with no right/wrong answers, but reveals different perspectives from different players)

This quiz is "competitive" in a fun, light-hearted way that brings the best out of people. (Or, even better, just confirms you are equally wise in the ways of relationships and a perfect match for one another!) If you're more about keeping things collaborative and personal, try the [CoupleQuiz: Harmony](/quizmasters/themed/holidays/valentine/couplequiz-harmony/) instead.

Have fun!
