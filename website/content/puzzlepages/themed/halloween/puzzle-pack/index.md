This bundle contains **40 pages of puzzles with a Halloween theme**! 

* **3 difficulty levels**: differentiated puzzles to suit someone's ability. Puzzles sometimes return later but with a twist to make it more challenging.
* Sorted from **easiest to hardest**. This makes the bundle suitable for a wide range of ages and skill. Many of the 1-star puzzles can be played by young kids who can barely read or count; many of the 3-star puzzles are great for high ability students and even fun for adults. 
* Many pages contain multiple puzzles of the same type, bringing the **total number of puzzles higher than the number of pages**. (Example: a very simple puzzle to teach the rules, then a harder one, then the hardest one.)
* A **wide variety of puzzles**. Some are about language, some about numbers, some about logic, some visual, and some about a mixture of those things.
* **Ink Friendly**: printing in color is _not_ required and we try to be minimal in our designs.

Some puzzles are adaptations/twists on a familiar formula, such as Sudoku, while others are unique Halloween puzzles we invented ourselves. As you can see from this online store, we are puzzle enthusiasts and don't want to bore kids with five more Word Searches ;)

As expected, all illustrations are a "cute" version of Halloween. Nothing that's actually scary, or gory, or off-putting. The only requirement, really, is that the puzzler can hold a pencil and draw lines.
