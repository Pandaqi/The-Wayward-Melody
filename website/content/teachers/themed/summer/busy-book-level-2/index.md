This is a book of exercises and solo/group activities for the youngest children (Level 2; up to age 6/7) about **Summer** and the **Summer Holiday**! It's colorful and thematic, fun to look at and play around with, as you explore the different challenges and exercises connected to summer.

About half of the pages require cutting out some material, and half do not. A large chunk of the busy book is educational, practicing core skills like counting, alphabet, logical problem solvoing, and more. As the name suggests, however, the _busy book_ is first and foremost meant as a fun and easy way to kill some time during the summer!

Because this book is for _Level 2_, it assumes children have at least a very basic understanding of numbers and letters. If their understanding is good enough, they can enjoy and understand this book completely on their own. Otherwise, a bit of help by a Guardian might be necessary. (For younger children, try the [Level 1 Busy Book](/teachers/themed/summer/busy-book-level-1).)

For actual _puzzles_ (e.g. sudoku, crosswords), as always, check out the Summer materials in the Puzzlepages section. For a full _escape room_ to play during summer or while camping somewhere, check out the Summer materials in the Runaways section.
