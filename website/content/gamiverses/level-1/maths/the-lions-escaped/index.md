**The Lions Escaped!** is a physical, quick, educational game that explores "matching" (or "visual discrimination"). Are these two things the same? And if not, what is different? It does so in a very physical and intuitive way, giving players plenty movement as they try to physically cage the escaped lions. 

Every round, you reveal a card like "has a tail" or "has two ears". Immediately, all at once, the players have to move their cage and actually "capture" one of the many lions on the table that matches!

The game explores matching prowess at two levels.

* The first game mode explained keeps it simple by making all cards _lions_. It is about matching _features_ of the lion, such as having a tail or not.
* But it also has an expansion/variant that introduces new animals. It also tests matching _the entire animal_ instead of _features_. Especially when combined with that first mode, you will get a fun game that challenges the skills of Apprentices.

For these reasons we'd call this a slightly more advanced "matching game" than something like [T-Rex Tells](/gamiverses/level-1/maths/t-rex-tells/).
