## Any Questions?

{{% section-foldable header="For whom are these materials intended?" %}}

Anyone can use them, of course. Education and fun activities are not bound to age or classroom.

They are, however, mainly used by **teachers** and **parents**. These are not intended to _replace_ an explanation or test. They are bonus material to help strengthen, expand or simply periodically remind someone of a skill or topic.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="What do I get?" %}}

Everything provided in this category is a (digital) **PDF file**, with (infrequently) extra files or interactive components. A single printer or screen is enough to enjoy them all. 

In many cases, you can give them to your kid/pupil to enjoy on their own. But we'll always recommend actually _sharing_ the activity with them.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="How am I allowed to use these materials?" %}}

**However you like!** Share them with an entire class. Prepare one as a surprise gift. Customize it for your kid.

You don't need a special license or permission for anything. You bought the experience---it's yours to experience it as you wish.
