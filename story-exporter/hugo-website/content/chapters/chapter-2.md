# The Sea of Wishy Wetter

Comm the Pear looked everywhere. He was a fruit and held a lot of water inside. You might not know this, dear listener, bit living fruit needs to drink to stay alive!

And so, he thought, all pears love to be near water. A nice ocean is the best place to live for his soon-to-be daughter.

He asked some friendly bananas: "Where can I find the sea?"

They peeled their attention away from the yellow sun, and said: "Oh, it's just behind that tree."

Comm ran that way, smiling and full of glee.

But the bananas stopped him. "Watch out, though, it's full of _tea_!"

Huh?

When Comm reached the beach, he saw what they meant. The ocean was brown, and hot, and had a vague lavender scent.

That's not good, he thought. My daughter might not even like tea! And so he kept searching for a different kind of sea.

He left the forest and ran, until he saw waves again. He made a new plan: this ocean would be his daughter's best friend.

But as he came close, he saw the waves were lemonade! That's not good enough, he thought, it might be a drink his daughter hates.

And so he kept searching for something better, maybe a lake. He found one made of chocolate milk, and one made of milkshake. 

As evening fell, he stumbled upon a river made of apple juice. Not great for washing yourself, he thought. By now he felt that he could only lose.

Every sea he found was even worse than before. Some were tastier, some were wetter. But none of them were clearly better.

Would his daughter like tea? Or would she like lemonade more? Or would she actually hate to live on a boring water shore?

As the moon rose, he was tired and unsure. He decided to stay where he was, around a lake with water somewhat pure. It wasn't great, but it was better than the home he was living in. It had no beach nearby at all, so Comm himself never even learned to swim!

The nice thing about lakes, of course, is that they're very big. Comm hoped to find a cozy place around it where his daughter would fit.

Maybe, he thought, water isn't important after all. Maybe what his daughter really needs, is a home that's very big and very tall ...
