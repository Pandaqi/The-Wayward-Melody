This is a pub quiz themed around **Holidays & Vacation**. It mostly focuses on the biggest of them all: the **summer holiday** and tourism/travels at that time. (Other holidays, such as Christmas or Halloween, typically get their own dedicated quizzes.) 

It's a perfect activity to play on a summer's night while on vacation! 

It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically. The questions are short, simple, and varied. Almost all of them include an image. One round also includes questions with sound.

It's divided into **8 rounds, 84 questions** total. As usual, each round is self-contained, so you can skip rounds for a **shorter experience**.

* Word To The Wise (guess travel tips and specific advice for dealing with local culture on holiday)
* General Knowledge
* Holidays Around The World
* Travel Trouble
* Tourist Attractions
* Mystery Theme Round (questions that are all connected by a secret theme you can guess)
* It's A Wonderful World (connect flags to landmarks/tourist attractions/interesting holiday facts)
* Summer Songs (listen to a part of a summer/holiday song, name title/artist)

This quiz is the "pro" version, with more questions, harder questions, and more creative types of questions. It's not extremely hard, but it also contains no obvious easy questions. For a summer quiz that's playable in a more leisurely way, and by young kids too, try the [Quiz of Holiday Family](/quizmasters/themed/holidays/quiz-of-holiday-family/).
