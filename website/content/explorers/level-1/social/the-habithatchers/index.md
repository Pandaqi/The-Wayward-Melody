This **bundle of 10 short stories** (or "chapters") explores the topic of habits, routine and patterns. Each story is short and can be read standalone, but when you read them all (in order) you get a bigger connected story.

The stories take place in a strange place where _everything_---and then we mean _everything_---follows some strict schedule and routine. Everything has rules. Everything must happen at a specific moment, and it always does, and it never happens at any other time.

In fact, there's a honorable job in this world with the responsibility to **hatch habits**. That's the _only_ way to get a new habit---a new rule.

Well, needless to say, some creatures are not happy with this. They want to learn to play the guitar, but they can't buy the habit. They want to sleep late when they feel like it, but it's not allowed. One of them even wants to change the seasons---breaking that pattern---because the winter is always so cold.

Follow the Habitbreakers as they explore this world and try to make it better! Will they forge the right habits and remove the bad ones? Or will everyone be too stuck in their routines to be ready for The Great Surprise?
