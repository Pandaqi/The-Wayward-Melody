This quiz has only one type of question.

> You are shown a bunch of things. Can you figure out which ones are connected, and **which one doesn't belong?**

This requires being able to discover patterns and similarities between the things. Being able to label them as a certain group, and noticing that only one element doesn't match the group. It's a fun blend of basic skills (comparing, grouping, matching), and some creativity and general knowledge of the world.

It's mostly meant as an **educational quiz** (in the classroom, for example). It's recommended to play one or a few rounds at a time (it has a _lot_ of questions) to gauge the general knowledge of your Apprentices. The questions are also easy and generic enough to **play with a family/casual audience**, though you might want to leave out a few rounds to shorten the duration.

The rounds included are,

* General Knowledge
* Geography
* Nature
* History
* Science & Tech
* Music
* Media
* (Pop) Culture
* Language

Of course, you can "connect" and "associate" in many ways, so the given answer is not necessarily the only one that's correct! If players picked something else, let them explain. And if their explanation is strong enough, reward them too :)

{{% remark %}}
Unlike most of our quizzes, this one has no images or interactive elements. We chose this to make it very easy to take the quiz with you on paper and play physically too, and to make this type of question more practical, because you're not wondering "what tiny detail in this image/song/video do I need to focus on".
