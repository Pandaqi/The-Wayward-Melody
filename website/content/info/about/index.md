## The Three Laws of Melodycoin

_The Wayward Melody_ does things a bit differently. It has a few Magical Webshopping Laws designed to prevent needless consumerism or shady tactics, to be kind to the climate, and to make it possible for us to sustainably offer these services.

> <span class="text-highlight">Law 1: Physical items shall arrive whenever they arrive.</span>&nbsp;<span class="text-unhighlight">(This rarely takes longer than 2 weeks, though. You'll get a tracking url.)</span>

> <span class="text-highlight">Law 2: Recycling shall trump refunds and returns.</span>&nbsp;<span class="text-unhighlight">(We do not offer the option to refund or return, for both digital and physical goods.)</span>

> <span class="text-highlight">Law 3: Thou shall reimburse at least 35 euros, but never shipping fees.</span>&nbsp;<span class="text-unhighlight">(Any order with physical items will cost at least 35 euros. Above that, there are no shipping fees.)</span>

Most of all, however, we at _The Wayward Melody_ obey the 0th law.

> <span class="text-highlight">Law 0: Kindness and sharing shall rule most of all.</span>&nbsp;<span class="text-unhighlight">(I'm an artist and human being. I only reluctantly monetize because I need money to survive. You can always contact me, and maybe we can work something out.)</span>

See the [Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](/info/faq/) for explanations for why these laws exist, and more specifics about how they apply.

## What spells do we cast?

This webshop sells both _digital goods_ (files you can immediately download) and _physical goods_ (physical products that are shipped and delivered to you).

Our spellbook mostly provides **activities and experiences**! Educational activities, escape rooms, pub quizzes, board games, and more. These are often digital goods that you can print or execute on your computer yourself, to create a fun experience at home with your family or friends. I am a big proponent of _learning_ (while having fun) and providing more _real-life (social) experiences_ to share with your loved ones.

On top of that, we offer **merchandise for our most popular works**. For example, you can buy designs from the Wildebyte Arcades, a long-running book series about someone stuck inside the world of video games.

And finally, we offer **bonus material** (on top of something else that might be free or sold through other channels, like bookstores). For example, you can buy all the source files and original assets for some of my projects, or extra short stories to devour between physical books in the same series.

_The Wayward Melody_ is a shared universe that contains many other websites. They might link to or promote products from within this webshop, when its relevant to the page you're visiting. Once clicked, they should refer you to _this_ domain.

## Who is this boy that created the shop?

Hi, I'm Tiamo Pastoor! I'm a freelance artist in the Netherlands. I've been producing professional works in dozens of different creative categories for 15+ years now. 

For the longest time, these weren't that great. That's where everyone starts. 

Then I became professional. I actually registered a company, started offering my services, and built 5 huge websites that are visited thousands of times a day now. I did all of that, however, for free. I was only concerned with _giving_ and _sharing_. With making my work as accessible as possible, never asking money or even so much as a donation.

Obviously, by the time this little boy became an adult, I had no funds and no income for the foreseeable future. But I _did_ need to eat---I'm not _that_ magical. After gaining enough experience, I decided to make a smart plan to turn _some_ of what I do into an income.

This webshop is the result of all that. A nice and cozy home for some of my _paid_ work. I feel somewhat comfortable giving these things a price tag, while keeping everything else I make as free and accessible as before.

This online store has grown into a massive project that aims to reinvent the entire curriculum. Tiamo started it, but he's not the only reason it exists anymore ;)
