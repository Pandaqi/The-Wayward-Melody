**Strateshape** is probably the most challenging of all the "shape games" we've made. Not because the rules are complicated, but because it requires a bit of counting skill (though a Guardian can help here) and because finding the best move requires a lot of strategy and spatial awareness.

Cards show random shapes, with colored stars inside. On your turn, you have to overlap an existing card with yours, and make sure one of your shapes _lines up_ with the card below. This allows many creative ways to overlap other cards, without being so easy that you don't have to think about it.

Your goal? Make sure _your_ colored stars appear the most, and you cover up _other player's_ colored stars!

A simple shape game with a lot of ... tactics and _strateshape_.
