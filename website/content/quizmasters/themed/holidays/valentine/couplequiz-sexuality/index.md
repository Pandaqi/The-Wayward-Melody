A **CoupleQuiz** is a special activity designed to experience as a couple (e.g. on a date night), or when playing with multiple couples. It works best with romantic couples (heterosexual), but can be enjoyed by people with any kind of relationship if needed. 

This specific CoupleQuiz is also suitable for (high) schools. It's just a fun and easy way to introduce topics (such as sexuality or relationships) that can be tough to introduce otherwise.

How does it work? This variant (_Sexuality_) is basically a light-hearted **Battle of the Sexes**! It has questions about romance, relationships, the ways in wich men and women are different or the same, what studies show about how to be a good couple, and more.

* You can play as a couple, against one another, to find out who knows best. 
* You can play with a larger group, say "Team Girls" against "Team Boys".
* Or you can cooperate with your partner and play against other couples.

Simple. Fun. Nothing else needed. A great low-stakes way to spend some time together and perhaps learn a thing or two.

You can display the questions on a screen (using our digital quiz tool) or print out the physical version.
