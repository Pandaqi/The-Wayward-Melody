The **Rainbow Market** is our most basic, entry-level (digital) puzzle game about **colors**.

On the easiest difficulty,

* You get a color to place. (The entire map turns that color to make it obvious.)
* Tap an animal that _wants_ the color to "deliver" it.
* Make all animals happy! (That is, clear the field.)

It contains 5 difficulty levels. Later difficulties, for example, add animals that will also _give you something back_. So you need to think a few moves ahead, making sure you get that GREEN from the cow because you need it to make that horse happy.

As expected, the game has no text, numbers, or complex actions. Just tap the animals in the right order. But thanks to these simple rules, it is an actual puzzle that requires matching colors and thinking logically.

{{% remark %}}
Whenever you match a color, a narrator will also say the word to reinforce it. This can be turned off in the settings. The game contains infinite puzzles and you can retry as often as you want without penalty.
{{% /remark %}}

Once your kid is comfortable with this puzzle, you can consider moving to more challenging ones such as ...

* [Potion Puzzle](/puzzlepages/level-1/maths/video-games/potion-puzzle/): introduces **more logic and more colors** as you try to change the contents of potions to be fully one color.
* [Colorcross](/puzzlepages/level-1/maths/video-games/colorcross/): introduces **mixing colors** (predicting what they will become is needed to solve the simple puzzles)
