Falling Leaves is a **set of 7 educational games** using **the same material**. Each game ...

* Has the same **simple setup**: throw the leaves in the air and let them fall randomly!
* Has at most **1 page of rules** (which includes variants, expansions, example images, everything)
* Teaches and practices a **specific subject/skill** (addition, spelling, memory, matching, planning ahead, etc.)

Some games are cooperative (you work together), some are competitive (one player wins). Some games have players all act at the same time, others are turn-based.

We created this set of games to play **during Autumn/Fall**. All games are very simple to teach and use the (fallen) leaves in different ways. You save a lot of time by only having to cut out the leaves _once_ (to play all the games).

The material is colorful, but printing in color is _not_ required. You can decide to cut the leaves accurately (which takes more effort), but we also indicate a "simpler" shape around the leaves.

Have fun!
