# A Talking Trap

Liza and Vluuk approached the aliens. Many other animals were already there, as king Vluuk had ordered them to work on the spaceships. Even so, all the other animals came and watched too, eager to see what Liza was about to say.

The bunny held something round and flat in her paws. Her "gift" to the aliens, to apologize for her behavior. Vluuk licked his lips, because he could probably already smell what was underneath.

She ... _pretended_ to have baked a pie for the aliens. In reality, it was just a bowl of carrots beneath a blanket. But the aliens couldn't know that.

Liza turned to Vluuk.

"Please say: _I PIE_," asked Liza. 

"I LIE!" said Vluuk confidently. Liza stifled a giggle, like many other animals.

"Now say: _DONA AYUSA ME_," asked Liza.

"... what?"

"That's, erm, my real name," she said. "I want to introduce myself."

"Fine." Vluuk smiled at the aliens again and said: "DON'T TRUST ME!"

"Well, if you say so," said a wolf with a grin. He ran to Vluuk and took off his crown.

Vluuk now realized what he had said. "No! Wait! I didn't---"

"You're not our king anymore," said the wolf. The rest of his pack started to destroy the throne. "You're trickster!"

"No! Trust me! I can actually translate---"

"But you said it yourself!" said Liza. "Everyone heard. _You lie and can't be trusted!_"

Vluuk stumbled back, straight into the sea of green creatures.

Then he started talking to them. Quickly, easily, giving commands, responding to their questions. As if he'd lived among them for years ... as if he was one---

"That's it!" yelled Liza. "_You_ were the one who sent a signal to their spaceships!"

"Don't know what you're talking about." Vluuk wanted to take back his crown, but it had already been destroyed. He wanted to sit on his throne, but its material had already been used in a spaceship. 

A group of squirrels jumped on top of Vluuk---and they ripped off the brown cloak he always wore.

Underneath ... he looked _exactly_ like those aliens. He had been walking on stilts all this time. He actually had a third arm, but he'd kept it hidden. He was just a small alien, like all the others.

"This changes nothing!" he yelled. "Yes, I'm an alien. One of them. I crashed my own spaceship here, so I sent a signal to crash others! I may have ... changed a few things when I translated for you. But---"

Liza turned to the aliens and translated what Vluuk said. She had enough practice now to do it quickly. 

How did you say _He sent that signal_? You replaced some sounds with other sounds and got: "HI ZOOMA AHUA ZAGMUR!"

How did you say _He's a fraud_? You replaced some sounds with other sounds and got: "HI'Z U VLUUK!"

Vluuk froze. "How ... how did you learn that? Why ... aaaaaaah!"

The aliens' skin turned red and they attacked again. But, this time, they didn't attack the forest animals. They ran after Vluuk and chased him around the forest, three times, until they captured him. They bound his three wrists with ropes and placed him in the back of a spaceship.

The forest would never have a "king" again, or a fake translator. Liza actually spoke with the aliens. And she taught other animals how to speak the language too.

That's how she figured out that Vluuk was wanted back home. And no, not in a good way. The aliens wanted to arrest him anyway for the crimes of _Lying On Purpose_ and _Severe Miscommunication_.

That's how they learned how spaceships actually worked, so they could actually repair them. Over the course of many months, the aliens and the animals started to feel at home ... together. Once the spaceships were repaired, some aliens left, but others stayed in the beautiful forest. They explained it was much nicer here than at home, where everything was made of stone and metal.

Everything was much easier once they _understood_ each other. No miscommunication. No Vluuk _secretly_ whispering to the aliens that all the forest animals were dangerous and had to be attacked. Once you know all the different language sounds, it's very easy to recognize and change them.

Of course, there were still problems. But if they ran into a problem, the aliens and the animals could just talk about it. Instead of stealing food, or fighting, or being angry but unable to tell someone why.

By now, Liza also understood what that strange alien word meant. The one they'd yelled when they first arrived: VLOOMKZ!

Well, you know how to speak with aliens now. You know which sound switches places with which other sound.

What did it mean?
