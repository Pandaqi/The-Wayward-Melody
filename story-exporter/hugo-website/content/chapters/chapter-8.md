# The Tops of Turn Around

Comm the pear looked everywhere. He was a fruit that currently lived in an awful place. He wanted the best home for his daughter, or a _better_ home in any case.

He looked far and wide, but was confused by what's around. Mountains and buildings were _tilted_, as if falling down!

He entered a home where no chair upright remains. The ceiling had a door, and the floor a window pane. Walking on that floor felt like walking up a hill. He took a step, slipped, and slid all the way back to the window sill.

This wouldn't work, he thought, a home laying on its side. A home should be flat and going _up_---this is not right!

And so he kept searching, but it only got worse. The next home was slanted even more, as if it were sick and needed a nurse. 

After walking all morning, he finally found a home that was flat. A flat floor, windows in the side walls, and a ceiling above his head.

Except the ceiling looked strange, decorated with carpets, house plants and a boot. And why would you glue a chair upside-down to the wood?

Then he realized this home wasn't right at all. It was _completely_ upside-down: going up the stairs meant you'd actually fall.

This was wrong and dangerous, that he knew. If his daughter grew up here, she might grow up upside-down too!

And so he walked and walked, but every next home was just rotated more. As if a single home were rolling, like a ball to the shore. 

But Comm was clever, dear listener, and he realized a thing. If a ball kept rolling long enough, it would become right side up again!

He ran along the line of homes, comparing each one to the one before. Until he was certain: he'd found a home again with a proper ceiling and floor.

This home was actually shaped like a ball, as opposed to the cube homes you might know from your world. It had a circular door, and circular windows, and was named _The Home of Whirled_.

"This is the place!" Comm said with a smile. Enough space, not falling over; his daughter would love to stay here a while.

Just to be sure, though, he stayed for several days. To see if any unexpected problems came, like loud neighbors or tsunami waves.

Days and days he lived there, in the ball-shaped home. But nothing strange happened, and so he was satisfied and went back home.

He opened the front door---but he couldn't believe his eyes. Mountains were upside-down; ocean waves filled the skies. Birds flew below him and rabbits hopped overhead. Oh no, he realized, he was standing on his peary head.

The problem with circle homes, dear listener, is that they can roll. While Comm had stayed inside for the night, the home had rotated and fallen to its side!

"Ugh!" he said, enraged. "I found my perfect home, but then it changed!"

You know what my daughter will always need? Comm thought to himself. The same that _every_ living thing needs: tasty food on the shelf ...
