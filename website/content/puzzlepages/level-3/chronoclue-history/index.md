**Put the timeline back in the right order. Check your answer by connecting the dots in that order and seeing what illustration comes out. Learn history, truly learn it, by solving fun puzzles.**

Each puzzle focuses on a different thread of history. Medicine. Democracy. Rise and fall of the Roman Empire. The Space Race.

It gives you an empty _timeline_ and a handful of _events_ from that thread. Just a small snippet, one or two sentences. **But read the snippets well, think logically, and you know what comes before an what comes after.**

You can't end a war if it hasn't started yet, right? And how could Cleopatra die if she had not been born yet? The asteroid can't wipe out the dinosaurs if they haven't arrived yet!

It's up to you to fix the timeline. If you put events in the right order, you can connect the dots (in that order), and get out an illustration confirming you did it right.

That's why it's a _puzzle_. Not a boring textbook, not a test, a _fun game_. You don't have to know exact dates and names beforehand. You _learn_ them as you solve the puzzles, because all the information you need for the correct timeline is right there.

Additionally, that's why it's _educational_. The puzzles are not just about random events or numbers. They are about "chains of cause and effect". The actual important part of history that should be taught to children. The exact dates or events don't matter; it's about what caused them and what consequences they had.

We know it can be hard to entice children to learn about history. This puzzle makes it much easier and more effective, and we went all-out on a colorful and exciting design to help out even more.

Have fun!
