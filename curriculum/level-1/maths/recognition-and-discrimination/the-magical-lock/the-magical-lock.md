# The Magical Lock

## Introduction

_On an island far away, there lived a hedgehog called Envi. On his travels he found many treasures. Now, as he is getting old, he wants to share his wealth with someone else. But of course, not everyone is worthy. You have to prove your cleverness first!_

_Envi prepared five treasures, but only one is real. You get a single chance to pick one. If you try to open the wrong lock, you instantly disappear to another planet and forget the island existed! Can you solve the puzzle and escape with the treasure?_

## Setup (for the Guardian)

An escape room like this works best if there is a tangible reward, such as actual weighty coins or maybe candy. Before the players enter the room, 

* Cut out the treasures and fold them. Put them in a line facing the players. (There is a hint at the _back_ of certain treasures.)
* Place the reward underneath the treasure with the Tiger.
* Cut out all the other (puzzle) tiles. Fold them to make "pawns" that stand upright, then randomly spread them across the available space.

Give the players the introduction and their goal. Tell them they're allowed to move around and use the tiles---be creative!---but they can't touch/move the treasures.

Please note that escape rooms are "advanced" puzzle experiences. It's supposed to be a bit challenging, requiring several attempts and creative ideas to suddenly realize what you must do. It's fine to help players if they're truly stuck, but don't be too eager to do so!

## Solution

This simple escape room has only five steps to finding the right treasure.

1. Players recognize that only certain tiles appear _multiple times_: the Tiger, Giraffe, and Elephant. (All other tiles are just noise, appearing once and to be ignored.)
2. By matching the puzzle piece/part sticking out, you can create a connected line for each animal.
3. When you look at the _back_ of that line, you see a key. Only the Tiger and Giraffe keys _match_ those displayed on the Tiger and Giraffe treasures. 
4. Now, when you look at the _back_ of the line of treasures, you see a Giraffe with red crosses and sad faces.
5. Conclusion: only the Tiger remains. That is the right treasure.
