# The Weather of Wheresthesun

Comm the pear looked everywhere. He was a pear who lived, like many other fruits in his time. His neighbor was a dancing date and his grandma was a laughing lime. Some were lucky, like bananas, and had thick skins. But pears only had a thin layer that could barely keep their warmth within.

He hated the weather at his current home. So unpredictable, so different every day. He wanted to find a place that was always sunny and warm for his daughter, and he wanted to find it today.

First he looked in the rainforest, but you can probably guess the result, my friends. It was warm, yes, but also had a rainstorm that never ends.

And so he traveled to nature's other neighborhoods. And after some brisk walking he reached the woods. It was colder here, but at least it was dry. Unfortunately, though, he could not see the sky. The trees so big, and so many, that they blocked out the sun. He walked in shadow and shade, and the day had only just begun!

He kept running and running, until he slipped and fell. He had reached a place covered in white, where snowflakes always fell. It was beautiful to see, and young fruits played with glee. But he shivered in the cold and moved again before he could freeze.

He reached a nice red mountain, but it turned out to be a volcano.

"You're just in time," said a talking tangerine. "The volcano is going to explode, a miracle to be seen!"

And indeed, not long after, there was a rumble and a crack. Fire and gray smoke created a river that pushed them back. Within minutes, the blue sky was gray and dark. It was _too_ hot now, so hot that every movement created a spark. 

This was far too dangerous, thought Comm, especially for a baby. But there's better weather on the water, maybe.

He kept running, and swimming, to reach an island far away. It was surrounded by water, of course, only beaches and bay. It had coconut trees, and the sun always shone. A paradise, he thought, and a very suitable home.

But as he stood in the sand, his feet suddenly felt wet. He turned just in time to see a giant wave come and attack! The waves crashed into the island and washed Comm away. We call it a tsunami, dear listener, and it's as dangerous as they say.

Comm found places where the sky was always gray, and places with a daily earthquake. He found places where grapes had sunburns, it was so hot. And places where thunder came down every evening, whether you wanted or not.

What was the best weather? Why couldn't he choose? He loved summer and sun, but his wife loved winter and cold. Some weather is better in one way, but worse in another, and maybe---

A running raisin came at him and yelled: "Hold!"

Comm received a message, written by his wife. Oh no, had his daughter already been born? Maybe this message told him what his baby wanted from life ...
