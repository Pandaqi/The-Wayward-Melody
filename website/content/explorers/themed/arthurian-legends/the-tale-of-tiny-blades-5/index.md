_On the evening that King Arthur becomes an adult, all the mistakes of his youth come to get him. Some of his knights are not acting so honorable, and neither is he. The people, and his own Queen, believe in him less and less. And so he decides to leave Camelot for a dangerous, uncertain, mysterious mission. To find the Holy Grail and prove his honor and innocence---while the knights he leaves at home decide to take advantage of his absence._

**Quest for the Holy Grail** is part 5, and the final part, of the book series **The Tale of Tiny Blades**. Like all the other books, it was written in such a way that you can read it on its own, without knowing anything about the series. We do _recommend_, of course, reading those earlier instalments. They're fun books that give a lot of extra detail and life to all the characters and places!

* It contains **@TODO:XX chapters** of about **2,000 words each**.
* The book contains several quests and magical artefacts with interesting consequences. But the majority is taken up by the **Quest for the Holy Grail**, which means chapters are not really standalone, but a series of steps in the quest.
* The book is written in our trademark style. **Simple language, big themes and events, lots of humor**. This simple and playful approach allows it to show core themes of the Arthurian Legends and be somewhat historically accurate. 

We are very proud of this set of stories. We hope they bring educational value (in teaching about the historic events that led to the Arthurian Legends) as well as exciting and emotional stories (about what it means to be honorable and a good man/king).

After finishing this book, you have seen King Arthur's story---his legend---from start to finish. We hope you enjoyed it. We hope it added a bit more honor into this world.
