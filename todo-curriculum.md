TARSIAS!
* Basically, you create a set of triangles where some of them have questions and others have answers.
* There is only one way to connect the triangles (into a bigger triangle, or hexagon, or whatever) where all the Q+A PAIRS are correct!
* @SOURCE: https://www.tarsiamaker.co.uk/
* @SOURCE: https://www.cre8tiveresources.com/why-teachers-should-use-tarsia-puzzles-in-the-classroom-and-online/

GOOD LEARNING STRATEGY: Numbered Heads Together
* @SOURCE: https://www.teachervision.com/group-work/numbered-heads-together-cooperative-learning-strategy
* The idea is to group kids/apprentices, but also assign each a unique number.
* When given a task, you discuss and collaborate and everything.
* But when the teacher asks a question/asks that you give the answer, they also say a random _number_. And only that person must answer.
* This requires everyone actually listens and contributes, so they know what they say (or what their "group answer" is) when asked.
