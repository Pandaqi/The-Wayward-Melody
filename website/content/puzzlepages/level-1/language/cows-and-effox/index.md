This is a bundle of **story puzzles** or pre-readers (or young kids). 

The idea is simple.

* Every puzzle is a story told by **5--7 different illustrations**, which you must cut out.
* The story has a clear **"cause and effect"** that puzzlers need to figure out! Something happened, which meant this other thing happened, which caused this ending to the story. Example: you left ice cream out in the sun, now it melted.
* You've solved the puzzle if you can place the illustrations on the timeline in the **correct order**.

It's essentially a full version of our "Timeline Task" formula found in earlier (teaching) materials. Some other improvements we've made are ...

* To allow easy **answer checks**, placing the images in the right order also spells a simple and related word.
* The puzzles are **color-coded**, so you always know which images/story belonged to which task.
* We've added the **actual written story** (per puzzle) too, which you can read to the players as confirmation, or which slightly older readers can try to read themselves for practice.

They're a great textless way to get used to stories and have fun thinking about common situations and their consequences. For more practice with stories, and considering consequences of actions, it's of course best to just read as many stories as possible and experience many different things in the real world.

And yes, Cows & Effox is just a bad pun on _Cause & Effect_. We're not sorry.
