# Donut Return

King Triangle's castle was in chaos. Tessella and Astra had no trouble getting back in. But once inside, they didn't like what they saw.

Piqafox was being chased around. Her and the Bearchitects were jumping through hallways, running over the ceiling, and clinging to paintings. The guards were right on their tail, and they couldn't shake them off.

So that's what happened. They had never reached a Shaeprint.

Tessella looked for a tool in her Toolbox. But she had nothing that could scare away so many guards.

Astra looked at her paint brush. Could she do it again? She _still_ didn't know what magical shapes she drew before, because she had closed her eyes in fear!

Then, all at once, all the burning torches were extinguished. The castle went completely dark. Everyone bumped into each other, not sure if it was a friend or an enemy.

What happened?

Something whizzed by. A gust of wind over Astra's head. The same wind that probably blew out the torches.

At the end of the room, a guard said "ouch!".

Something else whizzed by. And something else again.

The guards lit up a few torches again. And by its light they could see what had happened.

Arrow Hood and his Merry Band, that's what happened. Of course! They were still hiding in the castle!

They launched themselves across the room, taking out guards with their pointy ends.

"You'll never win! There's too many!" yelled Piqafox.

"But we can give you time," said Arrow Hood. "Move! Move!"

Astra and Tessella joined Piqafox' rebels. They ran through the nearest door.

"Where did they go!?" yelled a guard.

"I think it was---"

They fell still. They looked at the signs on the wall. Arrow signs. Three pointed left. One pointed right, but added a dangerous Cross symbol, as if to say "don't go there!"

"Huh. I guess they went left. Everyone follow!"

Once they were gone, Arrow Hood unglued himself from the wall. They continued confusing the guards for the rest of the day, dear reader, sending them going in circles around the castle. When someone asked where the bathroom was, they pointed him to the kitchens. When someone asked where the Rebels had gone, they pointed her to the bathroom.

What's more interesting to follow, dear reader, is what Piqafox did. We follow her through the castle, down a few stairs, and into the garden. We follow them as they walk around the hill on which the castle was built.

And they find a Shaeprint. A promising one. Complex shapes. A powerful tingly sensation---surely this was _very_ magical.

"What do you think it does?" asked Piqafox.

"Never seen this one before," said Lunib. Ancib looked through all his old blueprints, but they also lacked these shapes.

"I'm sorry," said Tessella. "I still can't really _read_ them. But I can _make_ it, yes."

Two of the shapes were easy. They were circles, but with a hole in their middle. Otherwise known as the Donut shape.

Tessella found a large Circle stone in her toolbox, and a small one. By pressing the smaller one into the bigger one, she could create a _hole_. She wondered if the magicians who made these Shaeprints used the same technique. Soon, she had two Donuts. They perfectly filled the Shaeprint holes.

The other two were more difficult. One was like two Donuts on top of each other, but with a Rectangle to the side. The other looked like a river, zigzagging up and down, but made of Rectangles. 

It wasn't easy, but she managed to find the right shapes and _combine_ them. After some more effort---cutting off parts that stuck out---she had the perfect shapes.

"Do we really want to do this?" she asked. "This could do _anything_! We don't know what magic it has. Maybe we _lose_."

Footsteps sounded nearby. Arrow Hood could distract a lot of guards, but there were always _more_. King Triangle was close tot, his voice screaming like he was being attacked by dinosaurs. They had probably figured it out and were searching the hill now.

"There's your answer," said Piqafox. "We have no time. No other choice."

Tessella placed the first shape. It filled the hole as it should, and she even heard a _click_.

Tessella placed the second shape. 

Everyone stepped back now. Some covered their eyes, or the eyes of their children. Some hid inside their clothes, or held a sword ready to fight whatever magic came out.

Piqafox stepped back too. It made her look at what Tessella had done in a new way. At the basic shapes she had combined into new ones, which filled the Shaeprints. 

And suddenly she understood. She remembered this language from the Rainbow World, where she came from. These were ... letters. The Donut was just an _"O"_. If you put two Donuts on top of each other, you get a _"B"_. If you zigzag a few Rectangles, you get an "_M_". 

Yes, it was so obvious now. The more complicated Shaeprints were just _letters_, making a word from left to right.

And this Shaeprint spelled: _BOOM!_

The hill exploded. Not the castle---not like before. This was different.

No, the castle was shot into the sky, as if it were a tennis ball smashed away. 

All the mushrooms around the castle were launched like rockets too.

With the castle gone, they could see the entire city around them. No more large shadow hanging over them. No gray walls.

Lunib and Astra were stuck to a mushroom as it launched. They didn't dare let go. They _couldn't_ let go, it seemed. The mushroom seemed magical and had a Shaeprint too. And so they shot into the sky, past the clouds, past the sun, and never came back down. Lunib read the moon as they flew past it too. All he had to say was that they were going to be fine. Shapesong would never see them again. But don't worry, dear reader, because as Lunib said, they landed safe and sound on some other planet called Somnia.

And they saw King Triangle, clinging to a mushroom as it flew away. His mushroom had a Shaeprint too. The King tried to fill the shapes in a panic. He tried to activate the mushroom magic, hoping it would save him. He seemed prepared for this, and he even _succeeded_: he grinned as the Shaeprint turned on and the mushroom slowed down. He was covered in a weird pink smoke, then started falling back down.

His guards quickly made a living tower. That is, by standing on each other's heads, they made a ladder and grabbed King Triangle's foot before he could fall any further.

Unfair! Tessella couldn't believe it. They would still lose! The King was saved!

All around them, stones, trees, and even sand was shot into the sky. Everything that was on top of the hill, was now living among the clouds.

Except King Triangle. He came back down safely.

Or ... wait a minute ...

The guards looked confused. They let go of the "king" immediately.

"Well? What are you doing? Capture the rebels! Put them in the dungeons!"

They all looked at the empty hill. "What dungeons?"

"Or anywhere else in the castle!"

"What castle?"

The King turned around, as if he wanted to set the hill on fire with his eyes. 

"I have a crown---" He realized the crown was gone. "I pay you in gold, guards---" He realized the gold was stolen too.

He stomped the ground. "I don't need to prove myself. I am your _King Triangle_, you can see it!"

"Can we?" asked Piqafox, grinning. "Then why ... do you look like a Donut?"

The King's eyes went wide. His mouth fell open as he ran to the nearest glass window, to see his reflection.

The mushroom magic. It had turned him into a Donut instead.

King Triangle had made all of Shapesong in his image, and then his image had changed.

Piqafox laughed. Loudly. Belly-shaking laughter. The rebels all joined her, pointing and laughing.

She couldn't have made a better Rebel plan if she tried.

"A _Donut_! Of all shapes! I knew a Donut once, and it let me down! Twice!"

King Triangle felt a literal hole in his heart. He sunk to his knees in shame, shaking his head as if it was all just a dream.

Penny the cat gracefully jumped down a tree and landed before him.

"Go away, evil king!" yelled Penny the cat. "_And donut return!_"

"Aaah ... I'm a donut ... aaaaaaah ... whyyyyyyy," yelled King Triangle. As he ran away in shame, over the hill, never to return. 

Nobody knows where he ended up. Some say he is stuck playing the Game of Symirror forever with that magician. Most never saw him again, and surely not sitting on a throne.

They had defeated King Triangle. No more Triangle homes. No more feeling bad about _not_ looking like a Triangle. No more Triangle soccer fields, or Triangle wheels that don't work, or Triangle apples for breakfast. Soccer fields were allowed to be Rectangular again. But also a Pentagon. Or a Circle. Let me tell you, many interesting new sports were invented in the weeks that followed.

Beau rolled through Shapesong, even faster than before. He visited every town, every city, every forest, to give the good news. All except Flowerfar, of course. He didn't know that peaceful village even existed.

Arrow Hood and his Merry Band were stuck inside the castle when it launched. But, and this is a great benefit of being an arrow, they could just launch themselves _back down_. Half an hour after the BOOM!, they landed on the hill like nothing had happened. They immediately started pointing at each other and saying it was _their_ fault, but Arrow Hood stopped them.

He realized they were stuck in a cycle. Get mad and accuse each other, then apologize and hug again. Sure, apologizing was nice. But it was even better if you just didn't do something bad in the first place. They decided to think a bit longer before they angrily pointed at each other again. Nobody knows if they kept their promise, as nobody could find them with their camouflage clothing. Arrow Hood could be in the room with you right now, but you'd never know. But Piqafox liked to believe they behaved now. And if an evil King ever returned, she would call them for help.

They rebuilt the castle, again. But this time they used all the shapes imagineable. The Bearchitects were invited to design the prettiest castle ever. Every tower was a different shape. Every door was a different shape. Pentagons and Heptagons had their own personal entrance that only _they_ fit through. There wasn't a single throne, but one throne for _every_ shape. This meant the Throne Room became very large and very full of furniture. They didn't mind---they were used to Ballhouse Beaucoup anyway.

And then, everyone agreed Prince Circlesnake deserved his father's title. He became King Circlesnake. And he had seen his father's cruelty, he had seen the Rebels and their good work, and he was never, ever, ever, going to demand that everyone become a Circle like him.

This was the Balled of Shapesong, dear reader. I'd like to say that they lived happily ever after. But I don't really know, because I haven't visited that planet in a while. Piqafox, Tessella, all of them left the planet too shortly after.

But I like to believe it. I like to believe that a world can be full of different shapes, all with their own flaws and strengths, and be happy and peaceful. I like to believe that anyone who only likes what _looks like them_ will be kicked off their throne before long. I like to believe there will always be enough shapes who want _freedom_ over anything else.

I like to believe that one day, this becomes just a fun, made-up story that _The Book Worm_ tells. And it finally helps them escape from their curse.

And then everyone lived happily ever after.
