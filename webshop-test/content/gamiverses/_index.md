{{% section-foldable header="What is the Fun Frontier?" %}}

I have always loved _learning_, but hated _education_. I've researched how people actually _learn_ all my life, literally since I was a boy, and came away with a few certain conclusions. One of them is that **playing games = learning**. 

With the Fun Frontier, I designed an entire curriculum of board games. Download them, print them, play them with your children or pupils. Watch them have fun while _actually_ learning.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="What do I get?" %}}

As stated, the project's website contains _all the base games_ for **free**. Education should be free for all. This webshop merely sells _extras_, such as expansions, to help keep the project afloat.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="How am I allowed to use these games?" %}}

**However you like!** Share them with an entire class. Prepare one as a surprise gift. Customize it for your kid.

You don't need a special license or permission for anything. You bought the experience---it's yours to experience it as you wish.
