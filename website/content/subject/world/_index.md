This subject teaches about all the wonderful parts of our **World**. This essentially comes down to any topic or subject that is _not_ Language or Science/Mathematics.

More specifically, this subject contains major fields like,

* History
* Money & Economics
* Society & Law
* Geography (& Traffic)

{{% section-foldable header="How To Teach It" %}}

Below are some general notes about teaching this subject as a whole.

Your Apprentices _live_ in this world. They experience the effects of systems every day. They hear about news and big events. They stumble upon parts of the world every day, wondering _why_ things work like that, or _what_ they even are.

As such, most of this subject should be taught in an "opportunistic" way. Ask your students about some news they've heard, or what interests them today, then talk about it. Make it about the actual world in the present, and you can link that to more abstract facts or history if you want later.

Not all of it, of course. 

Navigating through traffic / using a map is something you _do_. It's a skill and thus best learned with a game, puzzle, or other activity.

Understanding money and using it responsibly is something you _do_. It's a skill and thus best learned with a game, puzzle, or other activity.

The complexities of historic events or societies are way too difficult (and likely boring) to explain in a dry way or force students to read about. You need a _story_ that packages it in an exciting way. You need a fun _quiz_ to test all the common knowledge that students picked up along the way.

Our resources aim to offer these tools and help you teach about the world in this way.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="What Is (Not) Included?" %}}

This subject is about the **"external" world**. Anything about your **inner world**, such as personality and social situations, is part of the [Social](/subject/social/) subject.

This subject is also the one without a clear order or plan. It's the one where the number of things to discuss is near endless. It's mostly governed by student interest and current events. Over time, we will create more and more resources in a wide variety of topics, and we hope to be able to offer something for everyone.
