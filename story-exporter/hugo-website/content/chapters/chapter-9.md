# Guiding Star

Tessella had found another set of Shaeprints. These were cut out of a mushroom. She felt they did something important, but didn't know what. Should she be careful? Should she leave it alone?

Mwah. All the previous times she'd tried, the Shaeprints had done something great! It would probably be fine.

She was Star-shaped again today, like most days. Three of her pointy ends disappeared in her Toolbox to search for the right shapes.

When, suddenly, something bumped into her behind.

"My apologies!" said a deep male voice.

Tessella quickly came back up. Her hands already held the right shapes.

On the other side of the mushroom stood a Circle _bear_. She recognized the image on his shirt: this was one of the wonderful _Bearchitects_! Bears who could build anything they wanted, or so they said. Maybe _they_ build the Shaeprints long ago? Or designed this mushroom?

The Bearchitects were also known as very friendly animals. Tessella believed they would _never_ work for King Triangle.

"Oh, don't sweat it," said Tessella. "But you might want to step back."

One by one, she filled the holes in the mushroom.

"Oh. Stepping back! That's a new one!" said the bear, smiling.

"New one? Nobody has ever told you to step away?"

"No," said the bear, looking annoyed. "They always think I'm a cute teddy bear. They always try to _hug_ me! Ugh! Sometimes I can't move for hours---"

"Excuse me," said Tessella. "But what is a _hug_?"

The bear's eyes opened wide. "Y-You ... you have never been hugged?"

"Sounds like _mugged_. Is it like stealing?"

The bear considered Tessella. "Well, I guess ... being a pointy Star and all ..."

"Aha! That's the last one!" said Tessella, jumping happily. The final Shaeprint had been filled. "Prepare for some Shaeprint magic---"

A _plop_. A _poof_. A _crack_, as if the mushroom was filled with rocks. 

The mushroom bent over one way, then the other way. As if it was trying to choose if it wanted to be plucked by Tessella or the bear. 

Then it made its decision. It simply flew into the air and disappeared, like a rocket launching itself towards the stars. It even left a thick fog that made them both cough.

When the fog finally cleared, Tessella didn't see any difference. But she _felt_ different.

She felt her head, her ears, her back, her legs ...

Oh no.

She had become a _bear_.

Wait. Did that mean ... that the bear had become---where was the bear?

A Triangle-shaped carriage raced past her. It hobbled and bobbled through the forest, squeaking and creaking as it went. King Triangle himself leaned out the window and snatched the bear from the forest.

Or, well, he didn't look like a bear anymore. He looked like _her_, Tessella.

"Got you now! You filthy shapeshifter!" yelled the King. "I've chased you for months, and now you're in my grasp!"

"You're wrong!" said the bear. His voice shook with every bump the carriage drove over. "I'm actually a Bearchi---"

"You _really_ think that's going to work? I have eyes, my girl. Eyes! I can clearly see who you are! To the dungeons you go!"

The carriage was almost out of the forest. 

Tessella reacted just in time to grab something heavy from her Toolbox and _throw_ it. It bumped against the back wheels of the carriage, breaking them. Of course, it's much easier to break wheels when they're shaped like Triangles. They never rolled very well in the first place.

King Triangle immediately left the carriage. Five guards followed him.

Tessella tried to run, but stopped after just two steps. She wasn't used to this new body and tripped over her own feet. And, also, she wasn't going to leave the poor bear behind.

But that meant she had to trick King Triangle somehow.

___

Tessella sat down on the stump. The one that had a mushroom a few minutes ago. She wondered where it was now, perhaps flying among the stars. Maybe some confused aliens would think mushroom people were invading them.

The King looked at her funny.

"Are you a Triangle or not? I can't seem to figure you out, Bearchitect," he said. He circled her like a shark. 

Yes, King Triangle liked sharks. Or, well, the Triangle fin part of them. The only thing he could see above water. His guards had convinced him that the fin was the entire shark body. Just to prevent the King from walking into the ocean to arrest some poor shark minding its own business.

In much the same way, they had convinced him that Starfish didn't live in the ocean. No, no, it was just a rock. With colorful Triangle arms. That sometimes moved. No need to take a closer look, sire.

"It's the fur," said Tessella. She tried to put on a deep voice, like the bear. "It hides my, erm, true shape."

"Take it away then," demanded King Triangle.

"I can't."

King Triangle came even closer. His spiky Triangle nose almost bumped into Tessella's nose.

"Are you ignoring a command by your King?" he whispered. "I could throw you into the dungeons for that."

Tessella tried not to look at her Toolbox. She had quickly kicked it behind some other mushrooms. But it stuck out like a, well, like a very large blue Rectangle in a green forest. If only she could reach it ... it had to have something to defeat the King ...

"I wouldn't dare!" said Tessella. "Oh great king, I am afraid me and my ... friend had an accident."

"Friend? Accident?"

"Yes. The one locked in your carriage right now," said Tessella. She pointed with one of her Starry points for good measure. Then she realized it would give her away and stopped. "We activated a Shaeprint, but it was, erm, a _curse_! Yes. A curse. It gave us this weird fur and made us look all different."

The King frowned. Then he sat down next to her. "Tell me about it. One of them blew up my castle! Blasted things!"

Tessella sat perfectly still. She didn't dare move a single toe or finger, not with King Triangle literally breathing down her neck. "I promise you, great sire, that me and my friend are actually Triangles! Yes, the most triangular of Triangles! Please let him go. Also, _don't_ give him a hug."

"Why would I---ugh, this is really uncomfortable. Mind if we ..."

The King moved over to the next mushroom. One that was still alive, and had a nice large, squishy top. It also had the same Shaeprints as the other.

Maybe she could ...

"That reminds me!" said the King angrily. "My mother was a Star shape. An impossible shape, I tell you, Bearchitect."

It took a while for Tessella to realize he was still talking to _her_. Just because she _looked_ like a Bearchitect now. But she was still, you know, Tessella! Just a few different shapes on the outside, and the King could be fooled. It made her wonder how many animals around Shapesong were wearing Triangle costumes right now to fool him.

"My mother could never hug me. Whenever we tried, one of her pointy Star ends would prick me! Sometimes even _two_!"

"Ah. Yes. _Hugging_, which is when ... you ..." said Tessella, waiting for him to finish.

"Hold your arms around each other," said the King dreamily, "sharing warmth and a soft touch. I would've liked to be hugged as a child."

She didn't know what to say to that. But at least hugging made sense to her now.

"Anyway---it was impossible to hug her!" continued the King. "She couldn't carry me as a baby. She couldn't open a door without breaking off some splinters. Horrible! Very uncomfortable! I _hate_ Stars. Hate, hate, hate them."

Tessella held her breath. She tried really hard to think of something else. To pretend she was bored, to become a Square. Or that she was as angry as him, to become a Triangle. Anything to _not_ look like a Star beneath her bear appaerance.

In doing so, she noticed something.

She couldn't shapeshift anymore.

The magic was too strong. The mushroom had made her and the bear _swap places_. She felt the overwhelming desire to _hug_ someone---even the King! The bear, still locked in the carriage, probably wanted to grab tools and---

"Erm," said the bear, looking like Tessella. He popped his head through the window. Tessella thought it was _really weird_ to see herself. "Do you need help repairing those wheels? I'm quite handy with tools. I think. And I should have a magical Toolbox somewhere around her---"

"Oh don't mind him!" yelled Tessella. She stood up so quickly that the mushroom bounced back, like an elastic. King Triangle almost fell off. "He has an _imaginary_ Toolbox. Yes. He always dreams about Toolboxes, for some reason. But they don't exist! No need to look for them!"

King Triangle frowned once more.

"You are the _strangest_ Bearchitect I ever met. Nice, but strange." 

He took a final good look at her. He _huffed_, and _puffed_, and _hmpfed_. If Tessella didn't know any better, she'd think the King had swallowed a frog.

"But I can't just take your word for it," said the King. "I've searched for this Tessella figure _everywhere_! I won't let her slip away now. Everyone knows that King Triangle is clever and doesn't fall for tricks. Nobody can fool me! So your friend is coming with me!"

The back wheels had been repaired. Replaced with stones quickly cut into Triangles. The King drove off. And Tessella couldn't see where he was going.

She was certain now: she needed to hug someone.

To make the spell come undone, of course. And to recover from the insane stress she felt from King Triangle being near her, for so long. Explaining how much he hated her.

___

Night fell. A shooting star appeared in the night sky. A bright light, with a colorful trail. Like a rainbow bird on fire, flying amongst the clouds. 

It was beautiful.

Tessella saw it as a sign.

She followed the star. Out of the forest. Along some roads, where she found evidence of the carriage.

Then she met her first family. The children all pointed at her and yelled: "Teddy bear! Teddy bear! Can we hug him, mom?"

"Sure, sure," said a Pentagon cat.

But when the children came near, they got a glimpse of Tessella's real body. The spiky Star shape beneath. Just in time, they ran away again, yelling: "It's a trap! A trap! Teddy bear very spiky!"

"No! Wait! Come back!"

It was no use. The same thing repeated over and over. As soon as they noticed her Star shape, it was as if they also forgot what a hug was. 

She kept following the shooting star, which turned more and more into a falling star. She was getting desperate. She started asking for hugs herself.

She stumbled upon three weasels. She ran straight at them, fur swinging all around, and yelled: "Hug! Hug me! Please!"

The weasels ran away faster than they thought they could run.

Not long after, she stumbled upon none other than the legendary Piqarine Painter. She spread her arms already, saying: "A hug, please? A huggy for me?"

"Don't touch me!" said Piqafox, who seemed to be having a terrible night. She leaped away before Tessella could stop her.

She looked up again. The falling star was gone! The sky was just a dark black with very tiny, very normal stars sprinkled across.

That meant---she looked ahead and saw the falling star _hit the ground_. A small fire erupted. A brief explosion.

She ran towards it and saw she had been right. The star had landed right beside the carriage. The explosion had ripped off the doors, and the Bearchitect could escape.

She couldn't help but smile. Of course. The falling star ... was the mushroom she had shot into the sky. It had landed back on its feet, as if it had always grown in this patch of grass.

The magic wasn't a curse. It had, again, been very helpful!

The bear stumbled towards her.

Tessella felt small. Cold. She felt like she would collapse if she didn't get a hug now. As if only a hug would keep her standing on her two feet.

The bear knew this. "Ready for your first hug?"

"Not sure---"

He spread his arms around her. It was a bit strange to hug _herself_. But it was warm. And soft. 

She was sad to have missed this all her life. It just added to the sadness about never meeting her parents. The sadness of always being alone.

She never wanted the hug to end. Even as she felt herself change. The fur moved back to the bear. Her voice sounded completely like her own again. She wasn't a Star shape now. No, she had accidentally shapeshifted into a big red Heart shape.

"Okay, lesson two of hugs," said the bear. "At some point you have to let go. Or it gets awkward. Or, in this case, you're attacked by King Triangle."

Tessella suddenly let go. She'd almost forgotten! The King and his guards had left the carriage and finally found them.

She reached into her toolbox. With ease, her hands found a spray can. She didn't even know she had it, of course. 

She pressed the button, and a thick smoke appeared between them and the King.

"You know," said Tessella. She allowed herself a quick extra hug with the Bearchitect. "I've heard good things about some Rebelshapes. Maybe it _is_ time I join their ranks ..."
