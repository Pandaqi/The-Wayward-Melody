This is a **bundle with 3 "audioventures"**. Some of the shortest and simplest ones we made, which are ideal for young pre-reader kids who are just learning about their first stories.

An "audioventure" is a short narrated story, broken into chunks of ~20 seconds each. After each chunk, a question is asked about what just happened or what the expect to see/hear/happen next. It's a fun _interactive_ way to tell stories, as well as a great way to test _comprehension_ of that story.

These audioventures all have 8 parts ( = 7 questions). They are sorted from easiest/shortest to hardest, but the difference in difficulty isn't that great. They test story comprehension in a variety of ways, including imagery and sounds. 

{{% remark %}}
The Audioventure concept and these stories were 100% were written by us. But they were narrated using AI (and serious editing from us), as it was unfortunately impossible to do our own narration at the time. We think the audio quality is more than good enough, but we still want to update the narration with a more emotive human voice once possible.
