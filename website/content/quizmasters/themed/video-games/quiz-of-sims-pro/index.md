This is the **pro** (or "expert") quiz themed around **The Sims** (the entire video game franchise). It's a perfect gift for someone who loves playing The Sims, perhaps on their birthday. It's even better as a shared activity for a group of friends who _all_ love playing the Sims games.

This quiz is divided into 9 rounds, 100 questions total. (So roughly 11 questions per round.)

Yes, that's a lot! This is **not** meant to be played all in one go, as that might take 3--4 hours. We merely wanted to give you the most complete and diverse quiz we could, and _you_ can select which rounds to play in the settings. (In a way, you can do this single quiz 2 or 3 times.)

* General Knowledge
* Tales from the Developers (Sims history, about those who make the games, behind the scenes development issues, etcetera)
* Buildings Bottled (about the building part of the game)
* Sound Effects (about Simlish, its soundtrack, etcetera)
* Inexpensive Items & Crazy Careers (about general elements of the game, mostly items and careers/skills)
* Living the Lore (rumors, theories, backstories, lore established by fans over the years)
* How does THAT work? (crazy interactions and secrets within The Sims games)
* Townies & Frownies (about he playable families, recurring characters, etcetera)
* The Expansion Pack Pile (about the many many expansions and extras)

If you want to play an easier/shorter version with the whole family (including non-gamers), check out the [Quiz of Sims (Family)](/quizmasters/themed/video-games/quiz-of-sims/) instead! It contains easier and more general questions, and more hints (images, phrasing, repeated information, etcetera) to guide those not in the know.
