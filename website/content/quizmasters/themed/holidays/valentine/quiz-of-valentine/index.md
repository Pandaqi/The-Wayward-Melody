This is a **Valentine's Day quiz** for all ages. You can play it with your partner or at a couples night. You can play parts of it as an educational classroom quiz. Or you can simply play it with loved ones to celebrate a day that is, in fact, all about loved ones.

The questions vary in difficulty, topic, and method of answering. They are simple enough that anyone can understand and try to play. They often feature an image, and sometimes audio or video fragments. 

The quiz is delivered in such a way that you can play digitally, physically, or something in-between, with no other requirements than players ready to have fun.

Many of the harder questions have an optional hint. If absolutely everyone is confused, you can decide to show it and make the question a little easier!

It contains **8 rounds with 10 questions each**. The rounds are independent, which allows you to skip entire rounds to save time (or if those topics don't interest you).

* **Happy Holiday** (basic common knowledge about the holiday and its origins)
* **Valentine Forever** (common rituals and traditions)
* **Who is their Valentine?** (a fun round about guessing the other half of famous couples, fictional or not)
* **Romantic Songs** (Valentine's Day and love in music)
* **Love Around The world** (different ways of celebrating the day, or thinking about romance, around the world)
* **Romantic Movies** (Valentine's Day and love in film)
* **Romantic Food** (Valentine's Day and love as it relates to food)
* **Romantic Stories** (Valentine's Day and love in stories)

If used **educationally**, the rounds _Happy Holiday_, _Valentine Forever_, and _Love Around The World_ are your biggest friends. The other rounds are more focused on pop culture related to love and romance, than facts and figures about Valentine's Day itself. It's not really suitable for kids younger than ~7 as the quiz does _not_ have any easy questions like "What do we give each other on Valentine's Day?" or "What is the symbol for love?" ;)
