This is a **mini escape room** themed around **summer and holiday**. 

It's called "mini" because it's very low on material and space required, and the puzzles are textless self-contained slices (or "sun rays"). We did this to allow easily taking the experience with you while traveling, or playing in non-ideal circumstances while outdoors or at a camping. With 12 diverse puzzles, though, you'll still be challenged plenty and it will take some time to solve them all.

You can solve different puzzles "in parallel": you don't all need to look at the same sunray at the same time. Some puzzles depend on knowing the location of _other puzzles_, but that's something to figure out as part of the experience.

When all is said and done, you'll have "completed" the entire sun and a secret summer-related word should come out :)
