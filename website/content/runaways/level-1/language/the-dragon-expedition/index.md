This is an **escape room** about **figuring out what happened** on the Dragon Expedition! 

It's textless and meant for (young) kids, as you'll be placing **illustrations** on a timeline. With every illustration, you need to carefully consider what you're seeing, and what this means for when this event happened. 

{{% example %}}
You have one illustration with a burning campfire. You then find another illustration with just ashes or no fire left. Then you know the second one must come _after_ the first!
{{% /example %}}

The escape room has a beautiful dragon path, to place on the center of the table, which will contain the entire timeline at the end. The number of events to place is large enough to make this a true experience and puzzle, playable with many kids at the same time, but should not take longer than 30--45 minutes.

There _is_ a bit of text within the escape room. Namely, the "diary" of the travelers going on the Dragon Expedition. But this is a solution check, confirming if you're right at the end and adding some extra detail or flavor, and can be read by a single Guardian who _can_ read.

If you want something shorter and more standalone, look at our [Cows & Effect: Pre-Reader Story Puzzles](/puzzlepages/level-1/language/cows-and-effect/). This escape room is essentially a bunch of those tiny puzzles mixed and blended together.
