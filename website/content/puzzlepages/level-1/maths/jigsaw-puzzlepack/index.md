This product contains a large set of **jigsaw puzzles**, focusing on **basic shapes**. 

It starts with a puzzle shaped like a triangle, with triangle pieces. Then a square, with square pieces. And so forth until, at the end, the Apprentice is ready for a large detailed puzzle! It also includes extra high-contrast puzzles and advanced ones with irregular shapes.

Pick one, cut it out, and have fun putting the pieces back together again!
