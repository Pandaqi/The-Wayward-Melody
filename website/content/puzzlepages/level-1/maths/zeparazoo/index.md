**Zeparazoo** is a puzzle about placing fences between animals that really need a fence between them. It's a "precursor" to full-blown area division puzzles, such as [Splitzoo](/puzzlepages/level-1/maths/splitzoo) from the next topic.

It can be played **physically**, by placing the actual fences between animals on the puzzle. In fact, this is the recommended way to play. You can also play by **drawing** fences/lines/rectangles between the animals yourself.

Its rules are simple as always.

* The puzzle shows you which animals should be apart.
* Recognize whenever they are next to each other, and place a fence there!
* (If done right, you should use no more and no fewer fences than indicated.)

On higher difficulties, extra twists are added to keep things interesting and challenging. Maybe animals also need to be separated if they're further apart, instead of only when right next to each other. Maybe some animals love each other and should _not_ accidentally be separated by a fence meant for another. Maybe some animals are strong and need a _stone_ fence instead of a _wooden_ fence.

By the end, you should be an absolute master of comparing and _zeparating_ ;)
