The name (and logo) of this puzzle says it all: _Crossophone_ is a unique mix between the famous _Crosswords_ puzzles and phonics. Instead of writing _letters_ inside the squares, you place _disks_ for every phoneme you hear in the word. 

The first few puzzles have a single "special disk" which shows a specific phoneme. That one must be in the right place, of course, which usually means a square where two words cross over. Over time, puzzles get bigger, but the number of special disks you have (and know) grow too. Until, by the end, you are easily breaking words down into their exact sounds!

As expected, this is for _pre-readers_. All clues are given using clear, simple images of things that a young kid will recognize. That doesn't mean they will know the word _instantly_, all times! Like all puzzles and games, this is also simply a way to expand vocabulary and expand your general knowledge.

Most kids, for example, passively know a word like "wagon". But it will take a drawing, and some hints as to what phonemes to use, to "remember" it. Nevertheless, some guidance from a Guardian as they puzzle is certainly recommended.

We are very proud of this unique puzzle we invented. It's definitely a _puzzle_ that challenges you and makes you grow. But it's also definitely doable for young kids learning about phonemes for the first time, because the grids are small, the images simple, and the fact their words cross over gives many (many) _hints_ as you try to solve it.
