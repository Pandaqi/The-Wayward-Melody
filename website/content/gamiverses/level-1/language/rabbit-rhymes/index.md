This is a **physical ("board") game** for pre-readers about recognizing **rhyme and alliteration**. What does that mean? It means there are no pawns---the players themselves are the pawns. And the entire room is the board. 

The players are "bunnies" hopping from location to location, trying to reach the finish (first). How do you hop? By picking one of your cards and placing it on your current location. If it _rhymes_ with your location, you're allowed to jump over fences, which moves much faster. If not, you simply take a step to an adjacent location.

This product includes our _Rhyme Pairs Cards_ for free. They will be used to do the actual rhyming. Expansions add more custom rhyme pairs (not in that pack).

The base game already has players moving around a lot and practicing their rhyme/word sounds all the time, but it's a very simple and straightforward game. The expansions/upgrades add more creativity, more strategy, and other language play such as alliteration.

Have fun!
