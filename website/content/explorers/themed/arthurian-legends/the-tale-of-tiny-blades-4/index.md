_Arthur, the Promised King, finally sits on Camelot's throne. But all other kings in Britain think he is weak, too young, and now is the time to strike. They attack him from all sides, while Arthur just wants peace and unity. He embarks on dangerous missions to other kingdoms to try and become their friend. Will he succeed, or will that be his end?_

**Quest for a Kingdom** is part 4 of the book series **The Tale of Tiny Blades**. It shows Arthur becoming a young man. Trying to rule the kingdom he inherited, which is a tough job. Especially for a young boy who may be a bit too arrogant and believe he's already bigger and stronger. Such is the way of men.

* It contains **@TODO:XX chapters** of about **2,000 words each**. As opposed to earlier parts, the book contains more perspectives and more variation in storytelling now.
* Each chapter is written as a **self-contained story**. Each chapter has Arthur (or his knights) visit a new kingdom or a new part of old Britain. Trying to understand their culture---trying to make them friends instead of enemies. That said, it is a _book_ (not a story bundle), which means there's an overarching plot and it's best read in order.
* The book is written in our trademark style. **Simple language, big themes and events, lots of humor**. This simple and playful approach allows it to show core themes of the Arthurian Legends and be somewhat historically accurate. 

After reading this book, you might want to continue with part 5 of The Tale of Tiny Blades: [Quest for the Holy Grail](/explorers/arthurian-legends/the-tale-of-tiny-blades-5/). It's the final part in the series. 

We are very proud of this set of stories. We hope they bring educational value (in teaching about the historic events that led to the Arthurian Legends) as well as exciting and emotional stories (about what it means to be honorable and a good man/king).
