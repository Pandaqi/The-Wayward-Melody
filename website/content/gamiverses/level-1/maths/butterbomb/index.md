**Butterbomb** (also known as **Don't Explode!**) is an educational game of tic-tac-toe with an important ... explosive ... twist.

The base game is only a single page and is a **cooperative** (you work together), **simultaneous** (no turns, act at the same time) race to reveal your group of tiles with the same symbol.

It's the first step on the path to learning about _grouping_ related items. With each expansion / upgrade added, the game becomes more challenging, tactical, and exciting. Step after step, you move towards the final expansion, which means you're ready to practice _numbers_ and _counting_ too.

The game also has a **competitive** mode (you play against each other) and a **turn-based** mode (where you take individual turns). The entire game is **textless** and does **not** require printing in color. In fact, the material is only a few pages to print. After 5 minutes of cutting and taping, you're ready to play this game forever!

## Why Tic-Tac-Toe (with a twist)?

Tic-Tac-Toe is a classic for a reason. It's incredibly simple to play and teach and requires no prior knowledge (such as being able to read). As a bonus, it teaches the crucial skills of _grouping_ and _counting_ (up to three). The ability to see that a few tiles in a line have the same symbol. The ability to "group" those tiles into one thing in your mind.

But classic Tic-Tac-Toe is a two-player affair without much depth. We can do better!

We looked for a way to make the game ...

* Playable with more than 2 players.
* Playable cooperatively (instead of against each other) or simultaneously (no waiting on turns, good for impatient young kids).
* More approachable for pre-readers and pre-counters. (Such as by using pie slices to communicate "parts of a whole", and no text at all.)
* More varied and tactical, through a few simple rules and extra cards.
* Actually subtly _teach_ counting to three through its gameplay, instead of relying on all players already being able to do it.

This game is the result of that game design journey. It was such a fruitful journey, in fact, that we have another bundle of tic-tac-toe inspired games ready to launch in the future.

Have fun and keep learning!
