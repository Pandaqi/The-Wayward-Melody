**A letter grid. Hidden themes that connect words into rooms. Only one room contains two people: Killer and Victim.**

The new puzzle sensation that combines letters, logic, creativity, and cozy crime mysteries. What if Murdle and Murdoku had a detective baby with Word Search? What if that baby asked you to exercise your brain in many different ways at once?

**Easy To Learn, Hard To Master**: The rules easily fit on a single page. Find simple English words, group them into "rooms" based on a shared theme, discover the killer. But even the smallest letter grids provide a challenge in both language, logic, and creative pattern recognition.

**5 Difficulty Levels**: Mastered the current challenge? A new rule is introduced that keeps the puzzles fresh and exciting. Puzzles often introduce a one-time twist that fits the location of the crime. We do _not_ take the easy way out and just make the grids bigger each time!

**50 Unique Puzzles**: Every puzzle contains a whimsical little story, one-line suspect statements with clues, and a small Murderoom grid to solve. We wrapped it up in a professional visual design that puts you in the right mood.

**Batteries Included**: You only need a pen(cil) to play. Full solutions are provided at the back. They give both all the individual words to find, as well as the rooms, themes, and killer/victim/weapon.

**Standalone:** All volumes are standalone. You do not need to own any other Murderooms book, or do them in order, to enjoy this one. Furthermore, all puzzles are completely handcrafted. Computers wish they were able to generate these kinds of puzzles, but they can't! Ha!

Go on a journey from _Junior Detective_ to _Private Investigator_. Expand your vocabulary. Test theories. Laugh as you finally understand the logical leap.

Can you cut the letter grid into the correct Murderooms? 

Can you unmask all the killers?
