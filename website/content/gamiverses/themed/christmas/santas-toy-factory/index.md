**Santa's Toy Factory** is a very simple, fun video game for all ages. Play it on Christmas Eve with the whole family, for example, all bunched up behind a single screen or device. 

This is a _cooperative_ "One Button Game". It means that,

* You play with multiple people in the same room (e.g. on the same couch)
* Every player gets only a **single button**, so it's incredibly easy to learn and play.
* But it also means that players really have to **work together**! Because everyone only controls one machine in the factory---one location going up and down---you probably need others too to take over your gift and bring it further.

If you do nothing, your machine moves slowly up and down. Tap your button to change direction. Hold it for longer to _speed up_.

Conveyor belts (or "machine arms") automatically appear between machines. By positioning yourself well, you can angle them to catch gifts and make them roll into the right basket. But if you move your machines _too far apart_ ... the edge breaks and all gifts will simply fall through.

Have fun!
