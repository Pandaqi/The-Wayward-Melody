# My First Shapes & Colors

**Description:**
The first step to recognizing more complex things in life
Is to recognize the basic colors and shapes
This resource explores this in many different ways
Until the student can tell square from triangle without strife

**Extra:**
Shapes and colors are some of the first things that children learn to consciously recognize and play with. This resource teaches the very simple basics first, then combines them for maximum effect, and then goes beyond that by presenting them in weird configurations (such as an oblong triangle).

You might be surprised by how early (and easily) young brains can pick up on this. That's probably because it is a very natural first step to understanding more complex things in the world. Because, after all, what is a tiger, or a chair, or a smartphone, if not an amalgamation of basic shapes and colors?

**Contents:**
* Cut out shapes (of different colors).
* Discrimination in terms of connecting the same ones with lines or something.
* Group/Place the shapes in certain ways.
  * Maybe, if you place all squares in a 2x2, another pattern appears on them.
  * Colors could be placed on a COLOR CIRCLE, which then reveals something too! (Or, more simply, just place "RED COLOR" token on top of all animal drawings or whatever that have RED in them.)
* COMBINED: Color all shapes X color Y.
* NAME THEM 
* ADVANCE by providing the shapes in really weird configurations (like a slanted/oblong triangle), or colors where the shades are just a bit off

**Advanced Spells**:
* (Explorers): Some story about shape and color? "The Piqarine Painter" ("The Squirrel Who Invented A New Color", a bit like The Emperor's Clothes)
* (Quizmasters): Finish the shape, Recognize the shape, Name the shape
* (Puzzlepages): JIGSAW / Can I invent some simple puzzle about placing shapes in a grid?
* (Runaways): Same as _Recognition/Discrimination_, but now we can use variations in shape and color, as well as combinations and naming.
