This is a family quiz themed around **Holidays & Vacation**. It's mostly focused on the biggest of them all: the **summer holiday** and tourism/travels at that time. (Other holidays, such as Christmas or Halloween, typically get their own dedicated quizzes.) 

It's a perfect activity to play on a summer's night while on vacation! 

It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically. The questions are short, simple, and varied. Almost all of them include an image. A handful of them use sound.

It's divided into 7 rounds, 62 questions total. As usual, you can decide which rounds to play in the settings if you want to shorten it.

* Pack Your Bags!
* Travel Trouble
* Holiday Records
* Holiday Snacks
* Tourist Attractions
* Holidays Around The World
* Holiday Hits

This quiz is the "simple" version, playable in a more leisurely way and by young kids too. For a harder quiz that really challenges you, try the [Quiz of Holiday Pro](/quizmasters/themed/holidays/quiz-of-holiday-pro/).
