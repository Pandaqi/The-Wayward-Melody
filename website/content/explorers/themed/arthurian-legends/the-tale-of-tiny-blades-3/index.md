_Arthur has discovered he is the lost prince and returns to Camelot. He must hide that his Knights have been the ones fighting against King Uther and causing trouble all these years. He learns the ways of castles and kings, while trying to change Uther's ways. Can he change the king and prevent a terrible tragedy? Or have father and son grown apart too much, and must he grab the throne for himself?_

**Quest for a Queen** is part 3 of the book series **The Tale of Tiny Blades**. It shows Arthur as a teenager and adolescent. It introduces themes and stories for older children. Including, as the title suggests, one about love and marriage---an essential seed in all Arthurian Legends.

* It contains **@TODO:XX chapters** of about **1,500 words each**. As opposed to earlier parts, the book contains more perspectives and more variation in storytelling now.
* Each chapter is written as a **self-contained story**. It shows a new adventure in which Arthur meets a new knight---or a new enemy of his knights. That said, it is a _book_ (not a story bundle), which means there's an overarching plot and it's best read in order.
* The book is written in our trademark style. **Language is simple and sentences are short.** It contains a lot of whimsy and **humor**. But this simple and playful approach allows it to show core themes of the Arthurian Legends (honor, knights, kingdoms) and be somewhat historically accurate. 

After reading this book, you might want to continue with part 4 of The Tale of Tiny Blades: [Quest for a Kingdom](/explorers/arthurian-legends/the-tale-of-tiny-blades-4/). 

We are very proud of this set of stories. We hope they bring educational value (in teaching about the historic events that led to the Arthurian Legends) as well as exciting and emotional stories (about what it means to be honorable and a good man/king).
