**Chirpy Sorty** (or _"Surt The Bird!"_) is a game about sorting happy birds on different tree branches. This educational puzzle game explores comparing, sorting, sequencing, and grouping, and all of that with one simple rule and fun visuals.

Each turn, the player must **swap two birds**. That is, swipe from any bird in any direction, and it will swap places with the bird on the other side. 

{{% remark %}}
You can change this to a "tap" in the settings. That is, instead of swiping with finger/mouse, you tap bird 1 and then bird 2 in sequence. As always, the settings contain many other ways to customize the experience to the Apprentice too.
{{% /remark %}}

Each branch has a certain "rule". For example, at the easiest difficulty, branches show a color that means **all birds on that branch must be that color**!

Can you put the right birds on the right branches? Can you find the fastest solution?

Once an Apprentice is comfortable with the easiest difficulty, it's time to up the challenge. Later difficulties add ...

* Branches that want things to **not** be the same.
* Branches that ask you to **sort** (e.g. smallest to largest)
* Matching more subtle elements besides color/shape, such as **number of legs** (which also introduces counting).
* And more!

As this is an educational puzzle game, the easiest difficulties are meant to _teach_ and _practice_. The puzzles will be rather straightforward, but they help you grasp the ideas of matching, sorting, grouping, and all other skills required. From about medium difficulty onwards, the puzzles combine all you've learned with some new twist to create really challenging puzzles that older kids and adults will enjoy too.

We think this puzzle game hits the sweet spot between simple and accessible, and actually providing really fun visuals and challenging puzzles as you try higher difficulties.

Have fun and keep learning!
