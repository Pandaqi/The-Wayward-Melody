Help the Easter Bunny put the right eggs in the right places, before Easter is over!

As opposed to many other (logic) puzzles, this one is _not_ played by putting pen to paper and writing letters/numbers. Instead, you get a pawn that can move around following certain rules.

* Every puzzle presents a new "map" (of a town, city, region).
* You must hop your bunny around to visit all the places (where you must deliver Easter Eggs).
* The fewer moves used, the better. (Every puzzle has an optimal solution; many have only a single solution in the first place.)

Using a pawn allows young players (without fine motor skills) to already play. The puzzle is purely visual (using egg patterns and colors), which helps as well. Finally, this makes the puzzles reusable, as the paper isn't used or destroyed while playing.
