It contains a **wide variety of puzzles**. Some are about language, some about numbers, some about logic, some visual, and some about a mixture of those things. Some puzzles are adaptations/twists on a familiar formula, such as Sudoku, while others are unique Chrismast puzzles we invented ourselves. As this online store illustrates, we are puzzle enthusiasts and don't want to bore kids with five more Word Searches ;)

The puzzles are sorted from **easiest to hardest**. This makes the bundle suitable for a wide range of ages and skill. Many of the 1-star puzzles can be played by young kids who can barely read or count; many of the 3-star puzzles are just as fun and challenging for adults.
