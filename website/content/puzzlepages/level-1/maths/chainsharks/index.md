**Chainsharks** is a physical puzzle about learning to compare _size_. And, at later levels, _position_ and _rotation_.

It's a **physical puzzle**. You cut out a few fish tokens beforehand. Then, each puzzle tells you how to "set up" ( = where to place a few fish), and you solve it by physically moving the fishes around.

There are only two rules: fishes move forwards, and **big fish eat small fish**! 

And there's a simple objective: you win when only **a single fish remains**.

This turns it into a very simple and tactile puzzle that both young and old will enjoy. Each difficulty level adds a "special power" to one of the fishes, which allows keeping the puzzles small and simple ... but still challenging. 

The first power you learn---and arguably the most important one---is the one that gave the puzzle its name. After eating a fish, the Shark rotates to match that fish. This allows it to _chain_ multiple bites after another, clearing the entire puzzle if you do it right. Hence, Chainsharks ;)

Play the puzzles to find out what all the other fun fishes do!
