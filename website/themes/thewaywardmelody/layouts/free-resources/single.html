External Resources

The team behind The Wayward Melody maintains several large completely free websites that you might like.

  • Pandaqi Games: free board games and video games, for all ages.
  • Pandaqi Tutorials: free high-quality tutorials (text + image + interactive examples) on many topics.
  • Pandaqi Blog: contains the devlogs (or "developer diaries") for loads of projects, giving insight and nuggets of wisdom.
  • The Saga Of Life: free fantasy short stories all set within the same universe, for all ages.
  • Tiamo Pastoor: the author website of Tiamo Pastoor, which is another place where you can buy his books and read the diaries/progress on them.

Additionally, we want to shout out other free resources by people unrelated to us.

  • Khan Academy: just a great resource for learning, mostly focused on maths.
  • Duolingo: the famous app/website for learning languages.
  • SplashLearn: a curriculum of digital games, it's completely free for teachers.
