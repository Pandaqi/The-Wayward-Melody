This puzzle is a bit more experimental than the others in this topic (such as [Rhymatch](/puzzlepages/level-1/language/rhymatch-pre-reader)). It will probably fall flat with some Apprentices, but be an amazing learning experience for others.

The rules are simple: you must draw shapes in squares such that every row is _different_, but they all _end the same way_. (Just like ... rhymes!)

Once you can do that, the puzzles switch from shapes (rectangle/circle/triangle) to letter shapes. By the end of this resource, a pre-reader kid is actually rhyming and writing simple words without them knowing it. 

We saw this as a great opportunity to play with rhymes (and the "structure" behind it) as well as create awareness of letters and practice fine motor skills.

_We will release an advanced version (not-pre-reader) of this in the future. That will likely just be a really good puzzle that isn't experimental at all, because now the players can actually read and write! If we forget to update this page after release, let us know!_
