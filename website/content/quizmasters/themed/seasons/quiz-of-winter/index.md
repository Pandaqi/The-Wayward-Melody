This is a quiz about **Winter** (the season). It contains questions about what typically happens in this season and interesting properties about it.

It's a perfect activity to play during the winter, especially with young kids or in the classroom! The quiz is first and foremost meant to be **educational**, which means the questions are short, simple, and varied. It's likely too easy when playing with the adults or the whole family, but just right for those learning about winter.

It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically. Almost all the questions include an image, audio, or video.

It's divided into 5 ROUNDS, 42 QUESTIONS total.

* General Knowledge
* The Most ___ Time Of The Year
* Winter Sights
* Winter Activities
* Winter Around The World
