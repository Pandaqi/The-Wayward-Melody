# Arrow Hood & The Merry Band

Four hooded figures crawled through the city. They wore "camouflage" clothes. A word which here means they were painted like bricks and dirt, so they were invisible against an actual wall of bricks and dirt. That was their goal, of course. Nobody was allowed to see them on their secret mission.

The hoods had been made by the Rebels. Three weasels had stolen a _lot_ of silk from the King, somehow. They used some of it for these hoods. The rest was used for some other secret purpose.

Arrow Hood was their leader; the other three shapes were his Merry Band. But all of them were Arrow-shaped. Oh, they were plenty rebellious. Did not like the King one bit, and were eager to fight.

That didn't mean, however, they were any good at it.

"Okay," said Arrow Hood. They stood on a rooftop of some bakery, which knew nothing about it. They had successfully come near the castle without anyone seeing. "The King should be asleep by now. We're going to shoot ourselves through his bedroom window, steal his crown, then shoot ourselves back here. Understood?"

"It seems a bit far away," said one.

"What if he's not sleeping?" asked another.

The third one tried to aim their Arrow shape at the window, but failed. They had long suspected she needed glasses. But the only glasses they sold were Triangle now, and they refused to buy those. Arrows were too close to a Triangle as it was!

"Just follow my lead!" said Arrow Hood, irritated.

"Didn't Piqafox tell us to steal the King, instead of his crown?" asked a Merry Shape.

"I'm all for symbolic theft," said another Merry Shape. "But if we want to defeat King Triangle, we should really defeat the King, not his crown, aye?"

The third one aimed again. This time she would merely hit a tree if she launched herself.

"I'm your leader, I decide," said Arrow Hood. "I know better than Piqafox anyway. She has never been an Arrow in her entire life!"

"Maybe we don't _want_ you to be our leader!"

"But I am!"

"But _I_ want to be leader!"

The two arrows were pointing at each other now. As if accusing each other of something. For a few heartbeats, nothing happened.

Then they both turned away. They mumbled apologies, gave each other a hug, and pulled their hoods over their faces.

They grabbed each other's hands. With some help, the Merry Band all aimed at the bedroom window.

And they launched themselves. Like archers; bow and arrow but without the bow. They flew in the direction their pointy end was pointing, because, dear reader, that's how arrows work. If they put their other side first, they'd catch too much wind and slow down. Besides, the goal of arrows was to cut into something, and for that you needed a sharp pointy end first.

The Arrows were successful. For the most part. They flew through the bedroom window, even though it was high in the sky and shaped like a tiny Triangle. King Triangle was in dreamland, snoring.

Unfortunately, they landed in a tangle. One Merry Shape got his pointy end stuck in the door. Another had cut through the King's clothes. Arrow Hood stood upright, his pointy end stuck in the thick carpet on the floor. A Triangle-shaped carpet, of course.

"You bumped into me!" yelled Arrow Hood.

"I did not! You bumped into _me_!"

King Triangle woke up. At first, he didn't notice what was wrong. The Merry Band still wore their camouflage hoods. Their clothes looked just like the wall behind them, making them nearly invisible.

When he finally saw the intruders, he immediately jumped out of his Triangle bed and called for help.

"And now the King is awake!" said another Merry Shape.

"Because _you_ started yelling!" responded Arrow Hood. "It's _your_ fault."

They all pointed at each other again. The King relaxed. These silly Arrows were no danger. They were only a danger to waking up the entire castle with their silly arguments.

As quickly as it started, it ended again. They all hugged, pointed away from each other, and mumbled apologies.

"If you don't mind, sire," said one. "We'll be stealing your crown."

King Triangle frowned. "And if I _do_ mind?"

"We'll still be stealing it," said Arrow Hood. He shot himself across the room at a dangerous speed. He bounced off a mirror, and a wall, before landing on the crown. 

It was heavier than he expected. He struggled to lift it, until his Merry Band shot to his aid. Together, they could barely keep the metal crown on their backs. They prepared to launch away when---

The first guards appeared in the door. King Triangle pressed a button, and the window closed itself.

Arrow Hood looked around. They had nowhere to go! They could only launch themselves at walls or Triangle guards! 

"Aaaaand now we're trapped," said one Merry Shape, throwing off his hood. "Your fault!"

"Your fault!"

They dropped the crown to point at each other again.

King Triangle laughed. "Ah. The rebels must be getting desperate. To send such ... stupid animals my way."

As always, the Arrows mumbled apologies and made up. But by that time they were already in chains.

"We're not stupid! We are proud Arrows! You will never defeat us!" said Arrow Hood.

"Oh, I knew an Arrow once. A great athlete, gave him a gold medal many times," said King Triangle angrily. "But he pointed me in the wrong direction! All the time! Whenever I wanted to give him a medal, he'd just launch himself away! I _hate_ Arrows. Hate, hate, hate them."

He shut his bedroom door. Before Arrow Hood had reached the dungeons, he already heard the king snore.

Of course, they were not planning on ever reaching the dungeons. All of a sudden, they threw on their hoods.

"Woah! Where did they go!?" yelled a guard.

They launched themselves in random directions. Again, and again, and again, bouncing through the castle. Until Arrow Hood didn't even know where his brothers and sisters were hiding.

They were free, but not really. The guards were smart enough to close all doors and windows, and guard the staircases.

What they couldn't do, was escape the castle. 

___

Piqafox looked sad. "We have to assume Arrow Hood failed."

Beau rolled into the room. The Rebelshapes were gathered in the living room of Ballhouse Beaucoup. Not for long, of course. In a few minutes, the room would shut down and they all had to move to the next one.

"The King says they were trying to steal the crown," said Beau the messenger. "Not kidnap him."

Piqafox looked less sad now. She paced around, angrily pushing furniture out of place. "Then they disobeyed my orders! I _knew_ they couldn't be trusted. They were always fighting among each other."

"We don't know that," said Tessella. 

Piles of paper lay on the table. All of them contained sketches of Shaeprints. They had asked animals, from all over Shapesong, to _draw_ any Shaeprint near them. She had been looking at these drawings for weeks now, hoping to figure it out. Hoping to figure out what they meant. So they would know what magic a Shaeprint had _before_ they used it. 

They didn't want to accidentally make the wrong thing explode again.

"Roll back to the capital," said Piqafox to Beau. "See if you can learn any more. If there's a way to get his Merry Band out of the castle. I will admit that Arrow Hood did a lot of good things, too. And Arrows are too powerful to lose."

Arrows were quite rare in Shapesong. Circles, Squares and Triangle were absolutely everywhere. Even Hexagon animals were common. But animals were rarely born as one of the more unique shapes. On top of that, half the Arrows accidentally launched themselves into a volcano or something before they had children. Their ability to fly like a rocket was both a power and a curse.

Beau rolled away at maximum speed.

Piqafox was still surprised by how fast he was. He was back before she knew it. They had only moved to a different room five times in the meantime.

"Look through the telescope!" he said. "Point it at the castle roof!"

Ballhouse Beaucoup did as he asked. The living home pointed extended its telescope to its longest size. Piqafox was the first to reach the ceiling and look through.

Everyone held their breath. What could be happening.

Nothing, it seemed.

"I don't understand what I'm looking---oh, that!"

She shared the telescope with others. At first, everyone saw just a regular roof. Nothing special. Then, one by one, the Merry Band threw off their camouflage hoods.

They were standing on the roof. They weren't alone, though. They shared it with many other shapes, such as Crosses and even Triangles.

They stood in a line. And no matter how long Piqafox waited, the line didn't change. Nobody moved, nobody turned around, they stuck to that order.

Wait a minute ...

"It's like ... a language!" said Piqafox. "The signs are a simple form of communication. An arrow means you have to move in that direction. Here, an arrow left means moving to the _left_!"

Tessella chimed in. "Yes! And a Cross means we should _not_ do something, or that it's dangerous there."

"A Triangle probably means King Triangle, in this case. They're sending us a message! We have to _read_ it from left to right!"

Everyone tried to look through the telescope at once. Everyone froze, confused.

"What are they _doing_!?"

One of the arrows kept flipping between left and right. Now _all_ the arrows were pointing at each other. Then, a few seconds later, they all returned to how they were pointing before.

Piqafox sighed. "They just had another argument. They do this all the time."

"Oh, like a _cycle_?" asked Beau.

"Yes. A cycle of accusing each other of something, and then saying sorry and being friends again."

"Fine," said Tessella. "Then we just need to read their message at a moment when they're _not_ fighting."

They waited for that moment, which took longer than they wanted. As the line settled again, they read the whole thing.

"Square, move left, move left, move right, move up, move up, Cross Triangle Cross, move down, move down, move down, Triangle," mumbled Tessella.

"... what's that supposed to mean?"

"I thought they were telling us how to help them. But it's not. They only say _move up_ twice, but they're standing on the roof! That has to be a hundred _move ups_ to reach it!"

"Isn't the castle built near the city square?" asked Piqafox.

"It is," said Penny the Pentagon cat. She rarely joined these meetings, as she'd just be bored and push stuff off the table. 

"Then I know what they're saying," said Piqafox. "Write it down! Memorize it, everyone!"

"Erm, okay."

"And once we've defeated King Triangle, remind me to give Arrow Hood and the Merry Band a gold medal or something."

"... because?"

"They just gave us a map to the castle. They gave directions for how to get in and where to find King Triangle."

The Rebelshapes broke out in cheers. Penny high-fived everyone. Tessella hugged everyone. She grabbed her toolbox and, somehow, brought out a cake.

"And now we're going to get Arrow Hood out of the castle," said Penny. Everyone fell silent. "Right?"

"We will," said Piqafox, "once King Triangle is defeated."

Penny crossed her arms and wagged her tail. But she didn't speak any more about it. She had made Piqafox the boss of the Rebels, because of her experience fighting some huge threat called Endless Blackness in her homeland. She wasn't going to argue about everything, like Arrow Hood and the Merry Band.

After a short celebration, they got to work. All the money they had, all the silk they had stolen from the King, it would finally be used to end his tyranny now.
