# The Ink That Writes Our Story

The Endless Blackness was hungry. It raced through kingdoms like never before. Like black ink rewriting the landscape. Placing mountains where there were none, destroying home where there were some. But whatever it did, color was lost. Life was lost. Emotion was lost.

It had been a while since Purin had heard from Snowstripe. He feared the worst. They had hoped that Snowstripe would never fall. White was the complete opposite of black. Surely the Endless Blackness couldn't invade that snowy landscape? Surely it would be safe?

Now he knew better. _Nothing_ was safe from Endless Blackness. You had to fight it, all the time.

They had tried in the Dreamforest---they had failed. They had tried in the Kingdom of Ember Apples---they had failed. They had tried in the Lake of Skyfish---they had failed.

Only one kingdom was left standing. It was as Molmo had predicted. Sunstripe was the richest, the largest. And its primary emotion was yellow _joy_. Energy. The feeling you should be dancing, and singing, and being _alive_!

It was the best medicine against the Blackness. And so they made their final stand at Sunstripe. All the people you've met in these stories, my child, had come together. Each of them had their own strengths, their own favorite color. Except Leyla, of course, she was locked inside a prison cell. They had hoped that the Rainbow Soldiers would listen to _them_ now.

If only it were so easy.

They couldn't even agree on what to eat that morning.

When Purin walked in, they were already yelling at each other.

"Today might be the final day," said Skaia. She started crying about it. "So what's the plan?"

"We need to fight it! Rain fire on it!" yelled Ruby.

"No, no, we need to just _forgive_ the Endless Blackness," said Purin.

"Oh, sure," said Piqafox mockingly. "It's aaaaall a big misunderstanding, you think?"

Purin shrugged. "Have you _tried_ being kind to it before?"

"We should just bribe it," said Molmo. Everyone rolled their eyes. "What? Nobody has ever said no to a million gold coins!"

"The Blackness isn't a being. It's ... I don't know _what_ it is," said Nympha. "That's the problem. It's certainly not natural."

Everyone turned to face Nympha. She wore flowers in her hair and always walked around with at least four bunnies in her jacket.

"And I guess you want to _plant some trees_ inside the Blackness?" asked Ruby. "Thinking that will solve it?"

"Of course not, silly," said Nympha with a smile. "We should just leave it alone completely. Let nature do its thing."

"Unbelievable!" yelled Ruby.

"Please," said Zara. "Be nice to each other. We're all friends here."

"No we're not!" yelled the Rainbow Soldiers at the back. "We don't even know you!"

"I don't ... want to be rude," said Justan carefully. He painted the table orange as he spoke. "But all your plans sound very _dangerous_."

"You're right," said Piqafox. "Doesn't mean we shouldn't do it."

"Pff," said Ruby. "You're all cowards there in Orangestripe."

"And everyone from Dragonstripe can't control their anger!" Justan bit back.

"Stay calm everyone. This is all a misunderstand---" Purin tried to say.

"Calm? You're _too_ calm!" said Ruby. "Coldhearts are too slow and useless. We're doomed! _Doomed!_"

Now everyone started yelling to each other. Everyone had at least one mean thing to say about every other kingdom. Those from the Dreamforest were beasts, those from Sunstripe were arrogant, and those from Heartstripe would probably just fall in love with the Endless Blackness.

Skaia jumped on the table and stomped her foot. The room quieted.

"All your fighting makes me _very sad_!" she yelled. "We're not each other's enemies. The Endless Blackness is our enemy! Or do we want to sit here, and be mean to each other, while the world ends?"

"See," said one of the Rainbow Soldiers carefully. "That's why we need a Commander. Someone to decide our plan. And everyone should listen to them."

"We're not bringing Leyla back," said Ruby.

"Why not?" said Purin immediately. "I forgive her."

"Of course you do," said Piqafox, rolling her eyes.

"I could be commander?" said Tomsy. He was sketching some complicated plan, but now stopped and held up his pencil, like raising a finger. "I come up with all our smart plans anyway."

"No---"

The door swung open. Those closest to it were soaked in rain.

The Endless Blackness was so close that it was dark outside. Even though it was almost afternoon. They were right inside its thunderstorms, and couldn't wait much longer.

Someone stepped through the door.

"You're all wrong," said a loud voice.

"_You again!?_" said Tomsy.

It was Fabian. He had come as fast as he could. Interrupting the spy meetings started to become a habit.

"We don't need to do _one thing_," said Fabian. "That's the mistake the Rainbow Soldiers keep making. Forcing everyone to be the same. To follow their rules. To have only one emotion and nothing else. All attacking with the same color, every day."

Fabian shook his head. He wobbled to the table, tired and soaking wet.

"We need to do the opposite. We need to _all the things_. We need to attack it with all colors at once."

"How ... why ..."

"Has anyone ever tried it?"

"Of course not, most people can only paint a few colors really ..."

Fabian looked around. He smiled. Everyone else smiled as they realized it too.

Somehow, they had brought together a bunch of weird characters. From all the kingdoms, all over the world. Every color had one person in this room that could create it easily. Ruby could do Red, Tomsy purple, Molmo yellow, Nympha green, they had _all the colors_!

Fabian could barely believe it. They had accidentally brought together the perfect group. It was a miracle. A rainbow miracle.

"I like it," said Piqafox. She closed her eyes, and half the room received Piqarine fireworks. "I can see it working."

Tomsy quickly sketched a new plan. He drew where the Endless Blackness was on a map. That wasn't necessary---it was _everywhere_ now. And then he drew where everyone should stand. 

Everyone took a quick look. Then they ran out into the storm, to face the biggest monster of all.

___


Piqafox ran for her life. The Blackness was right on her tail. 

She passed Ruby, who was working up a sweat. With every heartbeat, she painted everything around her _red_. Piqafox had never seen such controlled anger before. Boom---a splash of red. Boom---a splash of red.

Piqafox jumped over her. Her tail narrowly escaped being sucked in. She landed next to Justan.

He struggled more. He still mumbled it was too dangerous. Piqafox gave him a thumbs up, and he shot orange lines into the Blackness. 

Fabian stood next to Justan. They had traveled by woodcart, together. Their brown and orange often mixed. Creating a color that looked a lot like Piqafox's fur. Until it was hard to tell if they were actually two different colors, or if brown wasn't just dar orange.

The fox nimbly continued. Molmo had perhaps the hardest task. It was _his_ palace that was being destroyed. His kingdom. And he had to, somehow, feel _joy_? It was the only way to paint yellow.

And so, Molmo had brought bags of gold coins. Whenever he didn't feel joy, he just looked at those. And then threw a few of them at the Blackness. 

"Who knows," yelled Molmo over the chaos. "I still believe the Blackness will go away if we pay it enough!"

"Keep believing!" yelled Piqafox back. She painted a Piqarine illusion of even more coins, then left.

She reached the sunflower patches. They looked gray and dull now, in the shadow of Blackness. Nympha and Rayo still liked being there. They said touching the flowers gave them strength.

Holding hands, they shot green paint at the Blackness. A splash large enough to paint a tree at the right size.

"How are we doing?"

"Don't know, don't know," said Piqafox. 

Which was true. She could only see one or two people at the same time. They had formed a half moon---half a circle---around the Blackness. As she looked over her shoulder, she _thought_ the monster seemed to shrink. But maybe she just _wanted_ to believe that.

She continued down the line, meeting Skaia. Any time the Blackness ate another sunflower, another honeycomb, she would cry. Her tears became the size of blue whales and attacked the Blackness. Not a fun sea to swim around in, Piqafox thought. But Skaia was doing just fine.

Still it felt like something was missing. Like there should've been one more person here. One more color. Maybe that would finally beat it.

To make it worse, the rebels were painting _black_ too. The color of fear. It's hard to stand next to Endless Blackness and _not_ be afraid. Without them knowing it, they would often add some black to the sunflowers themselves.

With a frown, the Piqarine Painter went on. Tomsy crept closer and closer to the Blackness.

"Watch out!" yelled Piqafox. "Don't get too close!"

"I have to!" responded Tomsy. "I need to learn new things. Look at the Blackness from nearby. That's how I get purple!"

She didn't like that. But Tomsy was a smart boy. He'd stay safe, right?

She continued to the final soldier. Clouds of Blackness chased her. Like ghosts trying to arrest her. Piqafox was barely fast enough to stay alive.

Where was the final---oh no, they're gone. 

"Zara? Marco?"

Piqafox heard a bell. A bike zipped past, with Marco peddling like he was about to win a race. Zara bounced on the back, trying not to fall off. They moved around, zigzagging between the others. And both of them shot pink heart bubbles into the air, like confetti.

Rainbow Soldiers tried to help. They tried to learn from the others, but it was too hard. 

They helplessly looked at Piqafox.

"What should we do?"

That's when Piqafox realized. They _were_ missing a color. Cyan. They missed cyan, the color of ice, the lighter blue.

Nobody could create it. Fabian called them spies, but all these people were really _rebels_. The fought for freedom. They fought for joy, for being allowed to be yourself. The freedom to be whatever color you want. That's what rebels stood for.

This meant nobody could create cyan---the color of control, of stubbornness, of cold organization. The color of the law, and the rules are the rules.

It also explained why they had no organization. No control. They were in complete chaos, shooting colors past each other's face. The Blackness ate a Rainbow Soldier, and nobody had time to notice.

Only one little girl could create cyan with ease.

___

Commander Leyla kicked against the door. She was locked inside that same carriage. The one that held twenty people yesterday, and lead to her arrest.

She had seen the Blackness eat everything. She had seen how the rebels couldn't win.

A few hours ago, this would've made her happy. She was right---they were wrong. Rules were rules.

But now ... she just wanted to go home. She wanted to feel safe. She wanted to be out there with them, fighting. The rules didn't matter when the world was about to end. The rules didn't matter if they just made things worse. How could she have been so stupid? How could she have betrayed her father? How could she have stolen people's food, forced them to become a soldier? 

The rules were there to protect people. But _who_ were they protecting? Where were all the _other_ commanders of the Rainbow Army?

That nasty fox suddenly appeared before her.

"Oh go away, Piqarine Painter," muttered Layla. "You've won. Happy now?"

"We haven't won. And I'm certainly not happy," said Piqafox. "We ... we need Commander Leyla."

"She is gone. Just Leyla now. Do you have anything to eat?"

"We have a plan."

"It's not working. Maybe you have something to drink?"

"Because we're missing one piece. You."

"No you don't. Don't think I can't see it. I've bullied everyone around me for years. People wanted to feel joy, and I, and I told them _no_! And I thought I was just following the rules ..."

Leyla started crying. "I only hurt people. Go away."

"You tried to solve problems," said Piqafox. "You tried to fight the only way you knew how. Rules, order, patterns. Never give up, never give in."

"I just wanted people to be safe," said Leyla.

"So do I. So does everyone." Piqafox thought about taking Leyla's hand. She didn't dare. Leyla still looked like a wild animal, dangerous, cold. "All animals are more the same than they are different."

Piqafox came closer. Leyla crawled back, until her wall hit the back of the carriage.

"I am going to open this door," said Piqafox slowly. "I'm putting all my trust in you. Go stand between Nympha and Skaia, and shoot your Cyan."

Leyla didn't answer. Her face was tense. Her eyes flashed cyan. She held the door tight with bony fingers.

Piqafox broke the lock.

The door flew open. 

Leyla hissed, pushed Piqafox aside, and ran away.

"Leyla! Think about this!" yelled Piqafox, as she lay in the mud.

"I have! I've been thinking about this all day!"

Leyla ran straight at the Endless Blackness. Piqafox struggled to keep up.

When the others saw her, they all raised eyebrows. They stopped shooting colors for a few heartbeats.

"YOU ARE FORGIVEN," yelled Purin at her from the other side.

"No, no, don't stand on the sunflowers," said Nympha as she ran past.

"Need a bike ride?" said Zara, suddenly braking in front of Leyla.

"I ... I ... you were serious?" Leyla looked over her shoulder at Piqafox. "You need my help?"

"Yes! Yes, that's what I've been saying!"

The Blackness _roared_. But not the roar of a dragon. Something worse. It had never done that before. It wasn't a sound, it was the opposite of sound. It was so big that it didn't just remove colors, it even removed sound now. What else would it remove? 

Best not think about it.

Leyla jumped on the bike. It squeaked under the weight of three people. Marco peddled for his life again.

Everyone continued shooting their colors. The Blackness sent its own black paint now. Like shooting arrows you can't see.

Justan and Fabian danced around them. Skaia had to cry some blue whales in front of her to stay alive. Nympha and Rayo worked together to paint so much green that a small forest appeared. And still it wasn't enough.

Leyla arrived between Skaia and Nympha. She raised her hands as if to cast a cyan spell. Then she lowered them again, looking around.

Everyone was doing something else. They shot colors at different times. Zara was so busy cycling around and saving animals, that she hadn't created pink in a while.

"Amateurs," mumbled Leyla.

"Everyone!" she yelled. "Get organized! We need to shoot at the _same time_!"

"What?" 

The Blackness was everywhere now. It was the thunder in the sky. It was the night at your door. It was the feeling of fear that never ends, the thousand spiders crawling over your skin.

"On my count!" said Leyla. She hoped everyone understood. She had no time to explain anymore, as the Blackness tried to blow her off her foot. 

"3!"

"What did you say?"

"2!"

"I'm not good with numbers," yelled Molmo.

"1!"

"Wand? I don't have a wand!" yelled Tomsy. "I'm _not_ a witch!"

"SHOOT!"

All the rebels closed their eyes. They tried to feel their emotion, as strong as they could. 

Molmo thought of all his happy memories. Ruby thought of all the evil things people had done to her. Purin thought about forgiving his father and mother for leaving him to die. Skaia thought of her pet, little Wonder the unicorn, and how she still missed its cuddles every day. Fabian didn't know what to think, but tried his best, as he always did. Justan remembered the dangerous woodcart races and Team FasterThanYou. Nympha remembered waking up with birdsong, the mammoth protecting her against the rock, dancing in the rain. Tomsy suddenly realized the answer to world peace, but he would forget it in a few seconds. Leyla remembered the times she actually caught criminals and saved cities with her rules.

And Piqafox? She didn't remember anything. She just imagined what the world would look like tomorrow. Beautiful. Safe. Still alive.

Their colors shot away from them. The paint tangled, mixed, formed new colors on their way.

The Blackness was covered on all sides. It roared again. It had to spit everything back out, give back the sound, and the sunlight, and the colors it had stolen. 

Piqafox dared open a single eye.

The Blackness shrunk. Fast. Molmo's palace reappeared, although its colors had all mixed. It spit out the Ice Dragon, but now it looked like a dragon made of leaves. It spit out two girls in a weird vehicle with only two wheels.

Zara and Marco raced around to save people, before they were squished by something that was spat out. Their bike squeaked and groaned more and more, as eight people sat on it now. Nympha and Rayo sent their bunnies in all directions to find more survivors.

The Blackness shrunk further. It was over the horizon before they knew it. 

Sunstripe was freed from Blackness within minutes. All the other kingdoms would soon follow.

They had done it. When they realized this, the entire sunflower patch was painted _purple_. And they fell into each other's arms. Hugging. Crying. Telling bad jokes.

The Blackness became Grayness, until it all evaporated. Like clouds. And the day was less and less cloudy. The sun broke through. The shadow hanging over their lives was gone. Molmo got his golden coins back.

As they watched the final bit of Blackness disappear, Marco parked the bike near Piqafox. It carried twenty people and animals now.

That was too much. The bike collapsed. The wheels came off and the steering bar rolled away.

Marco tapped it. "Ah. You did well, bike. You did well. Rest in metal pieces."

People had never been so happy to see colors. Even the colors they disliked the most. Every color was treated as a miracle. On that day, every possible emotion was felt, and sometimes all at once.

They were safe again. They did it. They defeated Endless Blackness.

___

All the rebels went back to their own kingdoms. There was a lot to repair. Homes, trees, entire mountains. 

And things that were harder to repair, such as Leyla's relationship with her father. He _had_ survived and hid from the Ice Dragons ever since. 

Piqafox went to see her mother when the Rainbow Soldiers released everyone they had arrested. She claimed she could see the new color, Piqarine, too now. Piqafox didn't know if that was true. But she was happy to have her family back. She started a mission to invent even _more_ colors, and collect even more paintings.

Skaia finally felt ready to get a new pet. Nympha tried to convince Purin that they _really_ needed to plant a forest in Snowstripe. Justan and Leyla worked together on a book of _safety rules_, hoping it would make the world less dangerous. Rule number one was to think for yourself. If you felt something was dangerous, it probably was, no matter what the rules said.

Molmo's family returned. They had waited it out in their "secret safe location". Together with all the kings and queens of other countries, and many rich people too. Some people say Molmo still spends his days counting his gold, and putting it in porcelain fruit statues.

Nobody knows why colors work the way they do. Nobody knows where the Endless Blackness started. It could've been Dragonstripe. It could've been the Dreamforest mazes. It could've been some kingdom nobody has discovered yet. Maybe some evil witch came up with the whole thing, and maybe Tomsy would one day discover this criminal through a smart investigation.

But we know why it could spread so fast. Fear just leads to more fear. The more you try to push away a feeling, the harder it comes back. The more you seek control, the less you'll find it. Sometimes you need anger, if a bully is mean to you. Sometimes you need sadness, to know what really matters to you. Sometimes you need friendship and love, as Zara would tell you. And sometimes you need to be stubborn or stick to a plan, if you know it's the right thing.

And now everyone knows how to keep Endless Blackness away. The ink that writes our stories should not be black, or white, or red, or one color at all. It should be all the colors of the rainbow---and maybe more.
