This is a quiz themed around **World Animal Day**. A perfect activity to play on the day itself! Its difficulty and questions were designed to suit classrooms and families. It has a mixture of easy questions and ones that will challenge anyone. There's an educational side to the quiz, but you can surely play it just for fun.

It uses our custom quizzing system, which allows it to be highly interactive and to be played both digitally and physically. The questions are short and simple. Almost all of them include images, audio or video.

It's divided into 7 rounds, 47 questions total, though you can select which ones to play in the settings.

* World Animal Day (General Knowledge)
* Animal Videos showing unique habits
* Animal Sounds
* Animal Riddles
* Awareness & Issues (commonly highlighted or focused on during Animal Day, such as endangered species)
* Cultural Animals (animals in well-known media, such as kids movies)
* Pet Pampering (questions about common pets and how they're celebrated on Animal Day)

{{% remark %}}
This quiz is not necessarily about animals or nature as a whole. It's _mostly_ focused on the day itself, what it means, how it's celebrated, etcetera. Quizzes about animals and more can be found at the related topics in the curriculum, such as biology.
