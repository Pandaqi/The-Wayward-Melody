This is one of the few puzzles that very young children can already play. Instead of drawing letters/numbers (like many logic puzzles), you are drawing simple carrots pointing one way or another. (Like arrows or lines, horizontal or diagonal.)

Your goal is to fill the entire puzzle while obeying a simple rule: carrots may never extend each other and create straight lines.

After a few introductory puzzles, more tiny rule tweaks are added over time. To keep it fresh, and to keep it challenging for older ages too.

Because it's a merely visual puzzle, no knowledge of numbers or letters is needed. Because you're drawing carrots yourself in different orientations, it trains fine motor skills and spatial awareness. And yet, the final few puzzles, with all rules added, will be a puzzling challenge to anyone!
