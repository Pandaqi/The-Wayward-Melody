{{% double-view %}}

{{% double-view-item %}}

Activity Aids are **educational activities** and/or general material to **help create your own activities**.

An "activity" differs from a game in the sense that there is usually no win condition, no clear set of rules or actions you may take. There isn't a "system" being simulated or a winner announced at the end. Most educational activities are just about _doing something fun for a little bit_. Just _playing around with something_.

It felt wrong, to us, to sell "activities" because they'd just be a PDF with "here are some things you might want to do". Most people can come up with activities themselves just fine. Probably even _better_, because they know their group and its limitations. As such, we only create such products if we can actually **add value** by adding useful things like flash cards, thematic images to use, and more.

{{% /double-view-item %}}

{{% double-view-item %}}

{{% section-foldable header="Why would I be interested?" %}}

* If you're **looking to do activities** with a group of people (though we're mostly aimed at kids), but struggle to think of anything.
* If you want to **supplement theoretical lessons and testing** with something more interactive and physical.
* More generally, you can use the special things we make for **your own purposes**. We do what we do best---create illustrations, create nice documents, create playing cards---and _you_ can build activities around it.

{{% /section-foldable %}}

{{% section-foldable header="Why would I not be interested?" %}}

As explained above, the "Activity Aid" is not "done". You can't buy it, give it to a group of kids, and that's that. 

**They are tools** to help you plan and execute general activities and events surrounding specific topics or themes.

If you want something more clear-cut, buy Lesson Material or a Busy book. If you want something with more material and rules, decided for you, try Games or Escape Rooms.
