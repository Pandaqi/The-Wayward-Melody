**A letter grid. Hidden themes that connect words into rooms. Only one room contains two people: Killer and Victim.**

The new puzzle sensation that combines letters, logic, creativity, and cozy crime mysteries. What if Murdle and Murdoku had a detective baby with Word Search? What if that baby asked you to exercise your brain in many different ways at once?

This is a **free demo** that contains a handful of Murderooms puzzles. Try them! See what they're all about!

**Easy To Learn, Hard To Master**: The rules easily fit on a single page. Find simple English words, group them into "rooms" based on a shared theme, discover the killer. But even the smallest letter grids provide a challenge in both language, logic, and creative pattern recognition.

**Batteries Included**: You only need a pen(cil) to play. Full solutions are provided at the back. They give both all the individual words to find, as well as the rooms, themes, and killer/victim/weapon.

Can you cut the letter grid into the correct Murderooms? 

Can you unmask all the killers?
