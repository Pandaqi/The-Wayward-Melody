**The Noisy Games** is our crowning achievement for _Language_ in _Level 1_! It's a very simple game that people of all ages can play and enjoy, which can practice language on several different levels.

The Code Cards display an illustration as well as how to break that word into syllables/phonics/letters. The card basically says "this creature means A, that creature means D, and so forth". The player whose turn it is now has to create a _new_ word with that code. They arrange the monsters to display it and give a hint.

Everyone else must guess what the hint means and what word you wrote. In so doing, they try to "crack the code", and guess what each creature represents. The more people guess right, the more points you score. But if you made it _too easy_, and everyone got it right, then you get nothing!

This extra step of creating a "code" (through cute recognizable monsters) makes it all possible. It means players don't have to know the alphabet yet, or know many words, and they can all play and communicate through them.

* If everyone understands letters, you can play the codes such that every Noisy Tongue represents a letter.
* If everyone merely understands phonics ("sounds in a word"), you can make every Noisy Tongue represent a phonic.
* If everyone merely understands syllables, the Noisy Tongues can represent syllables.

Once everyone is familiar enough with the game, you can even ditch the Code Cards entirely and let everyone make up their own codes!

Fun Fact: The Noisy Tongues are fun creatures (with prominent tongues ...) known from our early-language story [The Noisy Tongues](/explorers/level-1/language/the-noisy-tongues/).
