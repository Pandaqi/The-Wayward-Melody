This bundle contains a **collection of related short stories** (fairy tales the length of book chapters) focused on the topic of **basic shapes**. 

In the Shapeworld, King Triangle has suddenly declared that everything and everyone should be a Triangle! Many shapes try to comply, for they don't want to break the law and be punished. But the more they try to create triangular soccer fields, and triangular homes, or even triangular heads ... the more they realize this isn't a great plan. And what's up with those mysterious shapes cut out of the environment? Might those ancient stones be the answer? 

Follow a diverse set of Rebelshapes on a shape-related adventure as they try to battle the evil King Triangle!
