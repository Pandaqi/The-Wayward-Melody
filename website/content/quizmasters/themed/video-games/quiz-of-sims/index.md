This is a family quiz themed around **The Sims** (the entire video game franchise). It's a perfect gift for someone who loves playing The Sims, perhaps on their birthday. This is the _family_ edition, which means the questions are of easy-to-medium difficulty. People who barely know anything about The Sims can still play along!

This quiz is divided into 8 rounds, 79 questions total. So roughly 10 questions per round. Though you can select which rounds to play in the settings and are obviously not required to do it all (at once)!

* General Knowledge
* Buildings Bottled (about the building part of the game)
* Sound Effects (about Simlish, its soundtrack, etcetera)
* Inexpensive Items & Crazy Careers (about general elements of the game, mostly items and careers/skills)
* Living the Lore (rumors, theories, backstories, lore established by fans over the years)
* How does THAT work? (crazy interactions and secrets within The Sims games)
* Townies & Frownies (about he playable families, recurring characters, etcetera)
* The Expansion Pack Pile (about the many many expansions and extras)

If you want to play with die-hard Sims players, check out the [Quiz of Sims Pro](/quizmasters/themed/video-games/quiz-of-sims-pro/) instead! It contains much harder questions and obscure references.

**Fun Fact:** I created this quiz as a birthday activity/present for my little sister. She'd been playing the Sims religiously for _years_ at this point. I must confess I have never played the games myself, but I was surprised how much I already knew just from having my sister talk about it my entire life.
