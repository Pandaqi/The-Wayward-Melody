**Fun Fact:** I created this quiz as a birthday activity/present for my little sister. Let's just say that, whenever someone so much as mentioned a song or started singing its first line, my sister would always chime in by immediately stating the exact episode in which Glee featured this song. Unlike the Sims quiz that I also made for her, I have actually seen _many_ Glee episodes myself, thanks to the time I shared my workspace with my _older_ sister, who was watching entire seasons in a week while I was trying to do my homework next to her. And I'm a musician first and foremost, so I didn't mind diving into the music and coming up with questions about that.
