I am always open to feedback, good or bad. Even if you decided _not_ to buy something, it helps me a lot to tell me _why_. Truly, the biggest issue for a creative person is not a lack of ideas or effort, but a lack of _response_. Most of my websites have thousands of visitors a day, but it's exceedingly rare that any of them leaves behind a note, or review, or even a donation.
