# The Treasure Painter

Once upon a time, a colorful boat washed ashore. A hedgehog had arrived on the island Rominn to explore.

He'd heard the rumors months ago about a treasure buried here. But all he had was vague instructions, could those tell him how to steer?

_The treasure is next to a stone, but NOT next to the sea. It is buried BELOW a red totem, but ABOVE a green palm tree._

Hedgehog Envi stepped off his ship and went running without plan. After hours of searching in the sun, though, he dropped wearily in the sand.

You see, Envi had a strange disorder. I would call it curse; he would call it a wonder. In normal daylight, he was blind as an eel. But in the night he could paint pictures, and they looked perfect, almost _real_!

And so he waited for the moon to shine. He settled in the nicest spot he could find. He would move his brush fast and slow, and the resulting painting you can see below.

Was this the right place? Was the treasure hidden below those hills? Envi studied his painting ... then sighed and shook his quills.

He was next to a stone, but also next to sea! This wasn't the place, it was as wrong as it could be.

The next day he went the other direction, reaching a colorful part of Rominn. And when night fell, another painting could begin.

Had he found the treasure? Was it buried below his feet? Envi studied his painting ... then frowned and went to sleep.

He was next to a stone, and NOT next to the sea. But the only totem in sight, was as _green_ as it could be!

The next day he felt sluggish; it was hot and his water ran out. He had to find the treasure soon, and so he tried a different route.

Using his paws, he felt around himself as he toured. He touched the soft leaves of a palm tree, he touched the wood of a totem for sure. He stubbed his toe on a stone, but, surprisingly, it made him jump with a cheer! He could _not_ hear the sea, so the treasure _must_ be here.

He painted faster than ever before, so turn the page to see his painting once more.

Was he sitting on gold? Was the treasure nearby? Envi studied his painting ... then raised an angry fist at the sky.

The totem was red and the palm tree green. But the totem was _above_, when _below_ it should have been!

Envi prepared to return to his ship and leave Rominn for good. He'd curse this confusing island, if only he could.

But you see, my lovely listeners, that his disorder wasn't the end. A good night's sleep, another try, and Envi suddenly dug in the sand.

As the hedgehog had been sleeping, a wind had blown around. It had grabbed his last painting, and turned it upside down.

Now the totem _was_ below, and the palm tree _was_ above! He'd been in the right place all along, and now push came to shove(l). 

As the sun warmed his back, he made a deep deep hole. And when the moon came back, he felt a treasure whole. 

He could buy faster boats, and drinking water loads! He could explore islands where nobody had ever been. And he could paint pictures more beautiful than you have ever seen.

Now if you ever get on a boat, and visit the harbor towns, you will hear the rumor has changed, as Envi's story does the rounds. 

They speak of a hedgehog so rich, that he traveled to a distant place. But wealth is dangerous when you're magically blind ... and meet the _Watchers of Face_.
