**A proper puzzle for kids about language and creativity. Part of the famous Murderooms puzzle franchise, simplified for younger ages.**

The Murderooms puzzles have delighted many around the world. Though their rules are simple ... they are _not_ simple to solve for a kid who is just learning to read and spell.

This book bridges that gap. It starts much more simply, adding one small rule at a time, until it _ends_ with the same ruleset as proper Murderooms. This makes it playable by young kids, while still being interesting for older ages.

**Step-by-step Language Challenges**: The book has a progressive difficulty.

* The first few puzzles just ask the puzzler to find a few simple words in a grid. Look at the image of a CAT, then find CAT (in a straight line), like a word search but using images.
* Over time, however, the number of words grows. The difficulty of the words grows. They stop being in a straight line only.
* Near the end, you're not looking for _one_ word, but for _all words_ related to the image. See an Apple image? Find all _fruit_. To make it easier, all those words are connected to each other in the grid.
* What's that? All these related fruit words are a single "room". A ... _Murderoom_ ;) There will be only a single room that contains two people: the "victim" and the "bad guy".

Anyone who finishes these puzzles will have had a lot of fun, learned a lot of words, and be completely ready for more advanced word puzzles. Exactly what we want!

The story angle, with a clear goal, makes it easier to understand for kids and makes them more eager to solve it. Using _images_ (and icons) as much as possible removes most language barriers. Even so, we recommend guiding your kid through the first few puzzles if they are younger than ~7.

**40 Unique Puzzles**: Every puzzle is about a silly "crime", like taking the last cookie. It contains a few clear illustrations (the clues), a small letter grid, a place to mark "who did it", and a colorful design and story around it for those who seek it.

**Batteries Included**: You only need a pen(cil) to play. Draw lines on the book directly. Full solutions are provided at the back.

**Standalone**: All Murderooms volumes are standalone. You do not need to own any other Murderooms book, or do them in order, to enjoy this one. Furthermore, all puzzles are completely handcrafted. Computers wish they were able to generate these kinds of puzzles, but they can't! Ha!
