**Advances:** phantasy-phonics

**Contents**:

* _Add_ something to the start/end. ("What do we get when we add _th_ before _ink_?")
* _Remove/Delete_ something from start/end. ("What do we get when we remove _c_ from _cat_?")
* _Change_ a sound. ("In the word _sing_, change the _ng_ sound to the _t_ sound.")
* _Blend_ it yourself => pick any two phonemes and smash them together.

**@NOTE:** Often, the different parts are represented by _different objects_ first. (Such as actual different tiles in size/color, one for ONSET and one for RIME.) You touch the object representing a "part" of a word and make the sound. Once clear, you push the objects together and say the whole word/blended sound.
