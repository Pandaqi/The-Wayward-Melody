# The Food of Very Fruity

Comm the pear looked everywhere. He was about to have a daughter, though he hadn't been home in a while. He'd been looking for a better home, one that would make his baby smile.

If there's one thing babies need, it is food to eat. And so he went looking for a place that grew potatoes, candy, or maize.

A _home_ with walls made of candy would be fun too, but he could not be picky. He had searched a home for eight chapters already, but it turned out very tricky.

He found a field of grain, shining yellow in the sunlight. But then he saw a traffic sign saying: "Milk farm, on the right!"

He followed that sign and arrived at a farm. Cows stared back at him; milk in glass jars was getting warm. Milk and eggs were tasty, others had said. But were they _tastier_ than the grain that makes bread?

And so he left the farm, searching for longer. A home with tasty food nearby that would make this daughter stronger.

He found a vineyard that grew grapes, but that was it. He found a field of potatoes, but what if his daughter wanted tomatoes? 

Soon he found himself standing knee-deep in water. He was surrounded by white plants, tended to by a sea otter. It took him a while to realize that these paddies grew _rice_. You could make many dishes from that---would staying here be wise?

But then he saw a sign again, it said "Sugar Beets down the road!" He couldn't resist following it, even if it meant taking a watermelon boat. When he stepped out, a sweet scent made his mouth water. Sugar, yes, it's in everything, maybe this was the place for his daughter?

But then he saw a sign again. It said "Sugar is unhealthy! Visit my Apple orchard instead, down this lane!"

He followed the sign and found the orchard, with red and green apples as far as he could see. Could this be the perfect home? Could this be the place for me?

Comm had never eaten nice food, he would always say. When he was young he ate the same thing every day. Always apples---the kind that could not walk and stayed quiet. But eating the same thing all the time was not a healthy diet!

So no, he thought, it's no good to live here. His daughter should not suffer the apple diet, which was not meant for a pear. She should be able to eat different things every time. A vegetable now, a fruit later, and bread when the narrator looks for a rhyme.

He even found a candy home, but decided to let it be. A witch lived there anyway, and oven-obsessed was she.

No, Comm had to decide, I can't see what food is right. He stumbled away from all the trees and signs, when it was already dark and night.

Fortunately, in the distance, he saw a light. Tired and unsure, he walked towards it and found a campfire bright. Fruits sat around it, singing songs and telling tales. Humans and animals joined in, from sniffling snails to wailing whales.

Comm realized how alone he had been. He'd searched a home for many days, and barely anybody had he seen. Back home he lived alone too, no friend ever visiting their hut. And so he sat down at the campfire, a twisting and tingling in his gut.

Maybe what his daughter really needed was a home with nice people around. He decided to look for it the next day, and not to stop until it was found ...
