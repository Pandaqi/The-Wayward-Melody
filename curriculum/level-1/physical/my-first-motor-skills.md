# My First Motor Skills

**Description:**
The most important thing for any human, short or long
Is to move a lot and make their body strong
This resource gives ideas for training gross motor skills
But mostly specific exercises for fine motor thrills

**Extra:**
The "physical" category of this online store is understandably scarce. The "exercise" here is to ... simply ... go outside and exercise. The best thing for any body, but especially a young body, is to move a lot and challenge their muscles in many different ways. Sitting around a table, or studying some sheet on a table, is the antithesis of that. 

For every hour using this resource, we'd recommend letting your kid run around and climb walls for at least two hours. Such large movements train so-called _gross motor skills_. Once they're satisfied physically, you can start looking at these resources to train _fine motor skills_, such as cutting, folding, etcetera. 

This resource mostly focuses on fun and easy ways to accomplish _that_. The crucial activity of _Pencil Control_ (@TODO: Link?) proved so interesting and multi-faceted, though, that received its own teaching resource!
