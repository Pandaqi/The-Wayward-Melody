**Box of Belonging (Pre-Reader)** is a grouping and association puzzle. Each challenge presents a list of recognizable images, such as _cat, plane, train, and cow_. Then it asks you to sort them into a specific number of groups, like "2 groups of 2".

You can probably see the answer already. In this puzzle, the _cat_ and the _cow_ belong together (both animals!), as do the _plane_ and _train_ (both vehicles!)

The puzzles start small and simple like that, but grow to more images with harder connections to figure out over time. It's a pre-reader, image-based version of the NYT _Connections_ puzzle, if you're familiar with that.

The puzzle can be played **physically**, by cutting out the image squares of each puzzle and placing them on the solution squares. It can also be played by simply **drawing** the images into the squares, or even by just pointing at those that you think should be a group.
