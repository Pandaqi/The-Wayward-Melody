**Sleigh** is a fun video game for all ages. Play it on Christmas Eve with the whole family and have fun **moving the sleigh together**. 

Yes, that's right, it's a cooperative "One Button Game". It means that,

* You play with multiple people behind the same device.
* Every player gets only a **single button**, so it's incredibly easy to learn and play.
* But it also means that every player can only send the sleigh in **one direction** (only left, only right, ...) You _must_ work together to get the sleigh where it needs to go!

Collect the gifts and land on the rooftops of the right buildings to deliver them. And ... try to do so _before_ your sleigh is total loss because you bumped into everything along the way.

This game is great for having fun on Christmas. It's also a great educational tool, as it teaches colors/shapes, directions, dimensions, and spatial awareness. In fact, there are **two ways to play**:

* 2D: The city is "flat" (and smaller). The sleigh can only move in those two dimensions, which makes the game playable for young children.
* 3D: The city is actually three-dimensional. The sleigh can also move forward and backward in space, which makes it much harder to deliver things without issues!

The default is 2D. You can change this---and many other things---in the Settings. 

Have fun!
