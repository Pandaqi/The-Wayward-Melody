When designing these teaching resources, we must admit we almost forgot this essential skill. 

To any teenager or older, being able to grab a pen and write, scribble, mark anything you want (quickly) is second nature. We forget how hard it is to learn. And how it's one of those invisible skills that we never notice. But if you couldn't use it, a lot of doors would stay closed to you in life. (Including the many fun doors you can open in this online store, which sells many pen-and-paper puzzles!)

That's why we created this resource with a wide variety of challenges to test and practice pencil control. It shows the different grips and helps the Apprentice steadily get stronger and get used to the best grip/technique. 

We obviously recommend working through it all, from start to finish. Especially because it's a great way to prepare for the next big skill in Level 2: actual handwriting.
