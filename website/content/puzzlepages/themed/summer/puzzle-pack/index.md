This bundle contains **loads of puzzles with a summer (holiday) theme**! It has 28 pages, but most pages contain multiple puzzles, bringing the total nearer to 50. (Example: a very simple puzzle to teach the rules, then a harder one, then a harder one, then the hardest one.)

It contains a **wide variety of puzzles**. Some are about language, some about numbers, some about logic, some visual, and some about a mixture of those things. Some puzzles are adaptations/twists on a familiar formula, such as Sudoku, while others are unique summer puzzles we invented ourselves.

The puzzles are sorted from **easiest to hardest**. This makes the bundle suitable for a wide range of ages and skill. Many of the 1-star puzzles can be played by young kids who can barely read or count; many of the 3-star puzzles are just as fun and challenging for adults. In general, though, a kid who wants to fully enjoy this bundle (on their own) likely needs adequate skill in language and math.

We put a _lot_ into this bundle, making it the biggest and most diverse one yet. Hopefully it keeps you (or your kids) engaged the entire summer, and everyone can find a few puzzles they like the most :)
