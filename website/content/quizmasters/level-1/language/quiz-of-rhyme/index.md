This is a **digital quiz** (with 7 short rounds of increasing difficulty) because it needs to play **sound** for almost every question!

Rhyming is a great way to play around with language. It's intuitive to young Apprentices, and simply fun to do. It's also a great first introduction to the next step in language awareness, which is recognizing "phonemes": the individual sounds that make up words. 

This quiz approaches rhymes in all possible ways, using audio, images, nursery rhymes, and even popular Disney songs.

Unsure if this is for you? Or want more? Grab the free [Quiz of (Amazing) Alliteration](/quizmasters/level-1/language/quiz-of-alliteration/) that we made alongside this one.

The Teachers resource for this topic practices more or less the same things. In that resource, however, the Guardian and Apprentices have to make the sounds themselves of course, and it includes some extra activities/little games that a quiz can't do.
