This is a fun **Christmas quiz** for **young kids**! Ideal for use in a classroom or for a family activity at home if your kids are the right age.

The questions are simple and as textless as possible. Almost all questions have an **image, audio fragment or video fragment**. It aims to introduce some basic Christmas vocabulary and concepts, as well as other media (songs/movies). The quiz is probably too easy for any child above the age of ~7, in which case we recommend [Quiz of Christmas Family](/quizmasters/themed/holidays/christmas/quiz-of-christmas-family/).

It contains 6 rounds, 32 questions, as follows,

* General Knowledge
* Party Pictures
* Christmas Songs
* Merry Movies
* Yuletide Yes/No
* Odd Reindeer Out
