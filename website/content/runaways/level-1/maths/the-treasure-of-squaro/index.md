This is a simplified "escape room" to challenge your **shape knowledge** (and general visual awareness and logical thinking). 

It contains 10 simple puzzles that let you see shapes in a slightly different way each time. The solution of each puzzle is a "jigsaw piece". A part of a larger image/puzzle that's shaped like a puzzle piece. Once you've solved all puzzles, and combine the pieces gained, a recognizable image should come out and prove you did it right!

We know that "escape room for 4-year-olds" sounds insane. But we _knew_ this could be done, and we think we've found ways to do it. The puzzles in this escape room are still _puzzles_, but they're absolutely simple enough (and completely textless, of course) that young kids can understand and solve them. Moreover, the solutions are completely visual too, creating a bigger image as a reward. And if it's too hard? Too long? You can play only _some_ of the puzzles, by just giving the "pieces" of the unplayed puzzles right from the start!

We hope this is will be a great "first escape room" for many Apprentices.
