This quiz is about **piracy** in the broadest sense of the word. It contains a bit of history (with the Golden Age of Piracy), legendary pirates, pirate speak and culture, _why_ they came into existence, and many references to pop culture. Every round is different, almost every question has image/video, and people of all ages can enjoy the quiz.

You can play it (for example) in a classroom when talking about this era of history or at a pirate-themed party. It does expect a basic reading ability and some general knowledge, which is why we set the minimum age to ~9 years old.

Like all our quizzes, it uses our **simple system** that allows playing the quiz however you want: digitally, physically, hybrid, with custom answer sheets, and more. You can easily **skip rounds** if you don't like them or think the quiz is too long.

In fact, we've added a **file with 40 bonus questions (4 rounds; including images/video too!)** with our hardest and most educational questions. We left them out of the quiz, but you can play them if you think your group is ready for them.

Have fun!
