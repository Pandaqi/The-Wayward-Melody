In **Spinnershapes**, you're racing across a board to reach the finish line first. How (far) you're allowed to move each turn, however, is decided by the _spinner_. They act like dice: you give it a spin, and the option that ends on top is the one you must choose.

Is it all luck, then? Of course not! You decide _which_ spinner you want to use. And each one is a different basic shape with a different number of sides, which changes your probability of getting a certain outcome. Through extremely simple rules, this game tests color knowledge, shape knowledge, _and_ adds intuition for probability and strategy.

Oh, and the tactile element of spinners creates a tactile game that will keep children engaged.

Because _color_ is a major part of the game, printing in color is pretty much required. Double-sided printing is not supported, because it's recommended to glue the spinners onto cardboard/thick paper anyway, and use other household items to assemble the smoothest possible spinner!
