**Group Soup** is a revolutionary "forbidden word" game ... but for pre-readers. A "forbidden category" game, if you will. It teaches all sorts of common groupings that appear in the real world, as well as being able to reason about them and creatively mix them.

Each turn,

* The start player draws 2 random prompt cards. (They have clear images showing well-known concepts.)
* Then they invent some **group** that fits them both! (Example: "tiger" and "pineapple" might make someone say **Orange!**)

This continues until all players have given a clue, except one. That player randomly picks **one** of the original cards to see, and must now **guess** what the other card was! 

Correct guesses get your group closer to winning, wrong guesses are hilarious and instructive.

This simple base game can be played over and over, with any group. Each combination of random cards leads to new groupings and associations. Even so, once you're comfortable with it, you can move on to the expansions:

* More cards with harder concepts
* Simple twists for extra challenge, such as every player that gives a clue _also_ drawing extra cards.
* Deluxe: A set of cards with _written_ concepts, which are harder and require some reading skill.

In a way, this game is the "opposite" of how grouping games usually work. Many of our (grouping) games are about giving you a _group_ and then asking you to pick something specific inside of it. But this game does the reverse: it gives you specific things and asks you to find common groups for them. This kind of reverse thinking is an incredibly powerful learning tool that ensures Apprentices actually _understand_ what they're doing.

Have fun and keep learning!
