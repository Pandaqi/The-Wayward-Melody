# The Sand of Comparing Castles

Comm the pear looked everywhere. He was a talking fruit in need of a home, at least a better home than he had before. A soft crib for his daughter, to safely sleep and snore.

And so he looked at homes again, but not from the outside. He told himself he would find the perfect home before tonight.

A beautiful home is nice, sure, and so is one that's tall. But that doesn't matter if it's empty inside, that matters most of all! His own hut back home only had a lamp, some chairs and a table. But the table wobbled if you used it and wasn't even stable.

His daughter deserved something better, a place she'd love to sleep every night. A soft bed, instead of sleeping on the floor, and a table that properly stood upright.

After walking for hours, he had thirst and a headache from the heat. He yelled "ouch!" when something sharp pricked in his feet.

It was prickly green cactus, the only plant in sight. Everything else was sand, sand, sand, from the left to the right.

This must be the desert, thought he. No talking food lives here, except the silly pea maybe. It's not great to be outside, but that doesn't need to be a problem. Because he discovered a large sand castle waiting for an owner!

He stepped inside and found ... nothing. 

Of course, if nobody lives here, why would there be furniture inside? There would be no need for a bed, a table, or a night light.

Comm picked up the sand and was reminded of when he was a child. He would build sand castles---sand _anything_, really---and he smiled.

He did the same thing now, building chairs of sand, and sandy tables. But then something magical happened, something he only read about in fables.

The things he built became real! When he finished a bed of sand, it was suddenly a soft pillow he could feel.

"Yes!" he yelled, as he made a rocking horse from sandy beads. This was the perfect home, he thought, because he could just _make_ everything he needs!

He made windows, tables, forks and spoons. Plates and pans, doors and rooms.

At the end of the day he was tired and wanted to sit. He made a soft sofa of sand, which became a real version of it.

The moment he sat down, he fell fast asleep. Without wanting to ... he would stay here for a _week_!

Yesterday he was happy with his room, but now he wanted more. He had a sofa, that was nice, but living here was a bit of a bore. So he made a television of sand, and sandy paintings on the wall. And when he was tired, back to sleep he would fall.

Yesterday he was happy with his paintings, but now he wanted more. Stories are more fun than images, he thought, so he made a bookshelf full of heroes and lore.

Yesterday, he was happy with his books, but now he wanted more. Walking the stairs was annoying, so he built a sand elevator to bring him to the next floor.

At the end of the week, the home was packed with things to be had. Comm couldn't even walk anymore without stubbing his toe on this or that. He had more chairs than fingers, and more tables than chairs, and suddenly he realized: this wasn't good for pears.

Maybe it is _not_ always better to have more stuff. Maybe you just need a few things, and that's enough. 

He picked up the soft bed he'd made and some baby clothes. Everything else he left behind when he drew the door to a close.

As he left the desert, though, he did so with a frown. He had been lucky, so far, not to meet any danger, and to only find homes that weren't built upside-down ...
